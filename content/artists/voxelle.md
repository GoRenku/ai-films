VØXELLE is the credited music-project identity on the Suno release linked from the original video thread. The song page identifies **V6**. That establishes a song/project credit, not a named human vocalist or the visual-generation model.

## Why follow this project

[The Seventh Reset](/films/seventh-reset/) connects a constructed performer, a camera and a mirror to questions about images acquiring authority. Its sampled changing scenes give the song a directed visual subject beyond a static cover or repeating visualizer. The relationship between making an image and submitting to it is the reason for inclusion.

Individual direction, visual tools and team size remain undisclosed in the checked release. Those gaps are kept separate from the verified project identity and accessible video.
