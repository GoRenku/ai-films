## Why follow this filmmaker

Mark Wachholz publishes as **Particle Panic**; the channel links his own LinkedIn, Facebook and X profiles. Its catalogue runs from AI short films and concept trailers to video essays, with a recurring interest in cinema itself. [The Cinema That Never Was](/films/the-cinema-that-never-was/) imagines the films history never produced and won AI Film Festival Japan's 2025 **Best AI Documentary Award**.

He is unusually specific about method: the release of that film names each generation, music and voice tool and discloses creator-programme support. Those are the maker's statements about one film, not a general account of his practice; team sizes and budgets remain unknown.
