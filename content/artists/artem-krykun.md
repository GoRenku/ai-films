## Why follow this filmmaker

Curious Refuge names Artem Krykun as the maker of [John-O'-Lantern](/films/john-o-lantern/), which won its 2026 Audience Favorite award by public vote. The film inverts Halloween so that pumpkin families harvest humans, playing cheerful domestic dialogue against a gruesome premise.

The festival page is the verified credit source, not a personal portfolio. The organiser's gallery attributes the film to 41 Frames, and that label's link to Krykun is not stated. No personal channel, X profile or wider filmography was established.
