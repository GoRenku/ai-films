## Why follow these filmmakers

Runway credits **Sifang Chen & Yang Wang** with [Between Before and After](/films/between-before-and-after/), an eight-minute drama about two former lovers. It received a Merit at Runway's 2026 AI Festival. Sustaining a quiet two-person relationship across that length is a different challenge from spectacle-led AI shorts.

The festival page is the verified credit source. No personal portfolio, social handles or production details were confirmed.
