Lenssen’s portfolio includes **The Award**, **The Box**, **Capsules** and **Murder on the Heaven**, alongside explicitly labelled trailers and developing projects. His publication archive documents successive films across 2024–2026.

## Why follow this filmmaker

AI is a recurring dramatic subject in these stories: questions of control, moral status and dependence become mysteries or threats faced by characters. That continuing thematic interest makes the work worth following beyond any single tool demonstration.

**Autonomous** gives those concerns a thriller structure. In a future dominated by two data-centre nations, two guards investigate a series of killings while becoming targets themselves.
