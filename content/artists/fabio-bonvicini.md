## Why follow this filmmaker

WAIFF names Fabio Bonvicini of Italy as the director of [Steam](/films/steam/), in which a 1900s factory worker’s hallucination turns industrial labour into ballet. The film won Best AI Soundtrack at WAIFF 2026 in Cannes.

The festival credit is the verified public source. A personal portfolio, the soundtrack’s composer and social accounts have not been established, and none is guessed.
