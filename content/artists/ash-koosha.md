Ash Koosha is the writer and director of **Dreams of Violets**, programmed at Tribeca in 2026, and **Odysseus: The Fall**. He co-founded Fountain 0 with producer Pooya Koosha.

## Why follow this artist

Two features make this a useful case for following sustained production across different genres. That output is a reason to pay attention, rather than a verdict on the quality of either film.
