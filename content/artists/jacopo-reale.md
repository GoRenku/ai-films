## Why follow this filmmaker

Labyrinth Studio's biography describes Jacopo Reale as an Italian film editor with more than a decade of work for companies including Netflix, Disney, Warner Bros and Rai Cinema, and as a co-founder of the studio. His two catalogue entries show an editor's interest in what a cut can imply. [Love at First Sight](/films/love-at-first-sight/) tells a silent romance through looks. [The Fracture](/films/the-fracture/) moves a 1515 court drama between eras.

Love at First Sight won the **2025 Reply AI Film Festival**; The Fracture won **Best Direction** in Astana's 2026 Open Competition. Both had commercial backing: Kling commissioned the first for its 2.0 launch, and The Fracture was produced with the Animatix platform. Reply's October 2026 announcement identifies Reale as AI Creative Director at Labyrinth Reply, a Reply Group company, and he sat on Reply's 2026 jury. Those ties are noted because Reply also runs the festival he won in 2025.
