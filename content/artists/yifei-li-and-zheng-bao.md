## Why follow these filmmakers

ITU credits **Yifei Li and Zheng Bao**, working in China, as directors of [PLASTIC EROSION](/films/plastic-erosion/), the winner of the AI for Good Film Festival 2026. Their film puts an environmental argument into one image, plastic fish passing through the food chain to the plate.

The festival page is the verified credit source. A 2025 Korean award for a work of the same title credited to Li Yifei is noted on the film page; no personal portfolio or social handle was confirmed.
