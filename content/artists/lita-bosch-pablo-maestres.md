Lita Bosch and Pablo Maestres are the credited directors of [Luccia](/films/luccia/), a video for **Naghel**. Their collaboration is worth following for how a strongly tactile visual world is organised into a song-length story.

The Artlist interview identifies Bosch’s colour-grading background and Maestres’ contribution to visual structure. This profile credits a collaboration, not a permanent studio or a verified total crew. The commissioned/platform context is disclosed on the film page; personal X accounts remain unverified.
