## Why follow this artist

[Confession](/films/confession/) is a starting point for following Jang Gun-ho’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. Jang places a humanoid in the recognisably human ritual of confession. The programme describes grief and faith as the means of testing how the character understands its own emotions.
