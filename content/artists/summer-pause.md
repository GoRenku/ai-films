## Why follow this filmmaker

Summer Pause (여름방학) is the production credit on [Devoured](/films/devoured/), directed, written and edited by Eun Young Lee and Heui Song Son. The omnibus turns everyday Korean city spaces into body horror and won the AI micro-series category at WAIFF Seoul and at the 2026 Cannes finale.

Son has described the project as an attempt to bring body horror to AI and to replace effects work that would usually need a large crew; those are his own claims. Further work by the pair is not covered here, and no social accounts are guessed.
