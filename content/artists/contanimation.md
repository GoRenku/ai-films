**Contanimation Films** is the Madrid studio run by directors **Guillermo Miranda** and **Javier de la Chica**. Its biography describes de la Chica’s experience directing more than 100 animated-series episodes and Miranda’s background in television editing, 3D, VR and medical animation.

## Why follow these directors

Their anthology **Contanimation TV** starts with a specific storytelling choice: show the world as Don Quixote experiences it. That makes visual invention serve a character’s point of view, with comedy emerging when his imagined heroism collides with Sancho’s understanding of events.

The named collaborators on **The Sorrowful Figure** also make the studio useful to follow as a team-based production practice. Storyboards, voices, composition, editing and sound have identifiable contributors. AI animation is one credited part of that work, rather than a substitute for explaining who made the film.

## October 15 — CHUMBO announced

The studio has named **CHUMBO**, Issue 2 of Contanimation TV, as a forthcoming Magnific Original. The announcement extends the anthology’s escape-from-reality premise. Its short teaser is not a verified complete episode, so it does not become a released-film card.

## October 23 — CHUMBO released

[CHUMBO](/films/chumbo/) is now available as a complete standalone anthology short, superseding the earlier teaser-only status. Its official breakdown records named collaborators, Seedance 2.5, 272 final shots and a manual finishing process behind its room-to-room sequence. The practical lesson is that consistent staging and an apparently continuous camera move still depend on reference preparation, take selection and compositing.
