## Why follow this work

[Peking Opera Paradise](/films/peking-opera-paradise/) is a starting point for following Jiayang Liang’s filmmaking. The short appears in both OMNI’s awards and Sparknify’s short-film programme. OMNI’s score award makes the work a lead for following the relationship between Liang’s sound and image choices. Neither listing identifies the score’s production tools.
