## Why follow this work

[The Tree](/films/the-tree-ivan-yeh/) is a starting point for following Ivan Yeh’s filmmaking. The festival describes the tree as a fixed point from which Yeh imagines a century of change. That device gives a visual through-line to a future that the filmmaker can approach at several scales.
