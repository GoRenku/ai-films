## Why follow this artist

Anton Dubrovin works alone, and much of his work returns to the Second World War on the Eastern Front. [The First Day](/films/the-first-day-anton-dubrovin/), a five-minute film for his great-grandfather, an officer at Grodno in June 1941, combines generated scenes with restored archival photographs, and won AI FilmFest Serbia 2026. His channel also carries further war shorts, his own AI music videos and a Russian-language AI series.
