## Why follow this artist

The [Nuremberg](/films/nuremberg/) trailer builds on TUPACABRA’s earlier short interpretation of the 1561 sighting. In the trailer, figures on medieval rooftops and people working around modern machinery face variations on the same unexplained event. The project is a chance to follow how a creator develops a single speculative image into a story that spans several eras.
