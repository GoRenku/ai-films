## Why follow this artist

The [Nuremberg](/films/nuremberg/) trailer builds on TUPACABRA’s earlier short interpretation of the 1561 sighting. In the trailer, figures on medieval rooftops and people working around modern machinery face variations on the same unexplained event. The project is a chance to follow how a creator develops a single speculative image into a story that spans several eras.

## TEMPLES and the wider world

TUPACABRA also posts shorter world-building pieces between trailer updates. [TEMPLES](https://x.com/tupacabra/status/2101605649755124211), a 2:51 video first posted on September 20 and shared again on November 24, shows a modern city under hovering craft, monumental temples and a papal ceremony, and carries no caption beyond its title. Its production design is carefully finished, and it is collected on the [Nuremberg](/films/nuremberg/) page as part of the same project. We follow his account closely for further pieces as the feature develops.
