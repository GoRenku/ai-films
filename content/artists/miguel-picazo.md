## Why follow this filmmaker

Tamara Llorens explicitly credits **Miguel Picazo** as co-creator of [The Siesta Job](/films/the-siesta-job/). The short gives an elderly couple an escape, a vehicle and an appetite for trouble, making their shared rebellion the engine of the story.

This record preserves that collaboration. The available account does not allocate individual writing, animation or editing tasks between them. OPUS’s Best AI Animation result names Llorens; Picazo is linked as the film’s credited co-creator.
