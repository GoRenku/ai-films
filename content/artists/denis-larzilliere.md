## Why follow this filmmaker

Denis Larzillière trained at the Arts Décoratifs in Paris and has more than fifteen years of directing and editing behind him, according to his site. Working as Ninaï, he has made one AI film a month for Prompt Club since December 2024, alongside AI music videos and commercials, and teaches generative AI at Video Design Formation.

[Another Detail](/films/another-detail/), a retro-futuristic twist on a man who believes he has everything, won Best AI First Film at WAIFF 2026 in Cannes. His site lists Dreamina and Kling creative partner programmes; those describe his practice in general, not the tools of a particular film.
