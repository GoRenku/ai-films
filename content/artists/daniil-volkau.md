## Why follow this filmmaker

His catalogue is organised around recurring series, including **HOLED**, **Space Pirates** and **Wrong Planet**, alongside shorter standalone stories. That sustained practice is the reason for inclusion: the interesting question is how a creator maintains a dramatic world, its rules and its characters over successive episodes.

HOLED turns missing memory into a shared social problem rather than a visual gimmick. Wrong Planet's public premise places a space crew among societies consumed by procedure. Both offer specific authorial concerns: grief, evidence and institutions that struggle to recognise what is happening.

The [71-minute feature announcement](https://x.com/machina9000/status/2082083889762226638) and HOLED's accessible episodes are different forms of evidence.
