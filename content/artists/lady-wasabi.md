## Why follow this creator

**LadyWasabi / LadyWasabee** publishes as **LouieVerse Cinematics**, with songs under **LouieVerse**. The public profile and original uploads connect these names. A separate human vocalist or legal identity is not inferred from the music-project credit.

[Space Between Us](/films/space-between-us/) and [I Choose You](/films/i-choose-you/) use music to move a recurring romance from hesitation toward commitment. Glances, physical distance and ballroom movement make the emotional relationship readable across the song. Dialogue and action belong to that staged situation rather than replacing the music-video form.

These unofficial Malfoid fan-fiction works have their own authorship; they are not merged with schmede’s adaptations. The creator’s second-part description discloses Seedance through Dreamina, OpenArt and Topview, with creator-program context. Those credits remain attached to that part, without guessing versions or applying the stack to every earlier video.

**LouieVerseCinematics** is also a named contributor to [Lyrian Chronicles RIFT](/films/lyrian-chronicles-rift/). That collective production has its own [team profile](/artists/lyrian-chronicles-team/), preserving the other credited makers rather than assigning the series to one collaborator.
