## Why follow this artist

[The Living Corpse](/films/the-living-corpse/) connects an established horror source to a generated feature and a real theatrical release. Its unusually detailed official tool and voice credits make it a useful production reference.

The important question is adaptation: how does the film translate a distinctive drawn world into movement, duration and voices without reducing that identity to a generic horror look?
