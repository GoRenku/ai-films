## Why follow this filmmaker

Holden Boyles directed [Humans: Playtime](/films/humans-playtime/), which won first place in Chroma Awards Season 1's Experimental & Open Format category. It catalogues human play with deliberately absurd imagery and a self-confessed '90% accuracy'.

The film was released by ambiguous, a team of filmmakers, designers and coders working with AI and virtual production. Its channel names Cody Updegrave, Holden Boyles and Eli Pinkerton as leads. The channel also hosts other shorts, but Boyles's personal credits on them were not checked, and none is attributed to him here.
