## Why follow this studio

**Purga Studio** is an AI creative studio based in Buenos Aires, and most of its public channel is brand work. [Listen to Meh](/films/listen-to-meh/) is, by its own account, its first fiction short: a dialogue-free comedy about a gaucho and a sheep on his roof that won first place at the 2026 Higgsfield Global Film Festival. Señal News, reporting the studio's announcement, names Martín Montes de Oca as the project's director.

The move from commercials to an original, wordless narrative is what makes the studio worth following. Its advertising work and awards are context, not catalogue entries; Higgsfield, which ran the festival, has also published a case study on the studio's advertising work. The festival credits the film to the platform account rococo_table_radical rather than to a named person.
