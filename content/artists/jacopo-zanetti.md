**Jacopo Zanetti** publishes as **Jaio / Jaiostudios**. His official portfolio, original directing credit and linked channel establish those aliases without relying on a similar account name.

[What’s the Job?](/films/whats-the-job/) and [The Twin Earth](/films/the-twin-earth/) share an interest in people living under rules they did not choose. One uses a surreal comic fable of assigned roles; the other makes a duplicate planet a test of a society's capacity to accept an alternative. The connection is thematic, rather than a claim that the films share every model or collaborator.

AAIFF's original results confirm What’s the Job as its Best Visual winner. The older Twin Earth supplies a clearly divided generation and finishing inventory, while the newer release names finishing and music sources without exact generation models. Preserving that difference makes the body of work useful to follow without inventing a fixed house pipeline.
