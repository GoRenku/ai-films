## Why follow this filmmaker

OMNI credits **Maria Zozulia (Ukraine)** with [The Reckless Play](/films/the-reckless-play/), winner of its Envato Pattern Recognition Award at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia. The five-minute animated short builds a chase comedy from a chain of escalating decisions across a large cast of animal characters, and its release lists the tools used.

Her channel describes her as an AI filmmaker and screenwriter and claims a Berlinale 2026 top-five AI selection and a Megogo AI Film Festival 2025 win; PixVerse's presentation of a later follow-up describes the Megogo prize as Best Screenplay. Those are the creator's and platform's statements, not independently checked results. No personal website or X profile linked from her channel was found, so none is listed.
