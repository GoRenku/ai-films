## Why follow this artist

[One More Pumpkin](/films/one-more-pumpkin/) is a starting point for following Kwon Hansl’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. Kwon pairs Korean imagery with Halloween motifs to build a three-minute horror story. His festival notes identify text-to-image, image-to-video and AI upscaling as parts of the process.
