S.A.V identifies himself as a 3D artist, filmmaker, director and Phantom X co-founder. His original channel identifies him as the creator of **Exile Protocol** and publishes a continuing episode catalogue. The October 13 eighth episode is a complete release, not a concept trailer.

## Why follow this director

The interesting bridge is from 3D staging to generated anime. In a September 24 making-of post, S.A.V describes using **Blender and a phone** to build shots for Exile Protocol with **Invideo Agent 2**. Showing the intended shot can resolve a spatial problem more concretely than describing it in prose.

The series also asks that a cast, mechanical designs and action geography persist across substantial episodes. The channel distinguishes earlier Agent One-labelled releases from newer Agent Two episodes; the current platform label should not be retroactively assigned to the whole season. The checked sources do not establish every model version, the complete crew or a total budget.
