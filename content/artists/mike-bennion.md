## Why follow this filmmaker

Mike Bennion's site identifies him as a writer/director, and Reply lists him as a UK director. He wrote and directed [Kev](/films/kev/), a four-minute portrait of a blind man that uses the conventions of documentary: talking to camera, a voice carrying the story and a small set of everyday spaces. That form puts the filmmaking problem in performance and restraint rather than spectacle.

Kev won the **Micro-Short** award at the 2026 Austin AI Film Festival and was one of Reply's ten 2026 finalists. Reply names Midjourney, Nano Banana, Kling, Veo 3 and ElevenLabs as its tools. His wider filmography and collaborators are not reviewed here, and no social handle has been added.
