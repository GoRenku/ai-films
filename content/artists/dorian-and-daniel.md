## Why follow this duo

[The Well](/films/the-well/) puts a familiar animal-fable vocabulary inside a murder investigation. That combination offers a concrete filmmaking problem: making stylized characters carry suspicion, social hierarchy and withheld information.

The work belongs here as a complete narrative with festival recognition. Model names and a full crew count have not yet been confirmed.
