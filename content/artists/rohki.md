## Why follow these artists

[A Million Trillion Pathways](/films/a-million-trillion-pathways/) makes music, character and imagined history share a short running time. The craft question is whether a compressed world remains emotionally readable.

The work is selected as a sustained narrative experiment, with Hachi and IO credited by the festival. Its production toolchain is not inferred from the hosting festival’s software brand.
