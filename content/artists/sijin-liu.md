## Why follow this filmmaker

OMNI credits **Sijin Liu (Taiwan, UK)** with [Ici (Remix)](/films/ici-remix/), its Golden Sound: Music Video winner at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia. The video builds Bai yun's song from a handful of recurring symbols, and Stash's account of it describes a storyboard-led workflow with manual correction rather than prompt-only generation.

Her portfolio describes her as a London-based director and visual artist, founder of SijinLab, previously a senior creative at Art&Graft and a director at Bito. It also lists a 37th Golden Melody Awards Best Music Video nomination for Sakura Gansha and a 2026 Forbes Australia list of women AI filmmakers; those are her own listed credits. No X profile linked from her site was found, so none is listed.
