## Why follow this artist

[Papernaught](/films/papernaught/) ties a handmade-looking universe to a parent’s search for a child. The appeal is the connection between material design and emotional stakes.

A miniature or paper-like world can make fragility part of the drama. The useful question is whether movement and editing preserve that quality across the journey.
