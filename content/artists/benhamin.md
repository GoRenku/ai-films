## Why follow this filmmaker

**Benhamin** releases AI-made films on the Benhamin_AI channel, which describes its owner simply as someone who creates AI-powered films. [The Tortoise and the Hare](/films/the-tortoise-and-the-hare/) reworks the fable as a tomb expedition in which the hares misjudge their enemy, and won the Audience Choice award at the 2026 Higgsfield Global Film Festival.

The body of work is small so far: the channel's only other upload is a later short, Green Flag, labelled PIXLIGHT 2026, which has not been reviewed here. A full name, location and collaborators are not disclosed, and no social profile is added.
