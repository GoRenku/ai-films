## Why follow this artist

Desumbila’s portfolio spans sports campaigns, music videos and mixed-media work, including Labrinth’s Kill for Your Love and Riot Games’ HEARTSTEEL / PARANOIA. His own profile identifies him as a filmmaker and Sauvage co-founder. This gives [Looking For Perseida](/films/looking-for-perseida/) a useful context: an experienced image-maker applying an established directing practice to a new narrative workflow.

In his post-festival reflection, he describes building the short shot by shot and caring about whether the characters connected with an audience. That attention to emotional response is the reason to follow his AI work, beyond its visual novelty.

In Qazinform’s direct interview, he reports ten days of production, working alone at home and starting with a rainy ending before developing the story backwards. These are film-specific maker claims; his commercial crews and credits are not assigned to this short. No verified X account is established by the original profiles checked here.
