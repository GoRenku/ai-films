## Why follow this work

[Amal (Hope)](/films/amal-hope/) is a starting point for following Ridouan Ajhir’s filmmaking. OMNI places Ajhir’s work in its animation programme and awarded it in the under-ten-minute category. The programme verifies the format and credit.
