## Why follow this work

[The Pornographer](/films/the-pornographer-cummings-saint-pierre/) is a starting point for following Milla Cummings & Marie-Josée Saint-Pierre’s filmmaking. The screening programme frames the work as an inquiry into how generative imagery represents women’s sexuality. Cummings and Saint-Pierre approach that question through a progression of sensory images.
