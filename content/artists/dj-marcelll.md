## Why follow this filmmaker

Marcel Rutschmann works as **Dj Marcelll**, building comic situations around an undead suburban family in [The Walkers](/films/the-walkers/). The contrast between familiar domestic behaviour and horror characters offers a repeatable dramatic framework rather than a sequence of unrelated monsters.

OPUS credits **Dj Marcelll** with its Best AI Short Film winner. The creator has also developed other Walkers material; the exact festival cut and its runtime remain to be reconciled before an episode or full-release link is assigned.
