## Why follow this artist

[Where Do Grandmas Go When They Get Lost?](/films/where-do-grandmas-go/) and [Costa Verde](/films/costa-verde/) share an interest in childhood perception. The appeal is a coherent point of view: images are organized around how someone remembers or misunderstands the world.

Following both films helps separate a director’s recurring concerns from any one generation model. His published interview is a useful companion to the festival selections.
