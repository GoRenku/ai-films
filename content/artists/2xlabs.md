## Why follow this filmmaker

Reply describes 2xLabs as a Chinese creative studio and collective of visual artists; Digitaling lists it in Shenzhen. Its 2026 festival work covers two very different registers. [Centenarian Kindergarten](/films/centenarian-kindergarten/) is a near-future satire about four centenarians escaping enforced care. [The Last Span](/films/the-last-span/) is a roughly 17-minute folk fantasy about a girl, a river and a bridge.

The studio's own Bilibili credits name **Tian Junchao** (田俊超) as supervisor and director of Centenarian Kindergarten, and the Astana festival credits **Junchao Tian** for The Last Span. Centenarian Kindergarten placed **third** at Reply and won its **AI for Good Award**. The Last Span won **Best Story** in Astana's Open Competition. Credits differ by film, so named collaborators are kept on the film pages.
