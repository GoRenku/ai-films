## Why follow this filmmaker

[**Afahria**](/films/afahria-the-wicked-worm/) gives fantasy a domestic centre: a struggling family pub. Woodward connects that premise to growing up above his parents' bar and restaurant. The artistic signal is a personal setting translated into character comedy, rather than a succession of spectacular creatures.

His production account treats staging as a directing problem. A sitcom returning to two locations requires recognisable space and stable positions across generations. Preparing character, prop and environment variations beforehand offers a concrete way to support that continuity.
