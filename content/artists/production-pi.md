## Why follow this studio

Production Pi describes itself as making every kind of video, from advertising and entertainment to drama and AI work. [FIX](/films/fix/) is, in the studio's words, its first animated short: a school-band comedy set in a world made entirely of paper, which won Lumara's Mouse Award and a Higgsfield Global Film Festival honourable mention.

Lumara credits **Eunha Lee, Sora Lee and Hakhoon Lee** as the film's directors; Higgsfield credits the studio account, and the film's own credits name "Pi". The individual roles of the three directors and the rest of the studio's team are not disclosed. Its channel otherwise carries commercial and short-drama work, which is not treated here as part of its film catalogue.
