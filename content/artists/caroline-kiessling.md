## Why follow this filmmaker

Caroline Kiessling’s [Department of Good Memories](/films/department-of-good-memories/) makes an institution’s memory-storage rules into a character problem. Her production account explains how a restricted setting and repeated props support continuity.

OPUS awarded the film The Luminary Award, presented by She Builds with AI. Kiessling publishes under Idlespace and describes her production decisions openly, making the film useful to follow as an authored short as well as a production case study.
