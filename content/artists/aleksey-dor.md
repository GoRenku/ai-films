## Why follow this work

[REVIVER](/films/reviver-aleksey-dor/) is a starting point for following Aleksey Dor’s filmmaking. Lumara recognised Dor’s work in its experimental category. The official programme gives viewers a way to see the film in the same screening context as the other category winners.
