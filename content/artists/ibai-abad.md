## Why follow this filmmaker

Reply describes Ibai Abad as a Spanish stop-motion artist and director. He wrote and directed [The Child of the Sea](/films/the-child-of-the-sea/), a MotionField production that carries a stop-motion look across a journey through 17th-century Spain. His release notes frame the tools as a way to reach a scale he could not produce independently, while keeping writing, design, direction and editing as deliberate stages.

The film received a **special mention** at the 2026 Reply AI Film Festival. Other directors named Ibai Abad appear in film databases; this entry does not link them to him without a direct source. A broader filmography and social accounts have not been verified.
