## Why follow this filmmaker

The project connects archaeological imagery with speculative fiction. Its scale creates demanding directing problems: establishing recognizable characters, preserving spatial logic and controlling pacing across a feature.

This profile follows the identifiable directing credit. It does not infer a longer AI filmography or transfer the studio’s technical achievements to one person. A personal X account was not verified.
