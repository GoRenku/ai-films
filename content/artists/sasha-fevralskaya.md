## Why follow this filmmaker

Sasha Fevralskaya's [Kagome](/films/kagome/) won first place in Curious Refuge's 2026 AI Horror Film Competition. It sets a missing child against a night-shift worker's seemingly perfect home, and the organiser's jury singled out its cinematography and suspense. In under three minutes it relies on withholding information rather than shock.

The release names the generation tools used but no collaborators. The YouTube channel carrying the film is used as the public credit source; no wider filmography or X profile was verified.
