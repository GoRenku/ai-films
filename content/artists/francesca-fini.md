## Why follow this work

[SIMUL/ACTION #1](/films/simul-action-1/) is a starting point for following Francesca Fini’s filmmaking. Fini calls the work augmented performance art. Her production account says she directs herself and an actor and fencing master as real performers while using Seedance 2 through early Dreamina access to extend the action.
