## Why follow this filmmaker

ICO develops a character-led fantasy in [PRAY TO GACHA](/films/pray-to-gacha/). The capsule-toy collector ALO gives its surreal world an emotional starting point: attachment to objects and a desire for connection. Unplug describes the project as a joint development with ICO.

The completed 22:48 film received the OPUS Grand Prix on November 14. That award establishes the festival result; the earlier project announcement does not by itself establish playback of the completed cut.
