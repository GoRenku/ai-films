## Why follow this filmmaker

Loïc Ramboanasolo's [Remember Me](/films/remember-me/) won first place in Chroma Awards Season 1's Micro-Dramas category. It is a two-minute vertical story about someone erased from their loved ones' memories, made to work within a format usually used for serialised melodrama.

The film is released on the Tell me Songs YouTube channel, which the organiser links and which also hosts a trailer for his animation LEELOO. That is used here as his public release channel. No wider filmography, collaborators or X profile were verified.
