**Sho AI Studio** releases a sustained body of original AI-assisted animation: [ATLAS](/films/atlas/), its [NEXT sequel](/films/atlas-next/), [RAIDEN](/films/raiden/) and the romance [The Sea Breeze Between Us](/films/the-sea-breeze-between-us/). The original descriptions attribute story, characters, design, dialogue, scene/music direction and final editing to the releasing creator. A fuller personal name and complete supporting team are not disclosed.

The reason to follow the studio is its return to long scenes and recurring casts across complete episodes, along with a move from mecha combat into written correspondence and first love. The original [We Fight On!](/films/we-fight-on/) music video also turns those recurring worlds into song-led montage.

Tool disclosures remain attached to their actual productions: Seedance, Leonardo AI, OpenArt and a Suno ending song for ATLAS/RAIDEN; Seedance 2.0 / 2.5 and reference preparation for NEXT. They do not establish every romance or song’s tools. The creator’s authorship statements are attributed, not an independent rights or crew audit.
