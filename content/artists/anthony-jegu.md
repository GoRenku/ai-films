The original **Cracked Chimes** credits name **Anthony Jegu** as director, animator and editor. His portfolio spans sculpture, bas-relief, ink drawing, photography and murals, and links his digital studio **Moss+Stone**.

## Why follow this artist

This is an artist with a visual practice beyond generated video. That background makes the passage from designed still form to animated space worth following: a film needs its image language to hold across movement and editing as well as in a striking single frame.

The film belongs to a collaboration. **Dr Scott** is credited with production and story, **Brandon Hartley** with writing, and separate contributors with music and sound. The linked X account is the official film project; it is not presented as Jegu's personal account.
