## Why follow this filmmaker

Austin credits **Gavin Carlton** with [The Sultana Disaster of 1865](/films/the-sultana-disaster-of-1865/), its 2026 Documentary winner, and Carlton's own post links that film's upload on the **Generative History** channel. The channel describes itself as telling forgotten history through cinematic investigations; a second upload concerns German spies landed in America in 1944.

The work is worth following for the problem it takes on: using generated recreation for events that left no footage, while citing books and archives in the release itself. Carlton's personal site, X account, collaborators and exact tools have not been established; the channel itself does not name him.
