## Why follow these filmmakers

**Lucas Levitan** and **Fabián Jiménez** co-directed [Little Mes](/films/little-mes/), a Merit at Runway's 2026 AI Festival. Levitan wrote and art-directed it; Jiménez shared the AI execution and editing. The film is credited as a SALAD Collective production. Levitan's site describes Salad as a five-person collective of artists, filmmakers, sound designers and screenwriters working with AI, with a series and another short in development.

Levitan describes himself as a Brazilian illustrator and creative director based in Madrid. His illustration background shows in the film's character design. The website is Levitan's portfolio; no separate site for Jiménez or the collective was confirmed.
