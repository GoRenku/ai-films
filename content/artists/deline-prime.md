DELINE PRIME, a label of 株式会社SPEED EFFECT, produced [星の唄が聞こえる](/films/hoshi-no-uta-ga-kikoeru/), a 117-minute anime feature released free on YouTube on October 22, 2026. Its director, 結野弘崇, an entrepreneur in his first directing job, wrote, scored and designed the film.

## Why follow this studio

Most generated anime is a few minutes long. This is a full feature with a life-spanning story, human lead voice actors and, by the makers' account, a three-person team working for about three months. Whether the studio can repeat it is the thing to watch.
