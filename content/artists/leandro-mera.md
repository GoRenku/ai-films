Leandro Mera is credited with [Seven Domes (7 Cúpulas)](/films/seven-domes/), released on the channel of the Argentine studio Cráneo Films. The credits of Cráneo Films' earlier [El día que me quieras](/films/el-dia-que-me-quieras/), directed by [Matías Mera](/artists/matias-mera/), list a Leandro Mera among the voice cast, as Ceferino Namuncurá. ## Why follow this filmmaker

Seven Domes takes a conquest legend, the lost city of gold, and tells it as a fever: a Spanish expedition, an Indigenous elder bargaining for his granddaughter, and a desert where greed and mirage merge. It was made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest.
