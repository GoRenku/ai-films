## Why follow these filmmakers

**Dejawa (Yunsun Lee)**, **Dana (Jungmin Hong)** and **Panda Gomm (Kyungsoo Lee)** made [Souls of the Shipwreck](/films/souls-of-the-shipwreck/), winner of ITU's AI for Good Film Festival 2025. The upload credits them as TEAM 4eyes. In ITU's interview they describe working remotely across Korea and Vancouver and finishing the film in 48 hours. They say they chose metaphor over realism when consistency proved hard to control.

The website is Dejawa's YouTube channel, which published the film. No other shared portfolio was confirmed, and no individual social handles are added.
