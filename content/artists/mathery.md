## Why follow this studio

[Where Knights Fall](/films/where-knights-fall/) uses a familiar story as a structure to overturn. The opportunity is comic authorship: expectations created through a tower, a rescuer and repeated failure can be redirected through movement and timing.

Wonder TV provides a complete film, giving the work a stronger entry point than a reel of disconnected images.
