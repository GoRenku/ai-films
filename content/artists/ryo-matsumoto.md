## Why follow this filmmaker

Ryo Matsumoto (松元 良) is a director at Dentsu Creative Pictures whose profile lists television commercials and music videos for Japanese brands and bands. Among them is [Andy's Days #42](/films/andys-days-42/), the stranded-astronaut comedy that won AI Film Festival Japan's 2025 **Best AI Comedy Award**; the festival's nominations page also credits Satomi Okubo as co-director.

The interest is in seeing a commercial comedy director apply timing and short-form storytelling to an AI-made narrative. The checked sources do not name his tools, team or budget for the film, and no personal social handle is established.
