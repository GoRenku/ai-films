LedertsWorld Studios develops Elderwall across short-form character work and [The Last Nursery of Elderwall](/films/the-last-nursery-of-elderwall/), its feature-length Halloween story. The studio portfolio also lists a complete short, *Origins*, alongside individual scenes and character pieces. The creator’s campaign identifies *Origins* as a completed, separately funded proof of concept and describes six proposed episodes as a separate production.

The portfolio describes a workflow spanning writing, visuals, editing and sound. Those studio-level descriptions do not establish which tool made a particular Halloween scene. The available sources do not give a complete feature crew or budget.
