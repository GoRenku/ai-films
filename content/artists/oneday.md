## Why follow this studio

OneDay's series *The Decisive Moment* places viewers inside recent turning points in the news. [The Decisive Moment: Midnight Drop](/films/the-decisive-moment-midnight-drop/), its third film, won first place in Chroma Awards Season 1's Drama & Romance category. It cuts between a B-2 crew and a woman in Tehran on the night of the June 2025 strikes on Iran.

Samir Mallal and Bouha Kazmi wrote, directed and produced it. The studio describes combining live performance capture and cinematography with AI-rendered environments. Its credits include a cast, sound designer, editors and a Company 3 colourist, so this is a studio production, not a solo generative workflow. Only Midnight Drop has been verified for the collection; the earlier films in the series have not been checked.
