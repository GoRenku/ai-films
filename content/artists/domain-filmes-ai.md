Domain Filmes A.I publishes original speculative fiction through its own channel. [Earth Fall 2763](/films/earth-fall-2763/) makes the case for following it: a pact, a hostile landscape and bio-cybernetic giants return across four chapters, giving the series room to show how survivors adapt between encounters.

The channel also collects its installments into full cuts. [After Impact](/films/after-impact-domain-filmes/) returns to survival on Earth, while [Black Horizon](/films/black-horizon-domain-filmes/) and [Black Horizon Ancestral](/films/black-horizon-ancestral/) revisit an alien world. The work is credited here to the releasing studio. Individual crew credits, model versions and budgets are undisclosed in the sources checked.
