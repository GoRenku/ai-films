## Why follow this filmmaker

Nikita Guk's [Trick or Trend](/films/trick-or-trend/) placed third in Curious Refuge's 2026 AI Horror Film Competition. It turns the pressure of beauty trends into a Halloween bargain: a face borrowed for one night. The organiser credits Guk and embeds the film from the @nickguk Instagram account, which is used here as the public release source.
