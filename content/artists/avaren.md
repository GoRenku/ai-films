## Why follow this filmmaker

Avaren is credited as writer and director of [Out of Character](/films/out-of-character/), a seven-minute comedy about an actress who exists only inside the worlds she was made to perform in. The idea is shared with Alex Marco; the film is produced by The Blue Horizon, the studio behind María, María.

The premise is a good test of direction in a generated medium: the comedy depends on a character noticing that the scene around her has changed genre, so continuity of performance matters more than spectacle. Public biography beyond this credit has not been found; this profile does not infer one from the studio's.
