## Why follow this filmmaker

[The House of Amun](/films/house-of-amun/) develops Tutankhamun’s reign across ten released chapters, moving between the royal household, priesthood and army. Rodriguez’s original portfolio identifies him as an AI video director and lists commercial work separately from this narrative project.

The feature trailer credits writing, direction and production to Rodriguez. That identifies creative responsibility without assuming he performed every production task. Individual voice actors, exact models and the total crew remain undisclosed in the checked series releases. The announced November 25 feature version is a milestone to verify, not a completed release claim.
