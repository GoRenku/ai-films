## Why follow this artist

TechTalkNavigator develops an original robot anime at TechTalkNavi Studio. [Confronting the World](/films/eisenritter-confronting-the-world/) shows how the creator reworked the first four episodes for a theatrical screening.

## November 24 — FRIENDLY FIRE

[FRIENDLY FIRE](/films/friendly-fire-techtalknavi/) is a self-contained seven-minute mecha short made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest 2026. The creator lists what was generated (images, video, voices, music) and what was done by hand (titles, subtitles, the edit), and has published the project's design sheets.
