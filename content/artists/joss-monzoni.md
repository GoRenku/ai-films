Joss Monzoni, also credited as Joss Monzon, is a Canada-based creative director and AI producer. Wonder TV describes a career spanning more than 30 years in advertising, brand storytelling and visual production.

## Why follow this artist

[**The Fall**](/films/the-fall/) gives his work a clear narrative entry point: an animated fable with an inherited duty, a catastrophic loss and a quest to repair it. That dramatic structure makes it a useful addition to a collection concerned with authored films.

The craft question is how one creative voice connects writing, art direction, narration and music. Monzoni credits himself for writing, direction and art, and publicly identifies his tools. Those disclosures make the work worth following beyond its release; they do not establish that every production task was performed alone.

## A comic counterpart

[Grimble & Wobbles](/films/grimble-and-wobbles/) introduces two mice with contrasting temperaments in a compact animated comedy. Monzoni [identifies Director with Seedance in CapCut](https://x.com/JossMonzoni/status/2100743750716653919) as the workflow for its animation and introduction; he does not specify a model version.
