## Why follow this filmmaker

**Yuichi Ono** directed [UTILITY](/films/utility/), second at ITU's AI for Good Film Festival 2026. ITU describes it as a solo film made with repeated Seedance 2.0 generations to capture subtle emotion. CyberAgent says Ono joined the company in 2017, worked on advertising production and direction, and has worked in its generative-AI video unit since 2025. It also lists a jury special prize at World AI Film Festival 2026 in Kyoto.

The website is his employer's award announcement, the only verified profile. No personal portfolio or social handle was confirmed.
