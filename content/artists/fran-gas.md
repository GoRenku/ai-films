## Why follow this artist

[Latex Kid](/films/latex-kid/) is a starting point for following Fran Gas’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. Gas uses the musician’s artificial skin as the premise of a short about surviving an image-driven world. His programme notes credit AI for the film’s imagery.
