## Why follow this artist

[BRAINSTARE](/films/brainstare/) offers a feature-length test of direction and editing with generated performances. The published credits retain distinct writing and editorial roles.

That makes the useful question one of collaboration: how does a screenplay become a sequence of readable performances when the photographed actor is replaced by generated material? The journal does not adopt the production’s ‘world first’ claims.
