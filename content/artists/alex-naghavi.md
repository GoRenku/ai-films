## Why follow this filmmaker

Alex Naghavi's official site describes him as a Los Angeles creative director with a background in branding and digital product. His film credits show that design training applied to two different problems. [Le Drip](/films/le-drip/), co-directed with **Ezra Li**, sustains a hand-painted oil texture across a complete animated short. On [Railbound](/films/railbound/), written and directed by Jonathan Perry, he is credited as visual director and AI artist, working from real photographs rather than invented worlds.

Le Drip took **second place** at the 2026 Reply AI Film Festival and is on Austin's 2026 honourable-mention list. Railbound won its Chroma Awards Season 1 documentary category. Those results are recorded as facts, not as proof of quality. The credits list human roles for story, sound, colour and editing; Naghavi is one credited contributor on each film, not its sole author.
