## Why follow this artist

[SITARA](/films/sitara/) gives its protagonist a small but consequential ambition: to be respected and recognized by name. That choice puts character and social experience at the centre of an AI film, rather than treating spectacular imagery as the entire subject.

The creator uses **Abhick** on his personal profile and the original writer/director poster; AAIFF identifies him as **Abhik Banerjee** and links Rollsound. The original release is shared with his personal account, establishing the connection without relying on a name match alone.

His award post thanks @raayadingoria as a producer alongside Rollsound studio. Exact crew totals, model versions and budgets are not disclosed in the checked sources. No verified X profile was found in these original accounts, so an account is not invented.
