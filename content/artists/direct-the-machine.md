## Why follow this artist

In [You’re Too Young to Remember](/films/youre-too-young-to-remember/), Andrew puts the same couple into two versions of a first encounter. The shot-by-shot record lets viewers compare clean footage with the graphics laid over it and see where the era changes are carried by the cuts, the props or the interfaces. It also records the director’s approvals and revisions, showing where human choices shaped material produced with Claude.
