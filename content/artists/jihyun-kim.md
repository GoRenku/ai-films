## Why follow this filmmaker

AI Film Festival Japan credits **Jihyun Kim** with [FRIENDS](/films/friends/), runner-up for its 2025 **Outstanding AI Animation Award**. A May 2026 Hankyung report identifies Kim as an AI director at Westworld's Vision Creative Lab who directed FRIENDS and the science-fiction short WASTELAND, both released on TVING.

FRIENDS stretches an AI-animated family story to about 15 minutes around a dog, a boy and a robot caregiver, and the studio says its plot and direction were designed in advance. A personal portfolio, social handle, tools and budgets have not been established, so the festival listing serves as the public credit source.
