The original [Lyrian Chronicles RIFT pilot](/films/lyrian-chronicles-rift/) credits the combined efforts of **TheHolyBall, NightwayFantasy, Keeen_EN, LouieVerseCinematics, occultusnotitia and generalgravey**, with special thanks to **BrewerTheCoder**. This profile groups the explicitly credited production collaboration; it does not invent a registered studio or single lead director.

## Why follow this collaboration

The half-hour upload carries a recurring party through an established fantasy world, rather than presenting unrelated anime tests. Survival rules, limited abilities and cooperation provide a basis for serial storytelling. Its shared authorship is part of the interest: individual roles remain undisclosed instead of being assigned from social-media prominence.

The original credits **Leaflit Asari** as creator of Lyr in **Angel’s Sword RPG**. That underlying-world authorship is separate from the team making this pilot. Seedance 2 and AI-generated music are tagged in the release; exact versions, music and voice models, full team scope and budget remain unknown.

Nightway Fantasy’s October 20 announcement says another episode is a few days away. The existing pilot is the verified complete release, and the next episode remains forthcoming.
