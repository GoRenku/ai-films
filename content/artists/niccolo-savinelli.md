Niccolò Savinelli founded Realista and created [INFERNO — The Series](/films/inferno-the-series/). His portfolio combines direction, editing, music production and sound design. It also documents the 51-minute **Rottincuore** discofilm with Romina Falconi; that background is not evidence that Rottincuore was AI-generated.

## Why follow this creator

Savinelli’s practice connects image construction with composition and sound. INFERNO makes that division unusually explicit: generated images sit alongside music and sound designed in the studio, rather than an undifferentiated claim that everything was generated.

His portfolio separately identifies InVideo collaborations. Those commissions do not establish INFERNO’s exact generation models, budget or complete team. No personal X profile is verified by the checked sources.
