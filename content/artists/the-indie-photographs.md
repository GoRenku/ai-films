## Why follow this filmmaker

[AMRIT](/films/amrit/) won first place in Chroma Awards Season 1's Horror & Thriller category. It is a Hindi-language horror short built on a reversal of fear and laughter. The Chroma submission presents it as an Indie Cut production in association with XSNRG, directed, photographed and edited by Bharat, with Bharat Parmar, Nimish Choubey and Aashish Bansal credited as AI artists.

The film's release lists an extensive set of tools and says it was made in seven days; both are the makers' own statements. The X profile is the one linked from that release description. No broader filmography was verified.
