## Why follow this artist

[Ask Me Anything](/films/ask-me-anything/) is a starting point for following Lee Yong-seuk’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. Lee builds a feature around a recurring interview setup and a succession of worlds. The format connects the changes in setting to the participant’s memories and fears. BIFAN calls the film a hybrid psychological thriller.
