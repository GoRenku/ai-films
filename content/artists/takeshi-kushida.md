## Why follow this artist

[Last Dream](/films/last-dream/) is a starting point for following Takeshi Kushida’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. The short gives planetary history a single witness, compressing the history of life into an astronaut’s encounter with a rock. The festival programme credits AI across image, dialogue and music.
