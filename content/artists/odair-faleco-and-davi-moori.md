**Odair Faléco** writes and directs **Sinuca de Bico (Cornered)**; **Davi Moori** co-directs and handles its editing and sound design. Faléco’s portfolio describes two decades in the creative industry and includes other AI work such as *FungAI* and *Clothes of Deception*. The studio identifies Moori as the founder of Nave Madre, the film’s co-producer.

## Why follow these collaborators

Their collaboration brings writing, visual generation, editing and sound into an intimate story about an overwhelmed worker. That focus makes them relevant to AI cinema beyond spectacle: the challenge is to give ordinary pressure a subjective visual and sonic form.

Faléco’s official site links the X account above; it is his account, not a shared profile or a verified personal account for Moori. Faléco publicly lists several platform partnerships, but those affiliations do not establish which models were used on *Cornered*.
