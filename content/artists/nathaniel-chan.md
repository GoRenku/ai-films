Nathaniel Chan publishes as **xdeconstructed**. His own portfolio identifies him as the director of a growing collection of AI films and commercials, including **Midnight Snack**, **Two Fwnds**, **Clear-Vu**, **Midnight Prince** and **Based on a True Story… “Yogurt”**. These are individually presented works, rather than a claim that every item is an episode of one series.

## Why follow this filmmaker

[Midnight Snack](/films/midnight-snack-nathaniel-chan/) locates its dramatic question in a withheld response: what does consideration look like when someone has asked not to be questioned? Chan’s stated interest in psychology and small physical gestures has a concrete counterpart in that premise. The conversation and the decision to leave something unsaid carry the scene, rather than a succession of unrelated generated spectacles.

His portfolio describes an influence from 1990s Hong Kong and New York cinema, with grain, long lenses and nocturnal colour. Those are the maker’s account of his visual interests. The checked Midnight Snack playback and original release artwork show tightly framed faces and contrasting red and blue-green light; this is a limited viewing observation, not a critical assessment of every film in the portfolio.

## What is publicly credited

For a separate film shown in its filmmaker showcase, **Kyoso** credits XDeconstructed with direction, writing, generation and editing; **Kyoso and Jordan Daniel Chesney** as executive producers; **David LaChance, Jr.** as composer and sound designer; and **JDC Entertainment** as producer. The page says that film was shown at a ByteDance conference in Budapest. These work-specific credits are not transferred to Midnight Snack or the rest of Chan’s catalogue.

Chan’s portfolio describes a **100-minute Midnight Snack series in development with Dreamina Originals**. That is a development statement, distinct from the released 1:06 short. The checked pages do not establish a completed 100-minute release, its budget, a full cast or exact model versions.

## November 28 — The Fixer

[The Fixer](/films/the-fixer-nathaniel-chan/), released November 27, runs close to nine minutes, eight times the length of Midnight Snack. It is part of **PLAYGROUND Season 1**, an anthology produced by **Storybook Studios** and made on Cuelist, and it extends the interest in avoidance and withheld feeling from Midnight Snack to a whole character. Midnight Snack now plays from Chan's own YouTube channel.
