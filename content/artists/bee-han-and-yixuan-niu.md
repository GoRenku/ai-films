## Why follow these filmmakers

AI Film Festival Japan credits **Bee Han and Yixuan Niu** (China) with [Walled Dreams](/films/walled-dreams/), winner of its 2025 **Best AI Animation Award**. The film pairs a lonely girl with a plush pig in a walled city, a small fable that depends on making a toy companion feel alive.

The Full Moon Studio channel describes itself as an AI filmmaking team based at Beijing Film Academy and claims the same AIFJ award; it also hosts a second short, A Little Full. That self-description links the channel to the credited pair, but neither upload names the directors directly, so the alias is recorded with that limit. Individual roles, models and budgets are not established, and no social handle is guessed.
