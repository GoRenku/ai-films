Javi Lopez created [**Backrooms Nemoris: It Eats Light**](/films/backrooms-nemoris/), the pilot of a planned ten-episode anthology produced by Magnific. The official release credits him as creator; his accompanying account identifies his writing and DaVinci Resolve editing.

## Why follow this artist

Lopez’s account gives sound unusual prominence in a field often discussed through images alone. He describes shaping reverberation, placement and layers of sound to make spaces feel coherent and unsettling. That makes the pilot a useful entry point for examining how an authored world is established across both picture and sound.

His plan to recruit voice performers, a colorist, VFX artists and a sound designer for future episodes is also worth tracking. These are intended collaborators, not verified credits for the released pilot. The question for the series is how its visual and acoustic identity develops as production expands.
