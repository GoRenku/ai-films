## Why follow this artist

[Snowfall](/films/snowfall/) is a starting point for following Bae Jun-won’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. The director describes a story that starts where words cease, using the snowbound setting to carry the absence at its centre. Bae says AI was used across the production apart from the screenplay.
