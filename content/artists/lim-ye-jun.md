## Why follow this artist

[Big Ball](/films/big-ball/) is a starting point for following Lim Ye-jun’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. The programme describes a film within a film. The audience learns that an AI is creating the trader’s world while the character has to work out why it feels wrong. Lim developed the work through BIFAN’s Fantastic Film School Creator Course.
