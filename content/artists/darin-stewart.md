Darin Stewart directs [The Arc](/films/the-arc/), an Artlist-backed serial thriller. Artlist credits Happy Monday Labs with production, Kids Don’t Know with post-production, and Simone Ferretti as executive producer alongside Artlist.

## Why follow this filmmaker

Its recurring father-and-son stakes give the changing historical settings a dramatic reason to exist. The production is useful to follow for the decisions that survive a change of tools: reference design, continuity, sound finishing and organising a multi-episode story.
