## Why follow this team

Their first documented feature offers a concrete small-team production case, with a completed story and unusually explicit cost and schedule claims. The interesting question is how two creators maintain an ensemble, multiple locations and changing emotional stakes across a feature—not simply how quickly they can generate footage.
