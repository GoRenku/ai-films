Martin Weisz is credited by Utopai as director of all eight planned episodes of [Space Nation](/films/space-nation/) and co-creator with Marco Weber.

## Why follow this filmmaker

The project offers a specific test of serial authorship: maintaining an ensemble’s relationships and an action geography across an alien-war story. A completed-pilot announcement is a meaningful production step, while the full series remains forthcoming. Neither sales activity nor a credit list establishes the finished work’s quality.
