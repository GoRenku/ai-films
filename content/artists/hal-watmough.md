## Why follow this work


Watmough brings an editor’s perspective to generated narrative. Curious Refuge awarded [LATE](/films/late-hal-watmough/) first place in its animation competition. In [The Trials](/films/the-trials-hal-watmough/) he works on a larger scale, tying an alien challenge to the bond between a grieving girl and a cheetah. Wonder Studios’ [making-of](https://tv.wonderstudios.com/watch/the-trials-bts) traces that collaboration. The [Future Vision XPRIZE results](/festivals/future-vision-xprize/) also credit him for *Slingshot*, a pitch trailer.
