## Why follow this filmmaker

WAIFF names Jules Blachier of France as the director of [La Sélection Mécanique](/films/la-selection-mecanique/), in which a factory robot is displaced by a newer model. The film won Best AI Animation Film at WAIFF 2026 in Cannes.

The festival credit is the verified public source. A personal portfolio, broader filmography and social accounts have not been established, and none is guessed.
