Abel Art describes a background in traditional production and post-production and a move into AI narrative work in 2023. The portfolio traces a continuing practice through **Sh*t!**, **The Prometheus Expedition**, **The Dinner**, **Le Voyageur**, **Half** and **An Artist**.

## Why follow this filmmaker

There is a useful tension in this work: generated images are used to tell stories about human memory, displacement and belonging. **An Artist** makes that tension explicit by following a creative worker excluded from an automated economy. Its premise gives the technology a subject to interrogate, rather than making technical novelty the entire attraction.

The earlier **The Dinner** is also a concrete process reference: the portfolio describes a ten-minute experiment combining Unreal Engine with Runway Gen-1 video restylization. That disclosure applies to that film, not automatically to later releases. Follow the verified X account for new films and project-specific production notes.
