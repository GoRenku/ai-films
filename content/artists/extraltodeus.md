Extraltodeus is the public creator/channel identity behind **Chromatique** and **C’est de l’IA**. The same X account links the original YouTube releases. No legal name or separate human vocalist is established by those sources.

## Why follow this creator

[C’est de l’IA](/films/cest-de-lia/) gives a synthetic singer an identity problem: every attempt to appear human invites more suspicion. [Chromatique](/films/chromatique/) instead centres an expansive, changing world of reflective architecture and colour. Both establish song-length visual concepts rather than presenting an isolated model test.

The creator also discloses local image/video tools and distinguishes them from the language models coordinating production. Claims of overnight or unsupervised production remain attributed accounts, not proof that artistic intent or human release decisions disappeared.

## October 13 releases

[Sur mesure](/films/sur-mesure/) and [Le ciel sous la peau](/films/le-ciel-sous-la-peau/) extend Astra’s recurring identity into tailoring and bodily/architectural transformation. Their original complete-release descriptions separately credit local Krea2 reference images, local MinimaxH3 video, ComfyUI and Suno. Lyric credits differ: GPT-6.1 for Sur mesure, Codex for Le ciel sous la peau. These are work-specific maker claims.

## October 14 — Volta

[Villain Era](/films/villain-era/) introduces another credited performer identity, Volta, in a full 3:29 release. Those disclosures are recorded on the film page; the title does not establish a separate human vocalist.

## October 17 — Coming Home on Fire

[Volta — Coming Home on Fire](/films/coming-home-on-fire/) is the complete 3:24 release behind the [X caption “Innovation is conquest”](https://x.com/Extraltodeus/status/2111383907715154312). The original channel resolves the title and performer identity. Its sparse description does not repeat Villain Era’s model list, so that earlier stack is not assumed for the new work.

## October 19 — another song and an accessible cut

[La musique ne s’arrête pas](/films/la-musique-ne-sarrete-pas/) adds an earlier complete ClaudiaGI release, with its own Suno V6 mini credit and locally run visual-tool account. Its two opposed visions of the same world differ from C’est de l’IA’s authenticity satire.

The [English-subtitled C’est de l’IA release](https://x.com/Extraltodeus/status/2111872584338018562) makes that existing song’s argument easier to follow. This is a translated alternate cut, not another directing identity or a new dub. The newly teased French clip is still a preview, not a completed release.
