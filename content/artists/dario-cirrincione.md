## Why follow this filmmaker

Dario Cirrincione made [Present](/films/present/) as his 2025 diploma film at the Conservatorio Internazionale di Scienze Audiovisive (CISA) in Locarno. The film follows a woman on her fiftieth birthday as her mind comes apart, a subjective subject that asks images to carry perception rather than action. It won Best 8th Art Film at WAIFF 2026 in Cannes.

He is an early-career filmmaker; a personal portfolio, social account and wider filmography have not been established, and none is guessed here.
