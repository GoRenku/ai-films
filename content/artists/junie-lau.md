Junie Lau grew up in Guangdong. Wonder Studios' behind-the-scenes film says she won Peter Gabriel's AI music-video competition before directing [Goldfish Mayhem](/films/goldfish-mayhem/), the final film of Beyond the Loop Season 2.

## Why follow this artist

Goldfish Mayhem is a 27-minute Cantonese action comedy that draws on Foshan, kung fu and Hong Kong cinema, with three women as its heroes. Lau talks about authorship as the point of the tools, and worked with a human screenwriter. Her work brings a specific regional culture into AI fiction rather than a generic one.
