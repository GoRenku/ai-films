Zahid Iqbal founded ZetoMate and is credited with the story, screenplay and direction of **The Mother’s Monster**, released through MYTHRA.

## Why follow this filmmaker

The film has a clear emotional anchor: a mother and a village interpret the same creature differently. That premise gives generated spectacle a human conflict to serve. The next artistic test is sustaining character and consequence across further stories.

ZetoMate also sells brand production services. Its reach and production-speed claims are promotional disclosures, rather than an independent judgment of the film’s quality. An established personal X account was not verified; the studio and release channel are the current sources to follow.
