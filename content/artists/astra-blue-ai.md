Astra Blue AI is the public releasing project behind [P DOOM](/films/p-doom/). The account identifies itself as a slopcore artist. Its public project identity is used here without guessing an operator’s legal name or a human singer.

## Why follow this project

P DOOM turns a disagreement about risk into a pop-video premise: a synthetic figure receives control despite an acknowledged possibility of catastrophe. The interest lies in how an attractive performer and system-like overlays frame that power, rather than in the accuracy of the fictional risk estimate.

The release claims twelve hours of unattended production. It does not identify a specific model stack or named human director, and the phrase “Super Intelligence” is not treated as a technical credit.
