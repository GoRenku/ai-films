## Why follow this artist

Yang uses deliberately simple shapes and drawn faces in a short about growing up between Chinese and Norwegian cultures. He narrates the story in his own voice.


## November 19 — OPUS results

[When You Run From Your Mom](/films/when-you-run-from-your-mom/) received **Best AI Documentary** at OPUS’s November 14 ceremony, according to the organiser’s published results.
