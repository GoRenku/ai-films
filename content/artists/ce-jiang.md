## Why follow this filmmaker

Ce Jiang, also identified as **Lost Joker** in the official programme, develops the original anime project [Oni-Slaying Maiden](/films/oni-slaying-maiden/). Its drawn-character approach extends the festival’s range beyond photorealistic simulation.

OPUS records a completed 5:32 work and the Best AI Anime Film award. That supports a record for the screened film, without treating the wider original project as a verified released series. Detailed production roles and exact generation tools remain undisclosed in the checked programme.
