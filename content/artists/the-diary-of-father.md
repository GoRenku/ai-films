## Why follow this filmmaker

[Forever](/films/forever-special-episode/) places Atatürk’s final days in a personal frame. The project account supplies historical references and credits a creator partnership with Higgsfield. Its published name is the credited releasing identity.

## Kuvâ-yi Milliye

[Kuvâ-yi Milliye](/films/kuvayi-milliye/) is the project's first series. Its first episode, Toroslarda Kuşatma, released November 22, is set during a siege in the Taurus Mountains in the spring of 1920 and again links a bibliography and tags Higgsfield as a creator partner.
