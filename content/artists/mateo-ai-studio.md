## Why follow this work


The team behind [Raphael](/films/raphael-mateo-ai-studio/) met through Korea’s New Media New Technology Lab and co-produced the film with MBC C&I. The Korean Film Council reports that a seven-person team used Kling AI and Photoshop to keep appearances and tone consistent across generated footage. That continuity work is worth following at feature length. The studio portfolio also lists the shorts *Mateo* and *Witness* (목격자).
