## Why follow this artist

MINIMA’s experimental music practice informs how the images move. In [kusarabi](/films/kusarabi/), repetition and interruption carry meaning across both the invented language and the grotesque animation. The SAC portfolio documents a broader practice spanning music videos and experimental moving-image work.
