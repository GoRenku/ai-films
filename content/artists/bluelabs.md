## Why follow this studio

BLUELABS made [The Ice (빙氷)](/films/the-ice/), an animated drama about a father crossing a frozen world toward his old home. It won first place in Chroma Awards Season 1's Animation & Anime category and third place in the narrative category of the 2025 Korea AI Content Awards.

The release credits a team with separate roles: Choi Jeonghwan directing, Jeon Woojin producing, Lee Hanjae writing, Han Eunbin on VFX, Lee Seungmin on AI design and Kim Sungjun on sound, with the story from 21Studio. Exact models and a broader body of work were not verified.
