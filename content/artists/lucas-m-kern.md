Kern is credited as creator and editorial supervisor of **The First Anunnaki**, the second episode of Lucas Cinematic Studio’s Anunnaki Movie Series, produced in partnership with **Anunnaki Ancient Mystery**.

## Why follow this filmmaker

The series asks more of world design than a standalone spectacle: characters, political conflicts, ships and places must remain meaningful across episodes. That continuing dramatic context is the reason to follow Kern’s work. His release notes describe substantial attention to environments and spacecraft design.

The films are presented by their creator as fiction inspired by mythology, not historical or scientific evidence. Specific AI models, budget, full team credits and a verified X profile remain open research questions.
