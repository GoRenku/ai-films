## Why follow this artist

[Total Pixel Space](/films/total-pixel-space/) approaches generated imagery through an organizing idea. That is a useful alternative to treating every striking image as a separate attraction.

The craft question is how a conceptual argument becomes cinema: through duration, narration, musical structure and the ordering of images. Adler’s background as a composer makes that relationship especially relevant.
