## Why follow this filmmaker

Matías Mera directed [El día que me quieras. El viaje de Gardel](/films/el-dia-que-me-quieras/), an AI-animated account of Carlos Gardel's life produced by the University of Buenos Aires with Cráneo Films, which received AI Film Festival Japan's 2025 **Special Jury Award**. In an Infobae interview, as summarised by the broadcaster, he explains that the team chose AI animation over a costly conventional documentary while keeping human voice performances.

The project is credited to a named team, from screenwriter and AI realiser to archive researchers, rather than to a solo operator. Mera is reported to work with Cráneo Films; the production company's channel is used here as his public base. Specific generation tools and budgets are not disclosed.
