Gavin Purcell releases these documentary experiments through **AI For Humans**. His October 12 film credits his Claude Code agent **Fig** with writing and direction, and Purcell with production. The closing interview is a real recording of Purcell; the presenter, **Dr Imogen Ashby**, is generated.

## Why follow this producer

The valuable evidence is the disclosed division of labour. In [An AI Made This Entire Documentary to Find Out If It Can Feel](/films/can-ai-feel/), an agent reportedly writes, generates, checks and assembles a sustained documentary, while Purcell gives substantial creative and technical notes. The producer’s pinned comment specifically mentions fixes for shots and edits. That record makes the human intervention visible within a claim of mostly autonomous execution.

The experiment is relevant to filmmaking workflow and presenter continuity. Neither a fictional host nor generated first-person dialogue establishes that the system experiences feelings. Credits and tool roles are maker-reported, not an independently audited production log.
