## Why follow this filmmaker

Qiu Sheng comes to AI filmmaking from conventional features. According to the University of Hong Kong’s programme, he trained in biomedical engineering, his debut *Suburban Birds* (2018) was selected for Locarno, and his second feature *My Father’s Son* won Best Artistic Achievement at the Shanghai International Film Festival.

[A Dollar Story](/films/a-dollar-story/) applies that background to a single, carefully chosen device: one dollar bill carried across a century of screen history. It won Best A.I. Action Film at WAIFF 2026 in Cannes. A personal website or verified social account has not been established, so the organiser’s page stands in as the public credit.
