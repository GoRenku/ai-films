## Why follow this creator

[Cutting Room Floor](/films/cutting-room-floor/) makes selection and revision its subject. A recurring performer moves through deliberately constructed scenes while the song challenges the idea that generated production consists of one effortless instruction. The maker’s published outtakes make that position more concrete than the song’s argument alone.

In a direct reply, antic describes this as a fourth music video and estimates roughly 50–60 hours over five days, while acknowledging that no timesheet was kept. That is a developing practice with increasingly deliberate iteration, not a verified productivity benchmark. Local LTX 2.5, Grok Imagine and models accessed through Fal are named for this release; those disclosures should not be assigned automatically to the earlier videos.
