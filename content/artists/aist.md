## Why follow this studio

**AIST** — the channel explains the name as "Another Interesting STory" — describes itself as a team of filmmakers making AI films. [Fukushima](/films/fukushima/), a three-minute drama about one engineer on March 11, 2011, took second place at the 2026 Higgsfield Global Film Festival. The studio's other, longer festival entry, DETOUR, and its episodic science-fiction comedy The Great Solar Race are on the same channel; neither is yet in the collection.

The studio also sells its own production platform, AISTLabs, and much of its channel is tutorials and promotion for it. That commercial interest is part of how its film claims should be read. Individual team members and their roles are not named in the checked sources.
