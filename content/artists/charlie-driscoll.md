Driscoll is a director, cinematographer and producer associated with Northwoods Revolution and the creator of The Darkest Age. His earlier work explores Unreal Engine filmmaking and real-time animation.

## Why follow this artist

[**Tales of Grimwicke — Chapter I**](/films/tales-of-grimwicke/) connects that background to an extended, authored fantasy. His production account is unusually useful because it describes selection and editing alongside generation: hundreds of takes become a much smaller set of chosen clips.

He also articulates a visual approach. Imperfect camera movement, changing focus and weather are deliberate ways to make a generated world feel inhabited. These are choices to investigate in the film, not a claim that a tool automatically supplies cinematic judgment.

Driscoll says Chapter II is in production, with an initial release planned for his YouTube channel. A completion date has not been verified.
