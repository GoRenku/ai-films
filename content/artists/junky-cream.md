## Why follow this studio

Junky Cream presented [Kill The Rabbit](/films/kill-the-rabbit/), which won first place in Chroma Awards Season 1's Action & Adventure category. It was directed by Brian Kim and produced by John Park with UGI Production, CT Studio and Crevizu. Its credits include motion-capture actors and animators, a level-design and VFX team, and foley. That makes it a case study in AI layered onto a conventional animation pipeline.

The studio's YouTube channel mainly carries music videos, teasers and previews for its character IP, such as BRAZY and Paradox Veil. Those are not treated here as completed narrative films. The specific AI models used on Kill The Rabbit are not disclosed.
