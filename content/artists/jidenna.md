Jidenna is a musician, nominated for a Grammy, known for Classic Man and the albums The Chief and 85 to Africa. [KITA](/films/kita/), made for Wonder Studios' Beyond the Loop Season 2, is his first film as a director.

## Why follow this artist

KITA is a 26-minute drama about an elderly Nigerian man and a delivery robot, in a stop-motion style held across the whole film. Wonder Studios says he designed the world, wrote the characters and scored it himself, with no coding background, and that he made it because stop-motion stories about Black characters rarely get made. He is worth following to see whether that first film becomes a body of work.
