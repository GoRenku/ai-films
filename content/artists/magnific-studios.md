Magnific Studios is the in-house filmmaking team credited on **Candela**. Its releases and making-of material come from the same company that supplies the production platform; that relationship matters when weighing its process claims.

## Why follow this studio

Candela gives the journal a complete, accessible relationship story alongside a concrete explanation of how shots were developed. The craft question is how references, movement and sound support a recognisable character across a change in age, rather than how many models can be demonstrated in one reel.

The official film page links the studio's X account. Its production videos provide a useful source for future disclosures, while individual direction credits and a complete crew list for Candela are not supplied in the checked material. Credits from other Magnific-associated filmmakers are not transferred to this studio film.
