Magnific Studios is the in-house filmmaking team credited on **Candela**. Its releases and making-of material come from the same company that supplies the production platform; that relationship matters when weighing its process claims.

## Why follow this studio

Candela gives the journal a complete, accessible relationship story alongside a concrete explanation of how shots were developed. The craft question is how references, movement and sound support a recognisable character across a change in age, rather than how many models can be demonstrated in one reel.

The official film page links the studio's X account. Its production videos provide a useful source for future disclosures, while individual direction credits and a complete crew list for Candela are not supplied in the checked material. Credits from other Magnific-associated filmmakers are not transferred to this studio film.

## Roots — family continuity across ages

[Roots](/films/roots/) is another verified studio-authored short, with a complete **4:41** player on its official page. Fredo’s connection to a tree joins a family story to continuity across childhood and later life. The gallery separately lists several ages of Fredo; it does not identify human performers. Individual directing credits and the film’s exact generation pipeline remain undisclosed.

## October 26 — a separately credited maker's production note

Magnific's **[The Power of Now](https://x.com/magnific/status/2114356446615974273)** post explicitly credits **Patricia Camuñas**, rather than assigning the work to the in-house studio. The complete approximately **1:03** presentation links different lives through persistence. It is retained here as a sourced craft note on a platform-published motivational montage, without adding a standalone narrative-film card.

The [production thread](https://x.com/magnific/status/2114402169017573381) names **Seedance 2.5** for text-to-video with **no image references**, and **ElevenLabs Music v2** for the soundtrack. Its [separate poster note](https://x.com/magnific/status/2114402220804551017) says Camuñas used film frames as references with **Magnific One**. That latter image-reference step concerns posters, not a contradiction of the stated reference-free video process. These are platform/maker claims; costs, full crew and financial terms are undisclosed, and the tools are not retroactively assigned to Candela or Roots.
