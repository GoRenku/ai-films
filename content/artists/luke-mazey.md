## Why follow this filmmaker

Luke Mazey directs under **Slophouse**, with an official portfolio spanning short films, series and music videos. [The Reformation Room](/films/the-reformation-room/) gives repeated workplace procedures a moral consequence across a fifteen-part first season. [Solo Reign](/films/solo-reign/) instead turns choreography into a contest for power. Those different forms share an interest in readable actions and sustained character situations.

His filmmaker biography describes a one-person operation covering writing, directing, character documentation, shot planning, sound and editing. That is the maker’s account of his practice, not a verified complete crew list for every release. The work-specific sources do not disclose exact generation models or budgets.

The official Austin AI Film Festival results name **The Reformation Room** as the 2026 Series winner. The portfolio also lists forthcoming projects separately from its released work; those plans are not treated here as completed films.
