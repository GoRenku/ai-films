## Why follow this collaboration

[Asiri Ilu Awon Osu](/films/asiri-ilu-awon-osu/) brings Yoruba-language storytelling and Nollywood performers into a hybrid science-fiction production. The reason to follow it is the cultural specificity of the work: questions of power, faith and justice are carried through a local language and a recognizable performance tradition.

Tribune reports a joint statement identifying Mysterious Tech Studio and actress Tanwa Hameed as co-creators. The current official rental artwork credits **Tanwa Hammed** as producer and **Adeniyi Bukola and Yusuf Ahmad** as directors. The poster spelling is preserved in the film credits rather than silently changing the credited name.

The creators report a three-month production in June–September 2025 and an approximately 80% AI-generated visual component. This is a producer disclosure, not a measured allocation of every production task. No exact model versions or full team count are established by the checked sources. Promotional first-ever and longest-film claims are not adopted as historical facts.
