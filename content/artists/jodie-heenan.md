## Why follow this filmmaker

OMNI credits **Jodie Heenan (Australia)** with [Guardians of the Burrow](/films/guardians-of-the-burrow/), its short-form documentary winner at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia. Her release notes are unusually specific about method: documentary framing rules, hundreds of generated images corrected against reference photography, and an open acknowledgement of remaining glitches.

Her website describes more than two decades across animation, feature-film VFX, broadcast design and graphics, now applied to commercial campaigns and narrative films made with generative AI; much of her channel is commercial work. Her channel also cites wins at the AI International Film Festival, which have not been checked here. No X profile is linked from her site, so none is listed.
