## Why follow this filmmaker

[Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde](/films/dr-jekyll-and-mr-hyde/) offers a concrete way to examine hybrid direction: preserve the filmed actors while allowing the environment to change. The performer remains an anchor against which the plausibility and dramatic value of the altered world can be judged.

Absurd's interview identifies Raphael as the filmmaker and records her direction of the adaptation. It is a platform-published production account, with that commercial context explicit. A separate verified personal X profile, full biography and complete filmography are not established here.
