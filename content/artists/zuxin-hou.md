## Why follow this artist

Hou and Mark Wachholz worked as Bicycle Kids on [A Message for the Butterfly](/films/a-message-for-the-butterfly/). The collaboration brings a director with a documentary background into a film that treats memory as something that can travel across imagined bodies and futures.
