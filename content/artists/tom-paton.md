## Why follow this artist

[Where the Robots Grow](/films/where-the-robots-grow/) gives Paton a completed feature-length reference. **Non Player Combat** asks a different question about the boundary between writing, simulation and editing.

His interview about planning through the edit is useful for filmmakers: identify the scenes and performances the story needs, then generate material to fill those needs. That is more actionable than a promise that a new model will make a whole film unaided.
