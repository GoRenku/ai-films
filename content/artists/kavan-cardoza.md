Kavan Cardoza writes and directs as Kavan the Kid. **Echo Hunter**, produced with Arcana Labs and Phantom X, brings a credited ensemble cast into a generated science-fiction world.

## Why follow this artist

His work offers a way to explore the relationship between human performance and synthetic imagery. The actor remains part of the creative process, even when the visible world is generated.
