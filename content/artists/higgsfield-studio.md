Higgsfield Studio is the releasing studio behind **Passport Rush**. This profile credits the studio account, not an unidentified individual director. Its own project brief describes animators, an in-house color artist, a composer and manually finished sound; individual names and the total team size were not established from the sources reviewed.

## Why follow this studio

The useful evidence is the relationship between a complete story and the decisions behind it. Passport Rush uses a missed-flight deadline to organize escalating action, while its published breakdown explains where human animation and editing were needed to control movement and comic timing. It also documents failures rather than presenting every generated shot as a success.

Higgsfield sells the production platform used for the film. The breakdown is therefore a commercially interested first-party account, not an independent performance benchmark.
