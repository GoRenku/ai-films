## Why follow this filmmaker

Tamara Llorens and [Miguel Picazo](/artists/miguel-picazo/) co-created [The Siesta Job](/films/the-siesta-job/). Its elderly couple behaves like outlaw teenagers: physical limitations supply obstacles without removing their agency. That is a useful character problem for animated comedy.

Llorens credits Seedance 2 in Dreamina and editing in CapCut, and discloses support through the Dreamina AI partner programme. These are her production disclosures. OPUS names Tamara Llorens on its Best AI Animation award.
