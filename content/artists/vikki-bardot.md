**Vikki Bardot**, also known as Gizem Avcıoğlu, directs **A Woman Asleep** and is a credited co-writer of [Post Truth](/films/post-truth/). Her earlier **Dreamt by Another** has a MUBI listing describing an allegory about becoming real, identity and memory.

## Why follow this filmmaker

These works make synthetic imagery part of their subject: what it means to see, remember and exist. That is a distinctive direction for AI filmmaking, where the production medium can shape an argument or a subjective experience.
