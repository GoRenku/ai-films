**Odair Faléco** is the maker of [The Tool](/films/the-tool/), an authored short video essay about AI's creative promise and its capacity for control. His official portfolio establishes the public account connection and a broader filmmaking practice. His earlier [Clothes of Deception](/films/clothes-of-deception/) (2025), a minimal, slow-paced Zen tale produced with zero1cine, was a finalist at ITU's AI for Good Film Festival 2025.
