Stephane Benini wrote and directed [REDLINE](/films/redline-stephane-benini/), a Kling AI Original made with Kling 4.0. He was also one of the credited image and animation artists on PJ Ace's [Sky Line Heist](/films/sky-line-heist/), alongside REDLINE's post and VFX lead Bruno Detante.

## Why follow this artist

Racing is a demanding test for generated video: speed, consistent cars and readable geography. REDLINE is his first credited film as writer-director in the catalogue. His own channel and portfolio have not yet been identified.
