Kelly Boesch publishes visual art and music through her own portfolio and Kelly Boesch AI Art channel. Her creator-linked directory connects the website, X account, YouTube and music releases under **Kelly Boesch**. The project’s generated voice is not assigned to an invented human vocalist.

## Why follow this artist

[Between Me and You](/films/between-me-and-you/) places a recognisable emotional problem inside an unfamiliar fashion world. The song concerns the period when attraction has ended but a relationship continues. Elongated figures, sculptural clothes and futuristic dance give that inward distance a visual vocabulary, rather than simply illustrating each lyric.

Her production account also exposes a useful creative compromise: a Midjourney style reference kept imposing an extreme body shape. She retained it because the fashion and artificial appearance suited the work, then sought unusual movement through Kling. This makes the choice legible as art direction with constraints, rather than proof that the software delivered every intention unchanged. Exact model settings, collaborators and total crew remain undisclosed.

## A different visual workflow

[Come a Little Closer](/films/come-a-little-closer/) pairs an original song about trust and surrender with Pika keyframe transitions. Its [complete release description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-oK8_B31v1M) credits Boesch’s lyrics and creative direction with Suno assistance, and acknowledges Pika-supplied credits while describing the video as not an advertisement. That Pika workflow is kept separate from Between Me and You’s Kling animation.

## October 22 — Somewhere Along the Way

[Somewhere Along the Way](/films/somewhere-along-the-way/) carries Boesch’s memory of Japan into dance and sculptural costume. Her own lyrics, Suno song, Midjourney images and Veo animation are separately credited. This extends her song-length practice through another visual workflow, without assigning it the Pika or Kling pipeline of earlier videos.

## October 23 — a storytelling experiment

A [complete 1:51 public post](https://x.com/kellyeld/status/2113092624227922020) shows a library-like interior and recurring fantastical figures while Boesch explicitly describes trying **Seedance 2.5 through Dreamina** with supplied credits. This is a new, work-specific model disclosure and a move toward character-led storytelling. It is recorded as a production experiment, rather than assigning the title suggested in a viewer reply or presenting a separately verified finished film. Her released music videos retain their own distinct tool credits.
