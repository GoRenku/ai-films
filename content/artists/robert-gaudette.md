[A Face Only a Mother Could Love](/films/a-face-only-a-mother-could-love/) offers a concise entry point to Gaudette’s work.

## Why follow this artist

The attraction is character comedy: what someone believes about himself can diverge from what the world sees. A film built on that gap needs expressive reactions and timing, not just continuity of appearance. AIF’s 2026 Grand Prix provides a reason to investigate the work; it is not a substitute for judging it on screen.
