## Why follow this artist

[More Tears Than Harm](/films/more-tears-than-harm/) connects childhood memories of Madagascar with the present. Rakotomanana’s portfolio also includes live-action work and fiction. That range gives viewers a way to follow how the filmmaker uses images and rhythm across different forms.
