## Why follow this artist

Marcelo Pinto Coelho signs his work as Cello. CapCut describes [CLOSET](/films/closet/) as his debut short: a nearly wordless, stop-motion-style story told in two mirrored rooms, which won the Grand Prix at CapCut's CRE[AI]TE festival in 2026. Its strength is restraint: one look held across every scene and a structure that does the explaining instead of dialogue.
