## Why follow this studio

[Castle Walls](/films/castle-walls/) provides a serial rather than single-short test: recurring characters and political relationships have to survive an episode boundary.

AI Yapım describes itself as a production company spun out of AY Yapım. Its proprietary production platform is a studio-level disclosure; it should not be mistaken for a complete model list for every episode.
