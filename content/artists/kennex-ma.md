Kennex Ma is a Hong Kong-based AI filmmaker whose [original portfolio](https://www.kennexma.com/) describes more than 19 years in advertising. It links his own release of **What is Real?**, alongside commercial and experimental work.

## Why follow this filmmaker

[What is Real?](/films/what-is-real/) gives his practice a specific narrative question: how does a student judge reality in a world where technology organizes experience? That character perspective distinguishes a story about synthetic life from a catalogue of futuristic imagery.

In a [September 26 direct interview](https://hongkonglei.com/aifj_kennex-ma/), Ma describes working alone at home and integrating online generation with conventional finishing. He names OpenArt.ai, Google Flow and Jimeng as frequent platforms. That is his general practice, not a verified tool inventory for this particular film. No personal X profile has been established from the checked portfolio and release sources.
