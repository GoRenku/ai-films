Li Dongshen directs **Beyond Wukong**, a Mango TV series set after the events of _Journey to the West_.

## Why follow this artist

Serial storytelling brings a different creative challenge: carrying characters, environments and visual decisions across successive episodes. This is a studio-scale production, distinct from the solo and small-team films elsewhere in the collection.
