Jackson’s catalogue moves from early generated video to animation and photoreal period drama. The useful thread is his interest in **a film as a set of authored alternatives**: different visual treatments, protagonists and endings.

## Why follow this artist

Compare [A Very Long Carriage Ride](/films/a-very-long-carriage-ride/) with [Strings](/films/strings/). The former asks what a change of visual style does to the same material; the latter makes the ending variable. Those are questions about form and spectatorship, beyond the ability to generate an attractive shot.

His production pages disclose budgets and solo authorship as maker-reported figures. Claims of being the world’s first, or of indistinguishable human performance, are not treated here as independently established facts.
