## Why follow this artist

Solina’s portfolio spans real-time animation, virtual cinematography and genre shorts. In [DEADRISE](/films/deadrise/), his interest in lighting becomes a storytelling constraint. He sets the rescue aboard a shadowy ship, where how much the audience can see is part of how the horror works. His release notes make the move from virtual production to generative video legible by identifying the model, the production window and the sound editor.
