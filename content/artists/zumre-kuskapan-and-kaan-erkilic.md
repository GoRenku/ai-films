## Why follow this work

[Morek](/films/morek/) is a starting point for following Zeynep Zümre Kuşkapan & Kaan Erkılıç’s filmmaking. The competition describes a hand-painted world in which family ties and violence share the same setting. Kuşkapan identifies Erkılıç as her collaborator on the short.
