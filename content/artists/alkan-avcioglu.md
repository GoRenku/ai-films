## Why follow this artist

[Post Truth](/films/post-truth/) treats the credibility of images as its subject. The overlap between medium and argument is the reason to follow it: synthetic pictures can illustrate a claim, but they can also make the viewer question the act of illustration itself.

The official festival credits identify writing, editing and music roles, making authorship visible behind the generated material.
