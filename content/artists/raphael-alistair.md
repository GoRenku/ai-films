## Why follow this filmmaker

OMNI credits **Raphaël Alistair (France)** with [The Man behind the Cartoon](/films/the-man-behind-the-cartoon/), its Best Editing winner at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia. The short, which he wrote and directed, imagines a 1930s actor who secretly takes the injuries of a cartoon rabbit.

He presents it as the first installment of ANOMALIES, a fiction series exploring human behaviour through altered realities, and his channel describes his work the same way. Further instalments, a personal site, X profile and production tools have not been verified, so none are listed.
