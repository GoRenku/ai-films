## Why follow this filmmaker

OMNI credits **Muhammed Jabir (UK)** with [Lilies](/films/lilies/), winner of its Golden AI Performance award at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia. The film is released by Muja Studios, which describes itself as an AI-native cinematic studio in London telling original, character-driven stories; Big Screen Hack lists the team leader as Muja (Jabir Mohammed), working solo.

Lilies earns attention for its scale: a quiet two-person encounter carried by performance, made in 48 hours according to the studio. The studio channel also advertises brand work. A personal portfolio, X profile and wider filmography have not been verified, so none are listed.
