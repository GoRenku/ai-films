Eliott Mogenet’s own biography identifies him as a French writer-director based in Los Angeles, working in live action and AI production. His portfolio lists **Here You Can Stay**, an 11:30 mother-and-son beach drama, and **Foujan**, a seven-minute animated story about a DJ losing her hearing in Tehran. The portfolio’s **After the Stars** is expressly a hybrid trailer, while two features are in development; neither is presented here as a released feature.

## Why follow this director

The useful thread is a social situation turned into a dramatic pressure point. [The Offsite](/films/the-offsite/) makes corporate team-building the premise for horror. His other portfolio loglines concern family separation and the cost of a final performance. These are concrete story problems, rather than generic demonstrations of a model.

For The Offsite he names Seedance 2.5 on Dreamina, and explains working on a continuous camera pull-back. Those disclosures concern that short; they do not establish the tools or crew of his other films.
