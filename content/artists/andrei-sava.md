Andrei Sava is a Romanian director whose **Yuki** premiered at the AI International Film Festival in September 2026.

## Why follow this artist

The festival describes a parallel between a collapsing civilization and an animal in a coma. That premise puts a specific emotional idea at the center of the generated world.
