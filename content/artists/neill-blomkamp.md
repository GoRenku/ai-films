## Why follow this artist

[Nightborne](/films/nightborne/) is explicitly described by its maker as a production test. That distinction matters: the useful evidence is what it reveals about directing generated material, collaborating with concept artists and using real people’s faces and voices.

The journal includes it as a process case, not a declaration that established directorial credentials settle questions of artistic quality.
