## Why follow this producer

[Baby, You Flew](/films/baby-you-flew/) brings Claudia into a car-centred music-video fan film. The interesting disclosure is what happened after the first generated pass. Sabatella says the initial song made the idea worth pursuing, but the shots were poor; he manually redid them and then repaired the edit.

That is a concrete use of production experience: judging which material works, replacing it and making a sequence hold together.
