## Why follow this filmmaker

Andrew Salter's [JAILBIRD](/films/jailbird/) won Gold at Runway's 2025 AI Festival. His release description explains a method that is easy to follow and test: he drew illustrated storyboards first, described them to AI, then refined the generated shots for the edit.
