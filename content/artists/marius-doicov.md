## Why follow this work

[RendAI-vous](/films/rendai-vous/) is a starting point for following Marius Doicov’s filmmaking. Doicov describes a courtship disrupted by technology. The film puts the spectacle of virtual space to work as a contrast with the simplicity of a real encounter.
