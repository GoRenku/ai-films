Damien Steck and Julien Petit are the creators and directors of [Kira](/films/kira/). The original announcement connects their prior practice to cinematography, documentaries and **Cherry**; it identifies Steck’s cinematography role rather than assigning both directors identical credits on that film.

## Why follow these filmmakers

Kira’s living planet makes environment more than background: the world changes while its young protagonist searches for her father. Following how that idea is sustained through an episode is more useful than treating a concept image as a finished production.
