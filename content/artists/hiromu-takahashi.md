## Why follow this artist

[Let There Be Light](/films/let-there-be-light/) is a starting point for following Hiromu Takahashi’s work with AI in narrative filmmaking. The programme describes shifts through silent cinema, musicals and 1980s imagery as the character moves through simulated memories. The changing visual styles are part of the story’s experiment with emotion.
