## Why follow this artist

[Haydon](/films/haydon/) extends Sabean’s practice into an imagined historical world. [Founder Mode](/films/founder-mode/) and [Nothing Gold Can Stay](/films/nothing-gold-can-stay/) provide shorter entry points into satire and literary adaptation.

The range matters: a consistent editorial intelligence can be more revealing than a consistent visual preset. These works ask generated imagery to perform different jobs—reconstruction, parody and emotional reinterpretation.
