## Why follow this filmmaker

ITU credits **Vic Wanchana** (Vic Wanchana Intrasombat) with [HOMO SAPIENS FAMILY](/films/homo-sapiens-family/), third at the AI for Good Film Festival 2026. ITU says he has worked in concept art and art direction for sixteen years. It also reports that he sees the film partly as a message to his own industry and calls AI "a new brush".

The festival page is the verified credit source. No personal site or social handle confirmed as his was found, so none is added.
