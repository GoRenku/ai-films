## Why this film matters

**Candela** builds its premise around two moments: a first kiss and the memory of it on the same shore many years later. That makes identity and emotional continuity part of the storytelling problem. Small gestures and a repeated location carry more weight here than a succession of spectacular transformations.

Its inclusion recognises a complete dramatic premise, available full film and specific production evidence. It is not a claim of an independent critical review of the entire film.
