## Why this episode matters

The dramatic centre remains a recorded performance while the surrounding architecture becomes malleable. That is a useful hybrid filmmaking problem: a transformation must follow an actor's gesture and emotional timing, and the new room must still hold together when the camera moves.

Raphael describes a noir exterior reality and more expressionistic colour for Jekyll's mind and flashbacks. Her production account makes room geography, reference plates and comparisons with the original take part of the directing process. The result is worth exploring as a performance-led adaptation, rather than treating generated scenery as a substitute for acting.
