## Why this film matters

Whenever the dog behind the fence barks, Grandma pulls her little dog closer, and in time the little dog comes to hear that bark as the feeling of being held. One day she stops and listens instead of walking on. The premise is built on a learned association rather than an event, which asks a seven-minute animation to show feeling forming through repetition.

The release artwork sets the register: a painted field of pampas grass at sunset, an old woman in work clothes and two small dogs on the path. Junho Her's [The Gift](/films/the-gift-junho-her/), which won at Warsaw Glitch, also told its story through people and animals; WARM narrows it to a single household and one sound.

## How it was made

Junho Her wrote and directed WARM under his GRAB AI STUDIO banner, released with Korean and English synopses. It was created with Higgsfield for the Higgsfield Talent Contest. The models, crew and schedule have not been disclosed.
