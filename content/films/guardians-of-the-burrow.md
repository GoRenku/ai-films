## Why this film matters

Guardians of the Burrow applies the grammar of a natural-history film to images no camera captured. The maker describes holding herself to documentary rules (locked compositions, patient camera moves, macro detail in focus) and checking generated animals against published photography of the two species. That makes it a useful test of whether generated imagery can serve a factual subject without drifting into spectacle, and it raises the obvious caveat that the footage is illustration, not observation.

OMNI International AI Film Festival named it winner of **Documentary, Short Form** at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia in Sydney on July 3, 2026.
