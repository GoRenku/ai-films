## Why this film matters

The significant question is whether a director’s established visual sensibility survives a new production method. Concept art, performance selection and editing become the points at which intention must pass into generated footage.

Blomkamp calls this a test toward a possible feature. It is a released short, not evidence that a planned feature has already been completed.
