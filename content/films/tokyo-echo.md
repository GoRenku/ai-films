## Why this film matters

The short makes a city portrait out of the people who imagine it. It uses invented bodies to carry feelings gathered from accounts of the real city.

AI Film Festival Japan programmed the 5:00 short and awarded it **SPECIAL MENTION** at its 2026 edition.

TYO’s official release runs about five minutes. Uchino’s production note describes drawing on online comments about Tokyo to build fictional characters.
