## Why this series matters

A continuing series asks a filmmaker to keep promises: characters must remain recognizable, geography must survive reverse angles, and later episodes must develop earlier choices. That makes The Chronicles of Bone an important counterpart to Cardoza's Echo Hunter films.

## A specific continuity problem

Magnific's July account of **The Nightmare Below**, season one's third chapter, describes a conversation aboard a moving ship. Reverse angles had to preserve the vessel's direction, props and lighting. These are ordinary directing concerns made newly difficult by generated images.

For that episode, Cardoza used Nano Banana 2 for locations and character references and Seedance 2.0 for video. For group shots he supplied an image establishing positions, explicitly treating it as a spatial guide. That is a practical technique for protecting blocking across cuts.

The breakdown also describes composing music prompts around dramatic timing. Tools evolve between episodes; these model names belong to episode three's disclosed workflow, not necessarily the whole series.
