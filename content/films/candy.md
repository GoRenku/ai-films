## Why this film matters

Candy puts a moral proposition inside a concrete, absurd world rule. Its interest lies in whether an external punishment can change human intentions, rather than simply how convincingly a transformation can be rendered. That is an editorial reading of the filmmaker's stated premise, not a full-film review.

At roughly nine minutes, the original upload is a narrative short, not a sample generation. Together with Website and Waste to Energy, it gives viewers several distinct stories through which to follow Li's developing voice.
