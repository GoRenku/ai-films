## Why this film matters

Didi Li compresses a simulation premise into under three minutes: a woman is welcomed into an experiment that promises to replace her senses with a manufactured world. The difficulty is making an abstract question about consciousness legible through a single character's passage between worlds, rather than through exposition alone. It extends Jiaze Li's habit of building films around one impossible rule.

The Chroma Awards gallery places the film **third in Film - Sci-Fi & Fantasy** in Season 1. The festival entry is titled *Didi Li*; the creator's YouTube release carries a question-style title and presents the story as an adaptation of Chen Qiufan's *The Black Room*.
