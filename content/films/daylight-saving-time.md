## Why this film matters

Daylight Saving Time! won Best Long Film at the 2026 DISRUPT AI Film Festival, the category for films of three to fifteen minutes. The jury chair's report notes that the longer films were where restraint mattered most, which makes this the jury's pick among the most crowded and demanding entries.

The festival published the complete 9:48 film on its YouTube channel on November 18. Its synopsis, credits and tools have not been published.
