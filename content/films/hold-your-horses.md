## Why this film matters

A little pony watches the love of his life walk off with his perfect big brother, Thunder, and the film is about what he does next. Myrzakhan Zhumakhan tells it as a mockumentary: talking-head interviews, a stolen dating profile and, by the release's account, one interview that should never have been filmed.

The form is a sensible choice for generated characters. Mockumentary comedy depends on faces reacting to the camera and on cutting between what people say and what they did, so timing carries the jokes more than spectacle. That is the same skill Zhumakhan's [It Was Just a Joke](/films/it-was-just-a-joke/), Best Character winner at the Astana AI Film Festival, was recognised for.

## How it was made

Zhumakhan wrote and directed the four-and-a-half-minute short and released it with English and Russian descriptions. It was made with Higgsfield for the Higgsfield Talent Contest; the models and schedule have not been disclosed.
