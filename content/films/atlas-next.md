## Why this sequel matters

A hundred-year ceasefire is supposed to have secured the future. **Ten years later**, another threat arrives and a new generation takes the front line. That premise makes inheritance and interrupted peace the dramatic reason to revisit the mecha world, rather than simply commissioning another battle image.

Sho AI Studio released **seven numbered parts** between September 5 and November 7, followed on November 21 by a 27-minute **Final**. That mirrors the first [ATLAS season](/films/atlas/), which also closed with a separately titled finale. Part 1 remains the place to start.

## References before animation

Sho AI Studio explicitly describes **character design, storyboarding and visual references**, followed by individually generated scenes using **Seedance 2.0 and 2.5**. This is a useful distinction: the world and the scene’s intent are prepared before the moving image is generated. The description does not establish a version for every shot or a complete audio workflow.

## November 21 — the Final

The Final is the longest instalment of NEXT, a few minutes longer than any numbered part. Its description repeats the series premise and the same production method: character design, storyboards and visual references first, then individually generated scenes with Seedance 2.0 and 2.5. With eight releases over eleven weeks, the studio has now completed two serial seasons on a steady schedule.

Voice and music credits, production cost and team size have not been disclosed.
