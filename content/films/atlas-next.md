## Why this sequel matters

A hundred-year ceasefire is supposed to have secured the future. **Ten years later**, another threat arrives and a new generation takes the front line. That premise makes inheritance and interrupted peace the dramatic reason to revisit the mecha world, rather than simply commissioning another battle image.

The original releases establish **six complete parts**, from September 5 through October 24. Part 6 runs approximately **19:39**; Part 1 remains the starting link. These are distinct from the first [ATLAS season](/films/atlas/), whose finale is also preserved in its own guide.

## References before animation

Sho AI Studio explicitly describes **character design, storyboarding and visual references**, followed by individually generated scenes using **Seedance 2.0 and 2.5**. This is a useful distinction: the world and the scene’s intent are prepared before the moving image is generated. The description does not establish a version for every shot or a complete audio workflow.

Full original descriptions, release dates, native complete players, durations and real artwork were checked. Individual voice and music credits, production cost and team size remain unknown; no full-series critical viewing is claimed.
