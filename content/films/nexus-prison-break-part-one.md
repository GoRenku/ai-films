## Why this episode matters

Episode 8 brings Anika from Wet Markets of Kafar together with Jace from Squid Heist. In the mechanics-bay scene, the two swap prison clothes for work uniforms. Returning characters now have to act together inside one continuing story. PJ Ace cites Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind as an influence.

The original 4:40 release is Part I. PJ Ace announced Parts II and III. The production notebook below records his reported schedule, cost split and what he disclosed about the picture and sound.

## November 15 — Keeping a voice through the cut

In the [team’s public voice guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vY5tu8qaQG4elTXmgoS5qy5-bJ1o8uu6K2Esh7U1lQE/edit), the Crimson Knight’s voice starts with a different face. The team describes generating a scarred Viking to get a rougher reading, then extracting the audio to use as a reference for the masked Knight. The guide recommends keeping takes short for tests, leaving physical beats between dialogue lines and using the same chosen reference across shots. It describes optional human recordings and audio processing, with individual performer and sound credits still unconfirmed.
