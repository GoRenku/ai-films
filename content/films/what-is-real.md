## Why this film matters

Its central filmmaking problem is communicating doubt through a character’s experience of an apparently ordered world. The science-fiction setting gives visual design a dramatic purpose: help the viewer question the evidence available to Alice. Ma’s decision to work without dialogue makes framing, action and editing carry that burden.
