## Why this film matters

Remember Me works in the micro-drama format: vertical, two minutes and built for phone viewing, a format usually associated with serialised melodrama. It applies those constraints to a single high-stakes premise of being erased from other people's memory and fighting to be remembered. The test is whether that emotional arc can resolve inside a format designed for speed.

The Chroma Awards gallery lists it as the **first-place winner in Film - Micro-Dramas** in Season 1.
