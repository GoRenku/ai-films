Beyond Wukong premiered on Mango TV and Hunan Satellite TV on August 31, 2026. The first five episodes of about 40 minutes arrived in its opening week. Mango TV's official YouTube channel now carries ten full, captioned episodes, the sixth to tenth uploaded on October 7 and 8. A planned 30-episode run is not the same as thirty released episodes.

## Why this series matters

The demanding part of a series is keeping a world available for the next story. Li Dongshen's interview emphasizes that characters, settings and visual decisions can be reused, lowering the cost of later episodes. That is a more useful production observation than a generic claim that AI is cheap.

The reported 109 characters and 143 environments make continuity a substantial creative responsibility. This is also a nearly hundred-person production: the evidence supports a changing studio workflow, not a claim that professional roles have disappeared.

## The human layer

The director describes human control over writing, character and emotion, together with human dubbing. Generated imagery is one part of the production. The [director interview](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-09-11/doc-inirnnxu8872119.shtml) is the source for the asset-reuse argument and reported economics.
