Beyond Wukong premiered on Mango TV and Hunan Satellite TV on August 31, 2026. The initial release contains five episodes of approximately 40 minutes; a planned 30-episode run is not the same as thirty released episodes.

## Why this series matters

The demanding part of a series is keeping a world available for the next story. Li Dongshen's interview emphasizes that characters, settings and visual decisions can be reused, lowering the cost of later episodes. That is a more useful production observation than a generic claim that AI is cheap.

The reported 109 characters and 143 environments make continuity a substantial creative responsibility. This is also a nearly hundred-person production: the evidence supports a changing studio workflow, not a claim that professional roles have disappeared.

## The human layer

The director describes human control over writing, character and emotion, together with human dubbing. Generated imagery is one part of the production.
