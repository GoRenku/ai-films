## Why this episode matters

**Adventus — The Coming** turns the arrival of winged forces into a tactical scene. Armour, firearms and a ruined urban setting make the celestial premise physical: the characters must occupy and defend space, rather than appearing only as isolated fantasy portraits. Brief dialogue, movement and an established opposing force supply a complete released instalment within the maker's larger WRATH project.

The **September 13** English original runs **2:55**. The newly posted [Italian-subtitled version](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hHP0n346vCU) is the same-length access version, not a new film or Italian vocal dub.
