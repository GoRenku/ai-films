## Why this film matters

Set in **Tokyo in 1999**, this short follows a salaryman across a Sumida bridge. He passes a woman, then finds himself returned to the same encounter until he stops. The time loop makes attention to another person a dramatic choice rather than an abstract message. This reading follows the filmmaker’s portfolio synopsis.
