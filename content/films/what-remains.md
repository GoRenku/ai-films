## Why this film matters

*What Remains* borrows the mockumentary form to look at the present as if from its aftermath: a collapsing planet, a fractured society and an artificial intelligence that sketches the end of the human. The filmmaking problem is tone. A documentary register asks the viewer to treat speculative imagery as record, while the release's own description insists that a glimmer of hope still persists.

AI Film Festival Japan gave it the **Outstanding AI Narrative Award**, runner-up in its 2025 narrative category, at the November 2, 2025 ceremony in Tokyo. That result establishes exhibition and recognition, not a critical verdict.

## Production evidence

The complete film is public on the director's channel and his portfolio lists it among his AI short films. The official artwork names a producer, Daniel Damborghini.
