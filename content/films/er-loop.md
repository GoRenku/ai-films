The creator’s full release runs approximately **19 minutes 38 seconds**. Its premise follows a man who dies on his couch and must choose a new life.

## Why this film matters

Unlimited possibility can be an obstacle rather than a reward. That gives the film a comic engine: each imagined life is another decision the protagonist must confront, connecting changing imagery to a recurring character problem.

The extended short is also a concrete example of an independent filmmaker controlling story and music together. Inclusion rests on that authored premise and available release; this is not a claim of a completed critical viewing.

## A process worth examining

The creator-linked interview describes an unusual constraint: **83 scenes with a different set each time**, using only four character reference images. Magario assembled his own likeness from phone photographs in Photoshop. The description says he developed scenes sequentially without a prewritten screenplay, and used Seedance’s native voices with low-resolution rerolls to find suitable delivery.

These are the interview’s reported choices, not controlled comparisons proving that storyboards or image references are unnecessary.
