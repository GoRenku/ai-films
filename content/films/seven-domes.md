## Why this film matters

Early in the seventeenth century a Spanish expedition crosses the desert of La Rioja, in what is now Argentina, looking for a legendary city with seven golden domes. An Indigenous elder leads them. The Higgsfield project page adds the stake the release synopsis keeps back: the elder is bargaining for his granddaughter's life with the promise of treasure. As heat and suspicion build, the search turns into a fever in which greed and mirage are hard to tell apart.

It is a familiar conquest story, the lost city of gold, told from inside its delusion, and it suits the close, sun-struck faces of the release artwork. Cráneo Films, which published it, is the studio behind [El día que me quieras](/films/el-dia-que-me-quieras/), its AI-animated Gardel film for the University of Buenos Aires.

## How it was made

The release credits the film to Leandro Mera and says it was made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest. The models, crew and budget have not been disclosed.
