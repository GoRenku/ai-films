## Why this film matters

The premise is one of obsolescence told from the inside. A factory robot loses its place to a newer model, so the familiar fear of being replaced by machines is turned around and given to a machine. A short with a single, clear situation like this depends on whether a manufactured face can carry feeling, which is the problem the organiser’s thumbnail, a worn robot in close-up, puts forward.

At the World AI Film Festival in Cannes on April 22, 2026, *La Sélection Mécanique* won Best AI Animation Film, according to the organiser’s prize list and its own upload of the film.
