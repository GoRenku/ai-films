## Why this video matters

The October 22 release initially shared as **Universal High Income** now has the official gallery title **WHITEPILL**, the seventh Claudia film. This entry keeps its original URL, preserving one work rather than adding a duplicate. The complete song runs approximately **3:55**.

A recurring singer moves through an imagined space colony, while mathematical transformations become tactile paper, glass and fibre-like objects. The relationship between precise geometric motion and a consistent performance world gives the song a sustained visual concept. Its optimistic predictions about AI and income are the work’s subject, not independently established economic claims.

## October 23 — the work-specific record is available

The [official film page](https://claudia.gallery/whitepill/) and [full production record](https://claudia.gallery/record/whitepill/readme/) now replace the production unknowns recorded at yesterday’s check. anabology directs, Claudia is the synthetic performer, and Suno supplies the selected song. No separate human vocalist is inferred; a Suno model version is not disclosed for this work.

The account distinguishes development from final pixels. MiniMax H3 performance guides and stills from Midjourney v8.2 and GPT Image 2.5 feed a process whose final picture is Seedance 2.5. Wan 3.0 Prime appears in abandoned cuts and one guide, while Kling 4.0 was tested. Neither is silently promoted into the finished film’s picture credit.

## What made the final cut work

For singing shots, the maker describes supplying only the song’s isolated lead vocal, trimmed to the exact take length, with the words and their timing; the full song is laid back over the selected result. The interludes start from timed GLSL geometry and stills repainted in the chosen look, then are rendered into the same material world by Seedance. The final edit has **27 segments** cut on bar lines.

The record also preserves directorial revision: dance guides caused limb problems, so selected passages were restaged through beat-specific movement instructions; an overbroad reshoot then led the director to choose between cuts shot by shot. Selection and correction are central to the account, not just the volume of generations.

The reported **$588.59** is a scoped list-price API-generation tally, not a complete film budget. About **82 hours** describes the reported production period; **31 agent launches** are software activity, not 31 people. Original release, gallery, full record and sampled imagery were checked without claiming an independent audit or complete critical review.
