## Why this film matters

The Tortoise and the Hare turns the fable inside out. Instead of a race, the synopsis sends an armed rabbit expedition into a forbidden tomb after the Golden Tortoise, and makes the hares' certainty about their enemy the mistake. Recasting a familiar moral as a tomb-raiding adventure is a compact test of whether a four-minute generated film can set up expectation and then overturn it.
