## Why this film matters

An alien ship the size of a district hangs over a quiet Japanese city, and what comes down is not an invasion but an errand. Go, a grumpy old traveller, and Mo, a small, rude child with dangerous powers, have been sent to fetch back a vintage car that their lord left with an old takoyaki seller long ago. The joke is in the scale: a cosmic visit reduced to a neighbourhood chore, with the city at risk only because the visitors are bad at small things.

Allan Ripley's earlier [The Prompter](/films/the-prompter/) was a cold psychological science fiction. OTSUKAI swings to the opposite register: a seven-minute comedy drawn in the look of 1990s Japanese television anime, with Japanese dialogue and English subtitles.

## How it was made

Ripley credits himself as writer and director and lists the pipeline in the release: Seedance 2.5 at native 1080p for video, GPT Image 2.6 and Seedream 5.5 for images, all on Higgsfield; music from Suno; editing and colour in DaVinci Resolve with Dehancer, a film-emulation plug-in that suits the period look. The film was made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest. Crew size and budget have not been disclosed.
