## Why this film matters

Fukushima compresses a real disaster into a three-minute fiction centred on one person. The studio describes it as an artistic interpretation of the events of March 11, 2011 rather than a literal reconstruction, focused on the moment when professional certainty turns into helplessness and a small human decision becomes part of a much larger catastrophe. That choice of scale — one engineer rather than the whole event — is what makes it a drama and not a re-enactment.

It took **second place** at the 2026 Higgsfield Global Film Festival. It was the second film AIST entered in the contest.

## Production evidence

The release description, the Higgsfield project and the studio's channel establish the release, the studio credit and the project's leading image model.
