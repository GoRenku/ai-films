## Why this film matters

Horel treats AI as both the instrument and the subject. Her Weaver is a figure that observes, measures and archives, yet in the director’s statement fails to grasp what makes a person singular. The film puts that question into its method: public-domain illustration models are mixed with a model trained on her own face, so the collage that examines the machine’s gaze is partly built from the artist’s image.

It began as a commissioned chapter for ARTE France’s PhantasIA research programme and became a standalone short essay. The release lists the WAIFF 2026 Press Prize among its awards, alongside honours from Artefact and the ADC Awards.
