## Why this film matters

Railbound starts from an archive rather than a prompt. Mike Brodie photographed young people riding freight trains across the United States; the film animates those images and uses a father-and-daughter conversation to imagine the stories inside them. The entrants describe treating AI as a "temporal tool": moving a real photograph forward in time instead of inventing a new world, while keeping its grain and expressions.

It won the **documentary category in Chroma Awards Season 1**. The film itself is careful about genre: its release says it blurs documentary and fiction, with credited actors voicing the characters. It is catalogued here as a documentary drama for that reason.

## Production evidence

The release and the ADC entry give a full credit list, the six-week Flow Sessions schedule, the Google Flow / Veo 3 workflow and the scale of the shot refinement. Budget and exact model versions beyond those are not stated. Google Labs supported the residency and access to Flow. The film was uploaded **November 26, 2025** and runs **10:11**.
