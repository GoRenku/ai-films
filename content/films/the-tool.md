## Why this essay matters

Faléco uses generated imagery to question the conditions of generation itself. His stated subject is AI's double promise: bringing a person's vision into reach while creating new forms of dependence, exploitation and control. The title makes that contradiction concrete—an instrument is useful, but the relationship between a person and an instrument can also reverse.
