## Why this music video matters

November Treat commits fully to a pastiche: the 80s hair-metal power ballad, played straight, about Thanksgiving. The video holds a consistent period world, from big hair and leopard print to a black and gold Trans Am on a rooftop at sunset and a 1970s harvest-gold kitchen, and gives it running comic business in a pair of pilgrim salt and pepper shakers. A sustained look and a joke that develops across the song are what separate it from a loop of styled images.

## How it was made

Higginswerx, whose catalogue also includes the feature [Mirror9: The Hollowing](/films/mirror9-the-hollowing/), directed, edited and produced it. The release names Seedance 2.5, Gemini Omni and Nano Banana for generation and DaVinci Resolve for the edit, without saying which tool handled which shots.
