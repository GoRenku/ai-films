## Why this work matters


A captain framed for treason finds refuge in a hidden valley. The existence of a calf gives his pursuer a motive to follow him. The sequel moves Darkhan, Aru and the herd onto an old salt route, changing the problem from keeping a hidden place secret to protecting creatures whose tracks can be followed.

The studio uses prehistoric mammals as the basis for its giants and identifies generated visuals and voices in the sequel’s original release. Exact models, named crew and budget are undisclosed.
