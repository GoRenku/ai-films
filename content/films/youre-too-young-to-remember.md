## Why this work matters

A dating-app match and a nervy call on a household landline become two versions of the same encounter. Andrew keeps the couple the same age in both timelines. Their faces give the viewer a reference point as the ways they meet change. Claudia’s opening scan reminds us that the 1994 scenes are a reconstruction, and counters and phone interfaces make the modern sequences feel increasingly measured.

The video won first place in the second Claudia video contest, as announced by character creator anabology. The production account credits Andrew at Direct the Machine for directing this film. The sources identify no additional human crew or voice performer.
