The original release contains the first chapter, running approximately **22 minutes 42 seconds**. Driscoll describes a dark reinterpretation of the animal stories he grew up reading. This is an available chapter, not a claim that the planned series is complete.

## Why this film matters

An extended animal fantasy asks more of a production than an isolated striking creature shot: characters, spaces and tone must remain recognizable across scenes. Driscoll’s disclosure provides a concrete way to examine how that continuity was organized.

His emphasis on a reference archive and a selective edit is particularly valuable. The number of generations does not establish artistic quality; the useful question is which performances and transitions survive selection, and how they support the story.

## Explore the craft

Driscoll describes using location plates and character turnarounds to establish visual references, then allowing the video model latitude over framing. He favors handheld imperfections, focus changes and weather over uniformly smooth camera moves. Compare those stated intentions with the chapter itself.
