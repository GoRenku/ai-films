Astra Blue's recurring blue-haired performer takes on a running joke from gaming culture: that artificial general intelligence might arrive before the next Grand Theft Auto. The November 20 release runs **4:07**.

## Why this video matters

The concept carries the whole song. Each scene borrows the look of a sun-bleached Miami crime game: rooftop pool parties, a festival stage, a coastal causeway, a magenta supercar and a five-star wanted meter. Astra moves through them as both the game's heroine and the thing that arrived first. Title cards and on-screen game interface turn the lyric into a series of staged jokes rather than a lyric-only presentation.

Earlier Astra Blue videos such as [THE APP SAYS WAIT](/films/the-app-says-wait/) and [What Am I To You](/films/what-am-i-to-you/) turned AI power into unease. This one plays the same persona for comedy and pastiche. The release describes itself as an unofficial parody, not affiliated with the game's publisher.
