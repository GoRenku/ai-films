## Why this work matters


Higginswerx builds the first episode around evidence and testimony in the Vilet Torrez case. The creator states that the visuals are a dramatization and that the generated performers’ appearances are invented. The distinction lets viewers separate the evidence being discussed from the pictures created to represent it.

The Chroma entry credits Cherie Higgins and lists a running time of 33:53. Higginswerx names ElevenLabs without specifying an exact model or assigning it to individual voices.
