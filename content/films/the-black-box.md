## Why this film matters

The ten-minute opening episode combines a specific character motive with an invented arena. The filmmaking problem is to carry that motive through a coherent visual world, rather than letting the setting become the whole attraction.

Olivos identifies shot-to-shot consistency as a central challenge. His account connects character design, light and composition to a single narrative intention. That is the part worth tracking as this miniseries develops.
