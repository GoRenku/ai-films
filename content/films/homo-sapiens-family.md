## Why this film matters

HOMO SAPIENS FAMILY uses mock-documentary to turn evolution into satire: humans who stopped adapting become the exhibits, watched by species that kept going. The format gives the film a built-in rhythm of narration and observation, and its clay-like character design keeps the joke legible.

It took third place at ITU's **AI for Good Film Festival 2026**.
