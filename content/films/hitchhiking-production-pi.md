## Why this film matters

Jessica, a French backpacker heading for Cairns, escapes a suspicious man on an empty Australian highway and gets into the first car that stops. The kind family in the front seats are not human. Production Pi calls it a strange road trip between two kinds of stranger far from home, set in 2000s Australia, and asks who the real danger on that road turned out to be.

Comedy built on a reveal needs the ordinary to look ordinary first. The release's chapter list moves from a phone call from her mother to the suspicious driver, the friendly family, an accident on the highway, a repair shop, the reveal and the way home, a clear seven-part structure for a seven-minute short.

## How it was made

Three writer-directors share the credit, and the studio's first film, the paper-craft school comedy [FIX](/films/fix/), won awards at Lumara and Higgsfield. The release says Hitchhiking was made with Higgsfield and subtitled in Korean, English and French; the models have not been named. The studio also says it has won eight AI advertising competitions.
