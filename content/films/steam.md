## Why this film matters

*Steam* sets heavy industry against classical ballet. The synopsis has a single worker’s hallucination transform the factory: soot and iron give way to a performance, and the labourers’ movement becomes dance. A film like this rests on rhythm and on the cut between two physical worlds, which is why its music is central rather than decorative.

At the World AI Film Festival in Cannes on April 22, 2026, it won Best AI Soundtrack, according to the organiser’s prize list and its own upload of the film.
