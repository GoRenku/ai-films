## Why this series matters

The Diary of Father first appeared in the catalogue with [Forever](/films/forever-special-episode/), a special about Atatürk's last days told through his friend Salih Bozok. Kuvâ-yi Milliye widens the frame from one public figure to the irregular "national forces" that fought the occupation of Anatolia before a regular army existed. The first episode, **Toroslarda Kuşatma** (Siege in the Taurus), is set in April and May 1920, and the release reduces its premise to a single line: forty-five villagers, seven hundred French soldiers.

The poster puts a woman with a dust-streaked face at its centre rather than an officer, pointing toward the villagers of the tagline rather than commanders.

## How it is made

The project again links a bibliography for the episode, as it did for Forever, and tags Higgsfield as a creator partner. Individual cast and crew credits, the specific models and the terms of the partnership have not been disclosed. The first episode runs about four minutes and is released on X.
