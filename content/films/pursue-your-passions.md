Gossip Goblin’s **October 15** original release runs **2:56**. A ruler eats amid abundance while people explain that their district has no bread. His language of freedom and personal fulfilment becomes the source of the satire.

## Why it matters

The dramatic contrast is visible before it becomes an argument: a feast occupies the powerful while hunger defines the people addressing him. Close-up exchanges make the short depend on reactions and condescension as much as on its ornate setting. The familiar promise of time for creativity becomes uncomfortable when material security is absent.

It continues the studio’s interest in power, labour and technologically transformed societies, alongside [An Honest Day’s Work](/films/an-honest-days-work/). That thematic connection is not proof that either short is a scene from Gods Don’t Give Gifts.
