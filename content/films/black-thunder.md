## Why this film matters

Black Thunder treats the generated image as a genre pastiche rather than a hunt for photorealism. Its own description promises a retro 1980s short in the mode of practical-effects fantasy: bizarre creatures, outrageous heroes, cosmic mythology and dark comedy. Holding a twelve-minute adventure to that one period look, across costumes, creatures and set pieces, is a continuity problem as much as a stylistic one.

Lumara named it the winner of the **Serenissima Award** for the best film made with Venice, and the organisers' online winners programme opens with it. The maker also released it as six numbered parts and spun out a theme song, so the film works as the centre of a small serial and musical world on the same channel.
