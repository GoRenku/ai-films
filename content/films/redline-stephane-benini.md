## Why this film matters

Motor racing is one of the hardest things to film, practically and financially: speed, space, crashes and the cost of getting any of it wrong. REDLINE is an 11-minute attempt to do it with generation instead, and Kling's own release says the aim was to keep the human reason people race at the centre rather than only the spectacle. The opening card, Five years later, signals a story about a return rather than a single race.

## How it was made

Stephane Benini wrote and directed it with a small credited team: Yves Dalbiez editing, Rodrigo Orozco producing, Bruno Detante on post and VFX, and un.studios on sound and music. It was made with Kling 4.0 as a Kling AI Original, so the tool company commissioned and released it. Benini and Detante were earlier credited for images and animation on PJ Ace's [Sky Line Heist](/films/sky-line-heist/).
