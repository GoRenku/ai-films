## Why this film matters

TechTalkNavigator revisited four episodes to make a single festival cut. The creator describes adjusting the material and adding previously unreleased scenes. That makes the cut useful for looking at how a serial story is shaped into one screening.

The festival programmed this named cut and gave it the CapCut Award. The linked upload plays the full compilation and runs about 15 minutes.
