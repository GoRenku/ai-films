## Why this film matters

The premise makes a decision the engine of suspense. Its interest for this collection is how a filmmaker can establish a rule, make the audience anticipate a consequence and sustain tension within a very short running time. It also adds a horror direction to an artist already represented by the fantasy of [The Fall](/films/the-fall/) and the comedy of [Grimble & Wobbles](/films/grimble-and-wobbles/).
