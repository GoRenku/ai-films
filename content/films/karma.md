Monzoni released **KARMA** on October 10 with a deliberately spare setup: two buttons, and one wrong choice. The complete original X video is approximately **1:42**, rather than an announcement or trailer.

## Why this film matters

The premise makes a decision the engine of suspense. Its interest for this collection is how a filmmaker can establish a rule, make the audience anticipate a consequence and sustain tension within a very short running time. It also adds a horror direction to an artist already represented by the fantasy of [The Fall](/films/the-fall/) and the comedy of [Grimble & Wobbles](/films/grimble-and-wobbles/).

The release and playback were checked; this entry is not a full critical review. The creator’s competition statement does not establish an award or a commercial relationship.

## What is disclosed

The October 10 release did not identify the models, editing or sound tools, individual collaborators, budget or production schedule. The later CapCut disclosure below adds a production tool and campaign context. Tools credited on Monzoni’s earlier films are not assumed to apply here.

## October 29 — a further production disclosure

Monzoni’s [new share](https://x.com/JossMonzoni/status/2115811511826542953) names **CapCut Video Studio** and says the film was made for the **CapCut Halloween campaign**. The 1:42 player and original thumbnail match the existing short, so this is updated context for KARMA, not a second film or a new original release date.

The campaign association is now explicit alongside the earlier competition statement. The new post does not establish payment terms, the underlying model, separate sound tools or a complete crew.
