## Why this film matters

The form and subject meet at the same question: how do viewers decide that a person on screen is real? That gives cosmetic perfection a dramatic role rather than making it the sole measure of success.

AIF names the short a 2026 Honoree. Clark also discusses its relationship to his developing feature Touch Grass in his studio essay.
