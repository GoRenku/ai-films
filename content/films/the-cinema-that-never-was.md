## Why this film matters

*The Cinema That Never Was* turns a question about loss into a form: not abandoned projects, but films that were never conceived at all. As a video essay it relies on narration and an invented archive, so the filmmaking problem is making imagined film history feel remembered while keeping clear that it never existed.

AI Film Festival Japan named it winner of the **Best AI Documentary Award** at its 2025 edition, announced on November 2, 2025. The maker's release lists many other festival prizes; those are his claims, and this entry records only the AIFJ result from the organiser.

## Production evidence

Wachholz names an unusually full tool chain in the release description, from Kling 2.0 Master and Runway Gen-4 to Midjourney V7 Alpha, Udio and ElevenLabs, and says GPT-4o wrote the script from his concept. Platform creator-programme support is disclosed. Team size, schedule and budget are unknown.
