## Why this film matters

The camera within the story makes selection and memory part of the subject. The useful question is which ordinary moments become significant when a child records them—and how synthetic imagery can support the feeling of a personal archive.

AIF credits Léo Cannone and New Forest Film, and names the film a 2026 Honoree. The complete film premiered on NOWNESS on December 5, 2025 (see the November 2 note below).
