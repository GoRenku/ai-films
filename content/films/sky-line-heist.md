## Why this film matters

A fantastic creature becomes the center of a concrete conflict: Goldie, a squid that produces liquid gold, is held aboard an extravagant train. The premise turns world-building into stakes. The short extends Nexus's recurring interest in creatures under threat.

## What the team disclosed

PJ Ace reports a **$29,575 budget and ten-day production**, including five full-time animators. These are creator-reported figures, not a general price for making an AI film. The public credits name eight people across writing, direction, imagery, animation and editing.

## Practical decisions worth studying

- **Direct visible behavior.** The breakdown recommends specifying a pause, a glance or a change in posture instead of naming an emotion and accepting a generic performance.
- **Track story states.** A character reference should change when the story changes the character's appearance. The team created separate references for different physical states.
- **Establish relative scale.** A clean reference of a character carrying Goldie provided a size relationship that could be reused in other scenes.

These are the team's disclosed techniques, not universal guarantees. The linked post includes the released video and the full thread; its player includes additional material, so we have not assigned a precise narrative runtime.
