Realista lists **Selva**, **Antinferno** and **Limbo** as available episodes of its Dante adaptation. Further circles remain in production. The landing page’s season headline is not evidence that every episode is finished.

BAIFF’s official programme lists INFERNO in the October 15 afternoon session beginning **16:30** at **Fondazione Querini Stampalia, Venice**. Realista identifies the presentation as guest screenings of Antinferno and Limbo. The programme’s combined “23” duration is not assigned here to a single episode. A scheduled presentation is not a claim that the screening has already taken place.

## Why it matters

Dante offers a demanding structure for serial image-making: each new environment must express a different moral condition while retaining the journey’s continuity. The explicitly composed soundtrack also gives this project a clear artistic identity beyond its generation method.

The studio labels the early episodes free, but selecting one currently opens an **email-unlock form**. That access condition is why this entry is not labelled unrestricted Watch now. No email was submitted and full playback was not verified. Episode runtimes, exact visual models, budget and total crew remain unknown.
