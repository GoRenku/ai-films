## Why this film matters

The release opens on a reversal: the sun is not dead, it is held captive. An endless winter has fallen on a forgotten northern village, and a young woman is chosen to carry the last ember out into the frozen wilderness. Sasha Fevralskaya calls it a dark folklore tale about fear, hope, sacrifice and what it costs to bring back the light.

Fevralskaya won first place in Curious Refuge's 2026 AI Horror Film Competition with [Kagome](/films/kagome/), a restrained psychological horror built on a children's game. The Last Warmth moves from a domestic ritual to a folk one, and the release image, a hare mask painted like a festival object over a face wrapped in fur, suggests the same interest in costume and ritual as the source of dread.

## How it was made

Fevralskaya is credited as AI director. The six-minute short was made in Higgsfield Cinema Studio for the Higgsfield Talent Contest 2026, and the release links its project page. The models and crew have not been disclosed.
