Episode 1 follows Nora; the series is planned to retell the same day from four more characters' points of view.

## Why this series matters

Nora Bennett has been cast as Tree Number Three, without a line, in a school play her older sister once starred in. When her best friend Hugo freezes under the pressure of the lead role, she finds she has a bigger part than she thought. It is a small, warm premise, and the structure is the interesting part: one ordinary Brooklyn day, told episode by episode from five different points of view, so later episodes can reframe what Nora saw.

## How it was made

Magnific credits the full team, which is unusual: Cristina Polo wrote and directed, six artists including Polo are credited for the AI visuals, and Myriam Mira edited and did the sound and music. The tools are listed too: Seedance 2.0 for video, Nano Banana 2 for images, ElevenLabs voices and Suno music, all inside Magnific. It is a Magnific Original, made and released by the tool company itself, like [Candela](/films/candela/) and [Roots](/films/roots/).
