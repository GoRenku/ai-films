## Why this film matters

A tired old killer takes out a businessman from an empty construction site. It is a routine job, until a neon bar, a glass marked with lipstick and an envelope left "by the lady". In his cheap hotel room by the railway tracks he finds his own photograph circled in red, a note telling him to run, and a funeral notice. He does not run.

Three days after [OTSUKAI](/films/otsukai/), Allan Ripley keeps the 1990s cel-animation look and turns it from comedy to melancholy. The release describes the aim in material terms: hand-inked characters, hand-painted gouache backgrounds, long silences and cold grey streets. It is a genre with strict conventions, and the film's interest is in whether generated images can hold its stillness rather than its action.

## How it was made

Ripley again lists his tools by role. GPT Image 2 built the character, prop and model sheets, the reference pages an animation studio uses to keep a design consistent from shot to shot; Seedream painted the backgrounds; Seedance 2.5 animated them. Suno produced the theme, an original ballad in the manner of Showa-era kayokyoku, Japan's postwar popular song. The film was made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest. Crew size, schedule and budget have not been disclosed.
