Shared November 3 as **Touch Glass**, the same **3:19.7** film is titled **AERO** in its official production notebook. It is the ninth Claudia music video and a sequel to **[BLISS](/films/bliss/)**, not a second entry for the X caption. **anabology** is credited with direction, **Claude in Claude Code** with production, and **Claudia** is the synthetic performer.

## Why this video matters

A recreated Windows Vista desktop becomes the stage: Messenger, permission prompts, gadgets and media windows carry both lyrics and action. Claudia's effort to leave the screen turns interface nostalgia into a character situation. The familiar request to allow an action also gives the film a musical pause, release and visual consequence.

## A disclosed division of labour

The notebook attributes song and look decisions, take selection, a revised ending and repeated notes to anabology. Claude is described as handling planning, prompting, production coordination and assembly. The maker's X clarification says the first half required substantial iteration. The detailed record dates work **October 27–November 3**, while the release caption says “48 hrs later”; neither establishes an audited labour total or a one-prompt production.

## Exact tools and practical techniques

The work-specific record names **Midjourney v8.2** for worlds and plates, **GPT Image 2.5** for character insertion and first frames, and **Seedance 2.5** on **Higgsfield's API and Replicate** for moving performances and other shots. Midjourney's video model supplies loops; **Suno** supplies the song, without an exact Suno version established here.

A JavaScript/canvas desktop, with WebGL elements, is rendered in headless Chromium. **Blender Cycles** renders the curved-screen/coastal passages; **TripoSG** supplies meshes; **ffmpeg** assembles the result. Vocal-stem guidance and phrase-level alignment address lip sync. Matching still frames at rest makes transitions between the flat desktop and its 3D version coherent. **39 Vista/Messenger interface sounds** are reported as retuned into the song's key across **60 cues**.

## Counts and cost scope

The maker reports **95 Seedance takes, 43 used**, and **163 GPT Image calls**. Listed API generation totals are **$337.23**: $327.76 for Seedance and $9.47 for GPT Image, including three estimated image costs. Midjourney and Suno per-job charges, Claude tokens, render infrastructure and human labour are not included. This is a scoped list-price account, not the film's total budget. Agent roles are listed, but launch count and total human crew are not established.

The complete X player, sampled scenes, official film page and production record were checked. No full critical review is claimed.
