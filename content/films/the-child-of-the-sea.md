## Why this film matters

The Child of the Sea attempts something an independent stop-motion team could rarely afford: a road movie through 17th-century Spain, from Cádiz to Madrid, across villages, processions, forests and rivers, all in a handcrafted, stop-motion-inspired look. The release says it is inspired by the legend of the Fish-Man of Liérganes and describes it as a dark fable about otherness, belonging and letting someone you love find where they can be free.

Reply gave it a **special mention** at its 2026 festival, citing its experimental use of AI. The filmmaking question it raises is whether a consistent handmade texture and a single sympathetic character can hold across thirteen minutes and many locations.

## Production evidence

The bilingual release lists the core crew and the MotionField production credit. Reply names Magnific as part of the workflow; other tools, budget, schedule and team size are not disclosed.
