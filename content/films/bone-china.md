**Episode 1 — The Other Half** was uploaded on **October 2**, before its October 15 share. The complete player runs about **6:58**. The series begins in an alternate October 1917, after two centuries of war.

At night, Angels test soldiers’ bones with silver hammers and carry selected people to the Spire, a porcelain city occupied by an apparently deathless aristocracy. Trench runner **Klara Brandt** has received notice that her brother Emil has ascended. Unlike the others, she wants to be taken.

## Why it matters

The fantasy rule creates the motive and the danger at once. A soldier’s longing reverses an ordinary survival story: the thing everyone fears may be her route to someone she loves. Porcelain is therefore more than an attractive surface treatment; it links bodies, privilege and the cost of escape.

This extends Vołkaŭ’s recurring interest in institutions that classify people and regulate access to another world.
