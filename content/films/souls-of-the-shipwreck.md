## Why this film matters

Souls of the Shipwreck tells grief and recovery as a fable: a woman sings colour back into the souls of a wreck, and they mend the ship. Its makers say they chose metaphor and mood over realism because consistency and fine gesture were hard to control, which makes the film a clear example of a style chosen around the tools' limits.

It was the overall winner of ITU's **AI for Good Film Festival 2025**, announced in Geneva on July 9, 2025.

## Production evidence

The remote, 48-hour process is the makers' own account in ITU's interview. The tools used are not named. The journal has not watched the complete film.
