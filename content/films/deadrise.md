## Why this work matters

A ship’s crew brings a woman named Olivia aboard and gradually loses control of the space it knows best. In the lamp-lit passages below deck, faces and doorways give the viewer only a partial sense of what is there. The familiar sailor archetype, signaled by the Popeye fan-film billing, makes a sharp foothold for a story that turns a rescue into a source of dread.

The October 18 upload gives the film’s title as *DEADRISE* in its description and uses *Popeye Horror Short Film Using Seedance 2.5* as the YouTube title.
