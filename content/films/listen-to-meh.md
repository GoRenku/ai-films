## Why this film matters

Listen to Meh grows a short film out of one absurd image: a sheep standing on the roof of a rural house. The maker's description frames it as a dialogue-free visual comedy about obsession and control, in which a gaucho fights a problem before he understands it, and which shifts toward something more emotional once the reason behind the sheep's behaviour is revealed. Telling that turn without words puts the weight on staging, framing and continuity between one man, one animal and one house.

It won **first place** at the 2026 Higgsfield Global Film Festival. Purga Studio, a Buenos Aires studio that otherwise makes commercials, presented it as its first fiction short.
