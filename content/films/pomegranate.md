## Why this film matters

Abundance becomes the dramatic problem: if a character can experience almost anything, what could still matter? That gives the elaborate imagined worlds an emotional purpose and places the emphasis on weariness, desire and the rhythm of conversation.

It belongs alongside [The Patchwright](/films/the-patchwright/) and the announced feature [Gods Don’t Give Gifts](/films/gods-dont-give-gifts/). Production details from those other works are not automatically transferred to this one.

## Voice and production

London says he directed four voice actors in person over three or four studio days. Contemporary reporting on the original credits names **Sam Dale, Ben Kersley, Oscar Merry and Ayça Özkan** as the voice cast. **Alexandru Mihai** led animation, with **Benjamín Muñoz Alonso, Alberto Alepuz Fernandez and Nowell Englund**. **Răzvan Ilinca** edited and supervised post-production, **Ștefania Grigorescu** graded the film, and **Marian Bălan** handled sound design and re-recording mixing. **Anne-Sophie Versnaeyen** and **Juan Torán** composed the score. [Premiere interview](https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/) · [Original-credit reporting](https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gossip-goblin-pomegranate/)
