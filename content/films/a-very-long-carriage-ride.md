Jackson released the story in two aesthetic treatments: a stop-motion look and a classic animation look. Both retain the same story and performances.

## Why this film matters

This is a particularly useful comparison for filmmakers: does material read differently when its surface changes? Staging, rhythm and voice can be assessed across two versions instead of mistaking a new look for a new dramatic idea.

The official page links both streaming editions and extensive making-of recordings.
