## Why this film matters

The film gives a century of screen history a single, traceable object. A dollar bill passes between hands, eras and genres, so the continuity comes from the prop rather than from a recurring human character. That is a specific answer to a common problem in AI filmmaking: how to hold a short together when every shot can look like a different film.

At the World AI Film Festival in Cannes on April 22, 2026, the organiser named *A Dollar Story* Best A.I. Action Film. Qiu Sheng came to it from conventional features: his debut *Suburban Birds* screened at Locarno, according to the University of Hong Kong programme.
