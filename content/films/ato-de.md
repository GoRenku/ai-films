## Why this short matters

The film stays with one young man's first-person view through tea, work, travel and a call from his mother. Repetition gives an ordinary postponement emotional weight: the same small word can be harmless in one exchange and costly across a life. The restricted viewpoint makes attention itself the subject rather than relying on spectacle.
