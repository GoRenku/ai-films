The film examines alienation through shifting female figures and draws inspiration from Georges Perec’s **A Man Asleep** and its 1974 film treatment.

## Why this film matters

Generated images and a synthetic narrator offer a formal way to explore mediated experience. The interesting question is what the editing makes us feel or understand about disconnection, rather than whether individual images appear photographic.

Milliyet Sanat reports selection for Cottbus’s November 3–8 feature competition and a planned Başka Sinema release in 20 Turkish cities in spring 2027. The planned general release remains an attributed announcement. A full public stream is unverified. Exact generation models and total budget are undisclosed.

## Ankara Film Festival

The 37th Ankara Film Festival programmed the film in Taptaze / Fresh at Büyülü Fener Kızılay, Salon 3. Its ticketing partner lists **November 13, 2026**. The official programme credits Spongeworthy Studio for production and world sales and Başka Sinema for distribution. It describes the film as assembled from a pool of more than 50,000 generated shots using customised models and narrated with Bardot’s own voice clone. It gives no exact model names. ## Paris opening programme

Signes de Nuit lists the French premiere of **A Woman Asleep** in its opening programme on **November 9 at 20:00 local time**, at the Maison des étudiants arméniens, Cité universitaire, Paris. Its programme lists 79 minutes, while Ankara lists 80. [Paris festival programme](https://www.signesdenuit.com/paris/2026/open.htm)

The Cottbus ticket listings scheduled the film for **November 4 at 18:15** and **November 7 at 13:30** at Weltspiegel. These sources establish the programme dates.
