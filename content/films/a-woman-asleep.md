The film examines alienation through shifting female figures and draws inspiration from Georges Perec’s **A Man Asleep** and its 1974 film treatment.

## Why this film matters

Generated images and a synthetic narrator offer a formal way to explore mediated experience. The interesting question is what the editing makes us feel or understand about disconnection, rather than whether individual images appear photographic.

Milliyet Sanat reports selection for Cottbus’s November 3–8 feature competition and a planned Başka Sinema release in 20 Turkish cities in spring 2027. These remain attributed announcements; tickets and a full public stream are not verified. Exact generation models and total budget are undisclosed.
