## Why this film matters

Mira is an actress who lives inside the worlds she was created to perform in. One moment she is in a sunlit Western town beside an orc warrior who belongs to a different genre altogether; the joke of the poster is that neither of them knows what film they are in. The release describes a science-fiction comedy about creativity, free will and the line between fiction and reality, and about the value of imperfection.

It is an apt premise for a generated film. Characters made from prompts are, literally, written into whatever scene is asked of them. Out of Character gives one of them a point of view on that, which turns a technical condition of the medium into the story's engine.

## How it was made

Avaren wrote and directed the seven-minute short, developed from an idea with [Alex Marco](/artists/alex-marco/), co-director of the horror short [María, María](/films/maria-maria-alex-marco-tomas-bucetto/). It is produced by The Blue Horizon, the studio that also released María, María. The film was made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest, entirely on Higgsfield's platform; the models, crew size and schedule have not been disclosed.
