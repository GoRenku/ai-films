A 61-minute feature released in July 2023, assembled from more than 4,000 generated videos according to Jackson.

## Why this film matters

The airplane setting turns limited space into a dramatic constraint: the protagonist cannot escape either his public scandal or an old antagonist. It is a useful early reference for sustaining dialogue, identity and scene progression with much less capable generation tools. Its historical value does not depend on accepting a disputed ‘first film’ claim.
