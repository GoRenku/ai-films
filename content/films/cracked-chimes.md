The original film site sets the story in **early 17th-century Leiden** and identifies H. P. Lovecraft's **St. Toad's** as its literary source. A troubled English Separatist accepts an occult printing job and follows an obsession toward a corrupted church. The release describes minimal dialogue and a visual approach drawing on Bosch and Dürer.

## Why this film matters

An expressionist horror story is a promising use of generated imagery because distorted space can serve the character's fear. The important question is whether visual transformation follows dramatic intention rather than becoming a succession of unrelated surprises. This is a reason for inclusion and further viewing, not a claim that we have watched the whole film.

Its named sound and writing collaborators also provide a useful counterweight to simplified accounts of AI authorship. The film has a production team and an original musical contribution; generated pictures do not establish that every part of the work was synthesized.
