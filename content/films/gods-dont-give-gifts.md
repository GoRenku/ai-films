**Announced theatrical opening: October 30, 2026, in select U.S. cinemas.** The official screening section still says cities will be announced soon. It offers email alerts; specific venues and bookable screenings have not been verified as of September 17.

The film inhabits London's Second Cycle of Humanity: a world where bodies can be replaced and memories altered, while familiar desires for love, belonging and stimulation persist.

## Why this film matters

London has built a recurring fictional world through Gossip Goblin. Taking that world into cinemas creates a different artistic test: can the stories hold together across a feature, with pacing and sound designed for a shared theatrical experience? It also tests whether a following built online can become an audience for ticketed exhibition.

Those are reasons to follow the release closely, not a verdict on a film we have not reviewed. The journal will distinguish the announced opening from verified screenings and later audience or critical response.
