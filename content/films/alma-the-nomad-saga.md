The November 3 original Magnific release runs **10:43**. **Alma**, a young worker from orchards outside Almaty, serves at the launch of a global teleportation network. An imperfect apple she keeps connects the technological spectacle to home and sets up the story's change of era.

## Why this film matters

The short uses a small, recognisable object to connect personal attachment with a large speculative world. Its official premise introduces **Kazakh Dinosaur Era**, where nomadic life and dinosaurs coexist. Almaty and the orchard are specific story anchors, giving the world more identity than a generic montage of futuristic cities and creatures.

## Named authorship and platform relationship

Both original sources identify **Riz Esentaev** as writer/director, **Vlad Volochay** as creative director, **Artur Gubin** as cinematographer, **Sergio Shurupa and Sid Rivers** as editors, **Olya Danyukova** as producer and **Max Balter** as executive producer. The film is from **Magnific Creative Hub**, powered by Magnific; the platform-backed production relationship is explicit.

There is a concrete credit discrepancy. The **full YouTube description** names **Riz Esentaev and Victoria Yedigenova** as AI artists, **Sid Rivers** for post-production and **Ivan Yurchenko** as sound designer. The **studio's X credit thread** instead names **Riz Esentaev and Victor Korchik** as AI artists and combines sound/post under **Sid Rivers**. These are two published accounts, not an independently reconciled crew list; neither is silently substituted for the other.
