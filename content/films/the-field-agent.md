## Why this film matters

The Field Agent builds a character comedy around a figure who is usually set dressing: a scarecrow. Its logline makes him the drive-in's biggest movie fan, finally given a mission of his own, and the official poster plays him straight, in close-up, rather than as a gag. Giving a stitched burlap face enough expression to carry a six-minute story is the performance problem the film sets itself.

It took **third place** at the 2026 Higgsfield Global Film Festival. Like [Pelagos](/films/pelagos/), it builds a fantastic premise around a single, clearly drawn protagonist.
