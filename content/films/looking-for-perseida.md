In the festival’s synopsis, humanity is extinct and evolved dolphins rule the ruins. A lone robot crosses this world to retrieve a capsule containing Perseida’s consciousness. His mission is driven by love rather than survival.

## Why this film matters

The genre world gives an intimate desire an expansive stage. Robots, deserts and unfamiliar rulers matter because they stand between a character and the person he wants to recover. That relationship between scale and motive makes the film worth curating as a narrative rather than a visual demonstration.

AAIFF confirms third place in the 2026 thematic competition. The festival supplies an excerpt and the director confirms the short and audience response in his own post-festival account. Neither is presented here as a verified complete public stream. The journal has not reviewed the whole film.

## An ending-first production approach

Desumbila tells Qazinform he completed the film in ten days, working alone at home. He first imagined a rainy ending, then built the story backwards from it. This is a useful disclosed writing technique: choose the emotional destination and develop the events that make it meaningful.
