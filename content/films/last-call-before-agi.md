## Why this film matters

*Last Call Before A.G.I* imagines the end of human primacy not as catastrophe but as a party. Cross-cutting between a near-empty dive bar and a gala in a golden opera house, it uses performance and a found-footage texture to stage resignation and celebration side by side. The filmmaking problem is carrying an argument about obsolescence through two songs and parallel rooms rather than exposition.

AI Film Festival Japan gave it the **Outstanding AI Experimental Award**, runner-up in its 2025 experimental category, announced on November 2, 2025. That result establishes exhibition and recognition, not a critical verdict.

## Production evidence

The full film is public on the KNGMKR channel, which links Matt Zien's profile; the festival credits Zien as director. The camcorder claim in the release description belongs to the fiction.
