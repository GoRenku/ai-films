## Why this film matters

Le Drip sets itself a hard continuity problem: keeping a thick, hand-painted oil texture alive across every shot of a complete narrative, rather than using painterly style for a few frames. Reply describes it as depicting a fish's last day on Earth through living oil paintings; the makers call it a painterly existential animated French short. The comedy and the existential note both depend on that texture holding together while the story moves.

The film took **second place** at the 2026 Reply AI Film Festival in Venice, won OMNI's **Golden Storytellers Award (Short Form)** and is on Austin's 2026 honourable-mention list. The original concept came from screenwriter **Jessie Hughes**; **Alex Naghavi** and **Ezra Li** directed it after meeting at the inaugural Google Flow Sessions residency, according to the release.
