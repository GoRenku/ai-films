## Why this music video matters

A question of romantic comparison becomes a deliberately artificial domestic world. The original release names both ClaudiaGI and Gérard; sampled playback moves from a spectacular shell-like entrance to an ordinary apartment occupied by a male character. Familiar rooms and recurring identities give the song a coherent stage for jealousy and self-mockery.

The maker’s replies make authorship concrete. He describes choosing the version partly for the way its lines press against the rhythm, and identifies the character’s sarcasm and self-deprecation as intended qualities.
