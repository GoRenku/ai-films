## Why this short matters

A woman’s insistence that her dreams belong to her gives an abstract ownership dispute a physical confrontation. The sampled exchange places her opposite an older man with mechanical hands, using faces, reactions and dialogue to make power visible. The vertical framing keeps that encounter close.

This continues Gossip Goblin’s interest in economies that claim intimate parts of a person. The reason for inclusion is that dramatic situation and its sustained exchange, rather than a new character render or an assumed connection to the studio’s feature.
