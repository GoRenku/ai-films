## Why this film matters

BOBO, Fengshui Girl and Xixili set out to rescue their foster mother, Auntie Fen, and run into a data-immortality scheme run by the city's elite. Junie Lau sets it on an invented street in southern China and plays it in Cantonese, mixing kung fu, absurd comedy and street mythology. That language and that setting are still rare in AI-made fiction, and the film is a reminder that the tools can carry a local film tradition as well as a generic one.

Lau says the film comes from growing up in Foshan and loving Hong Kong action films, and frames it around women as the heroes. At 27 minutes it closes Wonder Studios' Beyond the Loop Season 2.

## How it was made

In the behind-the-scenes film, Lau talks about authorship as much as technology. She worked with a human screenwriter, planned scenes in OpenArt, used CapCut Video Studio's canvas and invideo's agent, and argues that vision matters more than budget. The series' executive producers, CapCut, invideo, OpenArt and ElevenLabs, supplied the tools. The video models are not named.
