## Why this music video matters

The miniature scenes of Claudia’s storybook recur as full-size sets. When the song breaks into a silhouette sequence, the change of technique still feels like turning another page. The book gives the production a consistent visual language across the table, the stage and the animated passage.

The choice of recording changed the cut. The first version used a Suno take selected without listening. Anabology picked the companion take by ear, and the team regenerated the performances timed to Claudia’s vocal. The production account preserves both versions, making it possible to trace how a song decision affected the pictures. The official player serves the finished 4:03 version.
