## Why this film matters

The Fracture is a twelve-minute period drama that has to hold one court, one marriage and one mystery steady while moving between eras. The maker's festival upload asks what happens if the person you love comes back from war with memories that are not his. Reply's synopsis follows Queen Anna in 1515 as she searches for a rupture in reality that reaches across centuries.

It won **Best Direction** in the Astana AI Film Festival's 2026 Open Competition. The painterly key art and the credit list point to a small, defined team. Writing and direction are credited to Reale, alongside named AI creative directors and a separate sound house.
