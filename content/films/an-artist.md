The original release runs about **eight minutes ten seconds**. Its synopsis follows a traditional artist seeking work and shelter after creative labour has been automated. An administrative system offers him a one-way journey to Mars.

## Why this film matters

The premise turns a broad debate about automation into a personal dramatic problem: what happens to someone's identity when their skills no longer secure a place in society? Making that story with generative tools creates a productive contradiction for the viewer to consider.

This is a complete authored short with identifiable writing and directing credits, rather than a style demonstration alone. **BIAIFF awarded it third place in its 2026 edition.** The award establishes festival recognition; inclusion here is based on the documented premise, authorship and accessible release, not a claim of a completed critical viewing.

## What the maker has disclosed

The release post names **Runway** and **Dreamina AI** and describes trying an animation style with “SD2.” It does not define that shorthand precisely enough to assign a model version or a detailed pipeline. Budget, full crew, voice credits and finishing tools remain unverified. The earlier portfolio's Unreal Engine/Gen-1 workflow belongs to **The Dinner** and should not be transferred to this film.
