## Why this film matters

Nathaniel Chan’s earlier shorts, such as [Midnight Snack](/films/midnight-snack-nathaniel-chan/), run a minute or two and turn on a single withheld reaction. **The Fixer** is close to nine minutes and keeps the same interest in what people avoid saying, stretched across a whole character. Its premise is a man who repairs everything for everyone on his block so that he never has to stop and face the one thing he cannot repair.

Chan opens the release with a first-person note about piling on extra work so as never to sit still with a hurt. That frames the film as a portrait of avoidance rather than a plot about repairs. The busywork is the symptom; the drama is in what it keeps out of view.

## How it was made

The release places The Fixer in **PLAYGROUND Season 1**, an anthology series produced by **Storybook Studios**, and says it was made on **Cuelist**. Chan thanks The Butcher’s Brain for believing in the film and quotes advice from Jordan Daniel Chesney, who also appears among the executive producers of an earlier Chan showcase film: show the work you want to make.

The models, voices, cast and crew behind the short have not been disclosed, and the release does not name the anthology’s other stories.
