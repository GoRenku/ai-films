## Why this series matters

The project places a Victorian expedition inside a deliberately old-fashioned screen fantasy. Its maker cites 1960s Technicolor adventure as the visual frame. Sampled Part Two footage moves through a forest pursuit, giving the elaborate creatures and settings an action to sustain rather than displaying them as disconnected tests.
