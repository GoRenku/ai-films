## Why this series matters

The project places a Victorian expedition inside a deliberately old-fashioned screen fantasy. Its maker cites 1960s Technicolor adventure as the visual frame. Sampled Part Two footage moves through a forest pursuit, giving the elaborate creatures and settings an action to sustain rather than displaying them as disconnected tests.

The complete **Part Two**, released October 30, runs **3:29** in the checked X player. It provides a released instalment for the series guide. The earlier **1:26 Part One opening** was described by the maker as a teaser and remains contextual project news on the artist page; it is not relabelled as a full episode here. The guide preserves the maker’s Part Two numbering without inventing a missing longer Part One.

Original release, current playback, named-credit source and project site were checked. A possible feature remains a plan; this is not a completed feature announcement or a full-series critical review.

## November 7 — Part Three released

The [original Part Three upload](https://x.com/ChrisGwinnLA/status/2119112594036605131) runs about **4:46** and credits **Grindhouse Glitch and Roninfilm** as co-producers. Gwinn names Dreamina and tags its creator programme. Exact models, crew size and financial terms for this part are undisclosed. The previous part’s model credits apply to that earlier release. The series guide preserves the original numbering and opens with Part Two, the earliest complete instalment verified here.

## November 15 — Part One in the guide

[Roninfilm now lists a Part One player](https://roninfilm.com/treasureofthehimalayas) on its official project page and describes the production as a live-action performance and generative AI hybrid. In [Gwinn’s November 15 release](https://x.com/ChrisGwinnLA/status/2121834058267156701), he identifies an approximately eight-and-a-half-minute Part One edit and names Peter Hyoguchi as co-producer. He credits Midjourney and Seedance 2.5 through Dreamina. The earlier 1:26 opening remains a teaser. The guide links Roninfilm’s Part One listing directly and keeps the extended cut’s reported runtime separate from the studio embed, whose playback and runtime were not checked.
