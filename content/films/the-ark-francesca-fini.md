## Why this film matters

Vera lives in a house sealed against a world that no longer exists, keeping its climates alive room by room: rain in the kitchen, snow in the bedroom. One morning the radio counts down, and she opens a red door onto an endless white sheet of paper where creatures made of ink are waiting.

Francesca Fini's [SIMUL/ACTION #1](/films/simul-action-1/) extended filmed live performance with generated motion. The Ark is a fully generated piece, but it keeps her interest in a single body in a staged space; here the house is an archive of weather and the outside is a blank page. The release artwork, a hooded figure facing scribbled figures on white, shows how far the film pushes toward drawing rather than photographic realism.

## How it was made

Fini says the film was made entirely in Higgsfield Cinema Studio with Seedance 2.5, for the Higgsfield Talent Contest. Other credits, crew and budget have not been disclosed. The YouTube upload does not allow embedding, so it opens on YouTube.
