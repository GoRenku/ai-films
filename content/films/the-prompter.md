## Why this film matters

Its premise makes control a dramatic problem. If feelings can be manufactured for someone else’s use, the important question becomes whose experience the audience is asked to care about: the researcher’s, or the people within his constructed worlds.

That gives the visual invention a narrative job. Changes of environment need to communicate shifts in power and perception, while recurring characters must remain emotionally legible. Those are useful criteria for approaching the film, rather than treating the number of generated shots as an artistic achievement in itself.
