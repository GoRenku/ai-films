Danioros released **Do Not Wake Her** on X on **August 17, 2026** and described it as the most ambitious short the maker had made so far. The original video runs about two minutes. Its release poster shows a city street emptying as a colossal face with coloured tendrils rises between the buildings.

## Why this work matters

The film marks a step in length for a maker who, by their own account, normally keeps videos under 90 seconds. It was a first attempt at a longer story, made in four days with Seedance 2.5. The later serial [Storm-Born](/films/storm-born/) took that further by planning cameras in a 3D previs before generating, and in November Danioros announced a sequel to this film, with **Part 2** as a placeholder title and no release date.

The release post does not describe the plot, and the maker has not published other tools, credits or a non-X upload.
