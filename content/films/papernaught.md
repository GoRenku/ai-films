## Why this film matters

The material idea and the story support each other: a fragile world gives physical form to a parent’s fear of loss. That is the specific reason to explore its visual language.

OMNI lists it as the long-form animation winner. The Short Film Show scheduled it in a 15-minute broadcast slot on September 19; the complete film itself runs about 11 minutes on the maker’s channel.

## November 2 — full film available

Glenn Triggs published the complete film on May 6, 2026, on his Dark Epic Films YouTube channel, which his own site links. The upload runs 11:00, so the 15 minutes noted above reflect The Short Film Show’s broadcast slot rather than the film itself. In the upload description, Triggs says he made the images with Nano Banana Pro and 2, and most of the animation with Veo 3 and Seedance 2.0. He also reports a cost of close to $400 and about a month of work.
