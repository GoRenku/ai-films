## Why this film matters

JAILBIRD has to make an animal's perspective carry a social argument: a chicken taken into a human prison, and what such a project can give both the bird and the people who look after it. The challenge is keeping a non-speaking point of view readable while the human context stays clear around it.

Andrew Salter describes the film as a translation of his own illustrated storyboards into generated shots, which makes it a useful case of drawn pre-production steering an AI workflow. Runway's 2025 AI Festival awarded it **Gold**, ranked below the Grand Prix.
