## Why this film matters

On his 80th birthday, a grandfather sits at the head of a laid table, and each time someone in his family looks at a phone, he loses some of his colour. The release image shows the whole problem from above: a red cardigan at one end, a ring of bowed heads around the cake.

The idea is made for a silent film. Without dialogue, the only measure of what is happening is the colour draining from one figure, so the story is told by the image itself. Ibraheem Diab's [The Beginning](/films/the-beginning/) won WAIFF's 2026 Best AI Emotion Film; Grey turns from a journey of refugees to a single room and a quieter loss.

## How it was made

Diab describes the three-and-a-half-minute film as wordless, in a clay stop-motion style, and released it with an English and Arabic title. The tools have not been disclosed.
