In the backstory of [The Last Nursery of Elderwall](/films/the-last-nursery-of-elderwall/), a showdown two hundred years ago between two sisters turned every dragon in Elderwall to stone. This short tells Veyara's side. It starts with Milos, the small dragon she carries inside her coat. When he begins to fade she borrows a little magic from another dragon, then steals it, and her hunt eventually leads to the last nursery, where her sister Brenna is waiting between her and the eggs.

## Why this work matters

A prequel can explain how a world got its rules, but this one asks for sympathy first. Veyara's theft begins as care for a single creature, which gives the catastrophe behind the Halloween feature a personal cause instead of a villain's plan. LedertsWorld Studios builds Elderwall across a feature, a proof-of-concept short and character pieces; this release connects them with a story about one choice. The sisters' confrontation is set to the studio's original song, Not Our Home.

The release description also calls the story *One More Dragon*. The studio has not disclosed the tools, crew or cost.
