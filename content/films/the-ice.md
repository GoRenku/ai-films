## Why this film matters

The Ice sets a quiet, restrained animated drama inside a disaster premise: time has stopped, people have turned to ice, and a father whose hand is already frozen travels toward the home he shared with his daughter. The challenge for AI animation here is consistency, holding one character's design and a cold, coherent palette across a journey, rather than spectacle. The story resolves in sacrifice for the next generation.

The Chroma Awards gallery lists it as the **first-place winner in Film - Animation & Anime** in Season 1. It was also placed **third in the narrative category** of the 2025 Korea AI Content Awards.
