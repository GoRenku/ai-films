## Why this film matters

Midnight Drop reconstructs Operation Midnight Hammer, the June 2025 US strike on Iran's nuclear facilities, through two lives that never meet: a bomber crew on a classified flight and a woman in Tehran beginning her morning. Cross-cutting between those viewpoints is a classical way of turning a news event into drama, and the film tests whether AI-rendered environments can carry it alongside recorded human performances. Because it dramatises recent events, it should be read as a docudrama, not as documentary footage.

The Chroma Awards gallery lists it as the **first-place winner in Film - Drama & Romance** in Season 1. It is the third film in OneDay's series *The Decisive Moment*.
