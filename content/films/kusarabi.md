## Why this work matters


MINIMA uses a banquet as a small society where responsibility and food are distributed unevenly. The mix of Japanese, invented words and archaic sounds leaves some meaning to rhythm and imagery. The artist describes using repetition, texture and interruption to make the feast feel familiar and unsettling at the same time.
