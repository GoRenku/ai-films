## Why this series matters

Its interesting boundary lies between generated behavior and the choices made by a human editor. A simulation becomes a story only when events are selected, ordered and given consequences for an audience.

AiMation Studios released four episodes of 33 to 38 minutes on its YouTube channel between December 8 and 29, 2025, a week after each reached its own iOS streaming app. The studio pitches the show as six NPCs in a battle arena forming alliances unscripted; how much of each episode is simulated and how much shaped in the edit is not documented. Paton announced the finale publicly at the end of December.
