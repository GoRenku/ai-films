## Why this film matters

Humans: Playtime treats ordinary human leisure as material for an outsider's catalogue, and its own one-line description, ending '90% accuracy', signals that the catalogue is meant to be unreliable. The formal question is whether a sequence of comic observations can hold together as a film through tone and framing rather than plot. Its official artwork, with a giant lobster attacking a lawn on the Moon, shows the scale of absurdity it works with.

The Chroma Awards gallery lists it as the **first-place winner in Film - Experimental & Open Format** in Season 1.
