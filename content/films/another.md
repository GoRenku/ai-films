## Why this film matters

A recurring body and a recognizable space give visual transformation a baseline. The filmmaking problem is deciding when to preserve that baseline and when to break it.

BIFAN screened the film in its AI programme. Curious Refuge gave it its online premiere on March 11, 2024; the release credits Clark as writer and director and list a small live-action crew, three actors and three AI and diffusion artists working with VFX supervisor Robert Nederhorst.
