**La Triste Figura**, the first short in **Contanimation TV**, adapts Don Quixote’s encounter with the windmills. The full upload runs approximately **11 minutes 35 seconds**, including credits.

## Why this film matters

The filmmakers’ premise is a strong example of using visual form to express an unreliable point of view. Rather than observing Quixote’s delusion only from the outside, the story gives his imagined battle the scale of an epic. Sancho’s perspective supplies the comic counterweight.

That is a concrete reason for the film’s spectacle: it belongs to a character’s interpretation of reality. The extensive named credits also make this a useful production reference for directors combining generated animation with specialist contributions.
