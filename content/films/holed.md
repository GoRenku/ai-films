## Why this series matters

HOLED begins with a huge, smooth-edged hole and an absence of evidence for whatever made it. The disappearance extends into memory: people cannot name what they have lost. Episode 2 develops the emotional consequence—grief can remain after the memory of a person has gone—and puts investigation against a Council's preference for comforting explanations.

This is a sustained mystery with linked episodes and explicit visual rules. Reserving a colour for one kind of evidence gives art direction a narrative job: a small departure from the rule can become a clue. That is a more useful filmmaking question than whether an isolated generated shot looks convincing.
