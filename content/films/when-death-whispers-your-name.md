## Why this short matters

A sceptical psychiatrist has to interpret a frightened young woman’s account in a secluded villa. The premise makes belief an active dramatic problem: if her story is dismissed, the unseen threat may be allowed closer. The Italian setting and illustrated release artwork establish a specific gothic direction rather than an interchangeable monster test.

The complete **7:06** October 31 release is presented as Grindhouse Glitch’s third annual horror special. Its making-of account connects exploratory character imagery to a subsequently written and staged story. The maker’s short schedule remains an estimate, not an audited labour total.

Original complete X release, advancing player, sampled opening and the full production reply were checked. No full critical review is claimed; Peter Hyoguchi’s credits on a different joint project are not transferred to this short.
