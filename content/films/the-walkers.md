## Why this film matters

The Walkers builds humour from a family’s ordinary routines inside an extraordinary undead household. That repeatable situation gives its characters something to do beyond presenting horror make-up or transformations. SHAIKE’s creator page explains the wider sitcom concept.

## Festival cut

OPUS names **The Walkers**, credited to **Dj Marcelll**, as Best AI Short Film. Its individual programme entry does not supply a runtime. The creator has published other Walkers cuts and episodes; their runtimes and playback are not assigned to the winning short without a confirmed match. The credited image represents the wider Walkers project, not a verified frame of the awarded cut.
