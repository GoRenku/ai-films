Mogenet released the complete approximately three-minute short on October 13. The premise is a company offsite in Costa Rica that takes team-building too far. Its original post identifies it as an entry in the Curious Refuge × Epidemic Sound horror contest; that is participation, not a selection or award.

## Why this short matters

Corporate retreat language promises safety and belonging, making it a useful foundation for horror about coercion. The specific setting gives the menace a social context rather than relying on a creature reveal alone. Mogenet’s account of refining a continuous camera pull-back also provides a concrete directing decision: tension can depend on how a shot reveals its surroundings, not simply what is generated inside it.

## Production and viewing

The maker directly identifies **Seedance 2.5 on Dreamina**. His separate shot reply says he worked to make the camera pull-back continuous. A complete crew, performer and audio-credit list, budget and production duration were not established by the original thread or linked portfolio. The competition branding is not evidence of a particular music licence or a production sponsorship.

Playback, the complete release, sampled imagery and the creator’s sources were checked. This is an introduction, not a complete critical review.
