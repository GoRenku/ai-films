## Why this music video matters

Kelly Boesch's videos are usually surreal fashion and invented bodies moving to her songs. This one tells a story. Sammy knows the names of flowers, Bella builds things in her head, and the video follows the two children as they find in each other someone who sees nothing wrong with the way they think.

Boesch says the change was deliberate. When she came to make the video she decided to try to film the song's story itself, and access to Seedance made a real storyline possible for her. The release still is a good sign of what that asks of a generator: two children's faces held in a quiet, naturally lit moment, close enough that their attention to the butterfly carries the shot.

## A personal song

Her note explains the song's origin. As a child with a large imagination and dyslexia, she spent years doubting her intelligence before realising her mind simply worked differently, and she wrote the song wondering how many children grow up hiding the things that make them special. She says more story-led videos, some of them quirkier, are coming in the next month.
