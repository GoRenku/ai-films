## Why this film matters

An ensemble crossing between worlds gives the filmmakers a continuity problem with dramatic consequences: relationships and choices must remain legible as the setting changes. The completed feature is a useful case for examining what a very small team can attempt, without treating production speed as a measure of artistic success.

OVIS calls this the start of a larger fictional universe. Future chapters remain an intention, not verified releases. The reported schedule and budget describe this project and should not be generalized into a reliable cost formula for AI features.
