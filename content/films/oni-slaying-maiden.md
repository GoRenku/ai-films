## Public release verified — November 19, 2026

**LostJoker Films’ original September 2 release** identifies this cut as **Oni-Slaying Maiden IV: The Gunner**. Shio Kuroki, the fourth Maiden and a cool-headed gunner carrying a transforming exorcism rifle, is sent into the ruins after Ren fails to return from a mission. The description specifies Japanese dialogue, English closed captions and a 2K release. The public player runs about **5:33**, compared with the programme’s **5:32**. Ce Jiang / Lost Joker is the identifiable creator; individual voice actors, exact models and budget remain undisclosed. The numeral IV belongs to the release title and does not establish that four complete episodes are publicly available.
