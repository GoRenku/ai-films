## Why this film matters

The film takes a real artist’s life as its subject and treats the drawings themselves as the turning point. Its synopsis moves from the political pressure of Brazil’s dictatorship, through the improvised stages of 1970s theatre, to private work made almost in a trance. That gives an AI-made biography a concrete visual task: showing how a body of images is formed, rather than simply illustrating a timeline.

At the World AI Film Festival in Cannes on April 22, 2026, the film received the CapCut Prize. Sky Screen AI, which republished it in September, calls it the grand-prize winner of CapCut × WAIFF.
