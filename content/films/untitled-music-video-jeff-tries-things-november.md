## Why this work matters


The maker describes a complete original-song video built from 44 shots, with one recurring character and princess cameos. Keeping one protagonist identifiable across that many cuts lets the audience follow her as the settings and other characters change. An early shot frames her at a counter as an animated star trails across the image.

The X post that first carried it gave no title; the maker's November 11 YouTube release names it **Is That Too Much to Ask?**, an original song credited to Sadie, the same performer persona as his earlier [Lately](/films/jeff-untitled-music-video/).
