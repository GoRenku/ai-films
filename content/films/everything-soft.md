## Why this film matters

Everything Soft won the Grand Prix, the top award of the second DISRUPT AI Film Festival in Melbourne, chosen from 487 submissions and 45 shortlisted films. In the festival's report, juror Tessa Conrad of TBWA\Worldwide picks it out as a hopeful film in a field drawn to dystopia, with gorgeous cinematography and art direction, blending a sense of reality with humanity.

The festival published the complete 4:20 film on its YouTube channel on the night of the awards. It has not published a synopsis, credits or a production account, and the tools Tasker used are not disclosed. DISRUPT is open only to filmmakers based in Australia.
