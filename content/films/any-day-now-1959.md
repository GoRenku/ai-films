## Why this video matters

Retrofuturist styling changes how an existing automation song reads. A recurring air-travel world, metallic workers and period presentation connect a contemporary fear to an older image of technological progress. The directing task is to sustain that contrast through a song, rather than rely on one amusing robot image.
