## Why this film matters

An operative climbs eleven floors of a hospital full of the infected to reach a vaccine, while his partner, already bitten, waits below. The whole 17-minute film is staged as one continuous take. That is a real filmmaking constraint: there is no cut to hide a weak action beat or a break in continuity, so the fight choreography, the camera path and the geography of the building all have to hold.

## How it was made

Korean studio [Junky Cream](/artists/junky-cream/) built it the way it built [Kill The Rabbit](/films/kill-the-rabbit/): performers and fight choreographers first, then motion capture, virtual production and real-time 3D, with generation layered on top. The behind-the-scenes film argues that this lets a small team finish in months what animation would usually need years and large crews for. It is the fourth film in Wonder Studios' Beyond the Loop Season 2, whose executive producers CapCut, invideo, OpenArt and ElevenLabs supplied the tools. The specific models are not disclosed.
