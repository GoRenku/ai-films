## Why this short matters

The workshop is a place of accumulated use rather than a pristine fantasy showroom. Shelves of worn toys establish the repairer's world before his gestures do; an elderly visitor and a small giraffe then turn the attention to hands, handling and patient care. The sampled exchange makes repair an emotional relationship, not just an attractive character design.

This is a useful extension of Boesch's practice beyond music-led fashion and choreography. The recurring mender, a recognisable room and a shared task give the two-minute short a sustained situation. Its gentleness depends on maintaining that intimate scale and on making objects appear to matter to the people who hold them.

The maker's November 2 release, current complete player, several sampled scenes, public replies and original artwork were checked. This is an introduction rather than a full critical review.
