## Why this film matters

Dense world-building can become empty exposition. This short is a useful study in selecting a few emotional anchors—memory, family and conflict—to carry an expansive imagined history. Pay attention to how music helps distinguish a shift of time from a shift of mood.

AIF’s 2025 screening room credits Hachi and IO, gives a runtime of 5:38 and records a Merit award. RŌHKI's own upload of the same 5:39 cut presents it as the first half of the second episode of its anime, entered into festivals while the rest was in progress. The release credits RŌHKI for animation and music, Paul Jenkins and RŌHKI for the writing, and Ko Mori as the voice of Mitra.
