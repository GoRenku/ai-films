## Why this film matters

He was painted in nine minutes with the cheapest paint in the shop, and nobody has ever stopped to look at him. One night he walks through the greatest paintings in the museum to fetch a single drop of blue for his mother; by morning the museum is on fire. The release poster adds a line about the masterpieces: it took lives to make them.

The premise sets a throwaway image against the canon, which is a pointed question for a film made from generated pictures. It continues the concern of Abhick Banerjee's [SITARA](/films/sitara/), whose worker wants dignity and recognition, by giving the overlooked figure a journey through the very works that made him look small.

The film is credited to Abhick Banerjee and produced by Rollsound Studios, which released the seven-minute short as a Higgsfield Talent Contest 2026 entry. The release does not list the models, tools or crew.
