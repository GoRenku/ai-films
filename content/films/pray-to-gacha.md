## Why this film matters

The collector ALO gives this fantasy a specific emotional problem: finding connection through objects. Unplug’s original announcement identifies the project as a collaboration with ICO. The premise makes the imagined world serve a character’s attachment, rather than existing only as a visual showcase. This is an assessment of the published premise, not a full-film review.

## Festival and availability

OPUS names **ICO** and **PRAY TO GACHA** as its 2026 Grand Prix winner and lists the completed film at 22:48. The project’s earlier YouTube announcements have not been confirmed as the complete awarded cut; playback remains unverified.
