## Why this film matters

*The Beginning* is a journey story built on kindness rather than danger alone. The synopsis follows Adamu across 3,500 miles to reach a school and turns on the people who help him on the way. The thumbnail’s boy in a cracked mirror points to the film’s real test: whether AI-made faces can hold close, quiet emotion across a long route.

At the World AI Film Festival in Cannes on April 22, 2026, it won Best AI Emotion Film. The organiser’s upload also tells how the director nearly missed the ceremony over a visa.
