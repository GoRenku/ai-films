## Why this film matters

*Another Detail* uses a retro-futuristic setting to puncture a portrait of success. The synopsis turns on a gap between what a self-assured entrepreneur believes and what is true, and the release artwork, a boxy robot holding a gun to its own head, signals that the reveal is the point. Telling a twist in under six minutes depends on withholding and timing, not on world-building.

Larzillière is a trained director and editor who makes a short AI film each month for Prompt Club. *Another Detail* won Best AI First Film, presented by ClapAction, at the World AI Film Festival in Cannes on April 22, 2026.
