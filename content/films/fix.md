## Why this film matters

FIX gives a generated animation a consistent material rule: everything in its world is made of paper, and nobody in the film remarks on it. Inside that rule it tells a small comedy of control. According to the studio's Higgsfield outline, Seoyun, the singer of a four-piece school band called Honey Killer, pins down everything that could go wrong at their last festival show, until the pins themselves cause the disaster and the show goes on in the rain without them. A single visual device, the red pushpin, carries both the gag and the theme.

The same film won Lumara's **Mouse Award** for young audiences and an **honourable mention** at the Higgsfield Global Film Festival. The two organisers credit it differently — three directors at Lumara, the studio account at Higgsfield — but the title, paper-craft design and Honey Killer story are the same in both.
