## Why this music video matters

Bai yun describes the song as written in a period of feeling lost, drifting from reality and trapped inside her own mind. Sijin Liu's video answers that with a small, consistent symbolic vocabulary rather than a plot: according to Stash, a white ant for erosion from within, hands that tighten into shackles, lily of the valley, and a ladybird the singer associates with hope. The credits combine live shooting with generated imagery, so the problem is keeping a real performer's likeness and a surreal language coherent across the song.

OMNI International AI Film Festival named it the **Golden Sound: Music Video** winner at OMNI 1.5 Hyperphantasia in Sydney on July 3, 2026.

## Production evidence

The release names the full video and music team and the Dreamina tools; Stash adds the storyboard-led workflow, the manual fixes and the three-week schedule. The size of the budget and the split between filmed and generated shots are not disclosed.
