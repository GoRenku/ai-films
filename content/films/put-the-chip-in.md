Javier Fan's November 22 release turns a news cycle about brain-chip telepathy into a slopcore pop number. The 3:42 video, titled with the subtitle **(TELEPATHY)**, imagines "hands free, eyes white" scrolling as a mass upgrade staged with chrome helmets, headsets and raised white gloves.

## Why this music video matters

The [javiexmachina](/artists/javiexmachina/) project treats each song as part of a recurring world, as in [I'M YOUR SLOP](/films/im-your-slop/). Here the creator pitches the concept as Neuralink meeting Total Recall in an atompunk style, which gives a topical joke a sustained visual idea rather than a one-off gag.

The announcement credits Suno and slopcannontv without explaining their roles. Other tools, versions and collaborators have not been disclosed.
