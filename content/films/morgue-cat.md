Absurd released **Episode 1** on October 1. Its X player runs about **4:24**; playback was confirmed October 2. The production account concerns the wider project and is not a verified inventory of this episode’s shots.

Jonesy lives with a pathologist on a space station. A mouse brought as a gift becomes a threat. See the earlier [production note](/journal/2026-09-30-morgue-cat-production/) for more context.

## Why this release matters

A cat’s behavior offers a concrete directing challenge: emotion must remain readable through posture, eyes and movement. Establishing ordinary play before horror also gives the threat an emotional baseline.

The production disclosures are particularly useful for collaborative filmmaking. Shared references need to survive changes of angle, location and contributor; adjoining shots still require deliberate continuity checks.
