## Why this film matters

A retirement-home escape gives two elderly protagonists the initiative usually reserved for young road-movie outlaws. Their bodies and their rebellious intentions pull in different directions, creating a concrete source of comedy. The CLOWD screening-room release plays the complete 2:45 film.

## Credits and production

**Tamara Llorens and [Miguel Picazo](/artists/miguel-picazo/)** co-created the film, according to Llorens’s production post. She identifies **Seedance 2 in Dreamina**, editing in **CapCut**, and support through the **Dreamina AI partner programme**. The post does not divide every production role between the two creators.

OPUS’s Best AI Animation result credits **Tamara Llorens**. The catalogue preserves that award wording and the separately sourced collaboration credit.
