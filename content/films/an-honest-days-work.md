## Why this film matters

The setting and the dialogue work against one another: sumptuous, old-fashioned privilege houses a dispute about a technologically altered economy. This is a compact example of speculative world-building serving a social joke, rather than asking the audience to admire machinery alone. The characters’ conversational reactions matter as much as the room’s ornament.
