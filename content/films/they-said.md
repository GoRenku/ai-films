## Why this film matters

Jerry guards a bank and lives quietly with a white cat called Mr. Snow. Then two visitors from very far away turn up at his house: they need a man on the inside of the Fifty-One Federal bank. The heist plan is sound; the partners are the problem. The release promises a Plan B when everything goes wrong, and invites a second viewing to catch the jokes planted early.

Jun Pabon's [Dominio](/films/dominio/) was a straight-faced speculative action film about rules and consequences. They Said keeps the genre machinery, a crew, a plan, a reversal, and plays it as comedy, which puts the weight on timing and reaction shots rather than spectacle.

## How it was made

Pabon credits himself as writer and director under Rizoma Film. The release says every shot, voice and character was generated and directed shot by shot, without naming models. It was made for the Higgsfield Talent Contest.
