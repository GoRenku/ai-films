## Why this film matters

A film about an artist’s obsession can use reconstruction to enter a subjective world that archival evidence alone cannot show. The important distinction is between a documented life and the images invented to interpret it.

The official site identifies generated imagery and an AI-composed score, and offers a trailer. OMNI records a 79-minute documentary and its long-form award. A public full-film stream has not been verified.
