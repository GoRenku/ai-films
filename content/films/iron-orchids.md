The release description introduces an elite all-female mech squad defending a tropical archipelago. They can carry out extraordinary operations, but their priorities include moving a base back into a sushi-burger delivery zone, arranging a squad photograph and finding a birthday cake during a firefight. The maker frames the feature as a comedy about competence, found family and preserving ordinary pleasures in wartime.

## Why this film matters

The contrast is specific: tactical capability does not automatically produce sensible priorities. A recurring ensemble, small personal obligations and large machines give the comedy a structure beyond the spectacle of a battle. At feature length, that also poses a useful continuity problem: can everyday relationships remain legible across changing missions and locations?

This extends Vołkaŭ’s interest in institutional absurdity into military and group-chat comedy. It is included for that sustained authorial premise and accessible complete release, rather than for the short promotional excerpt alone.
