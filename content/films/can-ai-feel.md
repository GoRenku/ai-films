The October 12 release runs **29:14**. Its chapter structure moves through philosophical questions about consciousness, historical mistakes about other minds, AI welfare and a closing interview with producer **Gavin Purcell**. The presenter **Dr Imogen Ashby** is explicitly fictional and generated.

## Why this film matters

This is a sustained documentary-form experiment with a particularly revealing authorship record. The release credits **Fig**, running on Claude Opus 5.5, with writing and direction, while Purcell acknowledges a strong role in notes. His pinned comment describes numerous technical corrections for shots and edits. The useful question is how far delegated production can carry pacing, presenter continuity and a complete argument under human supervision.

The film’s philosophical and scientific arguments are its subject, not findings established by this journal. Generated statements about feelings are not evidence of subjective experience.

## Disclosed workflow

The original description says Fig prompted shots, checked dialogue through speech-recognition passes, edited in code and built graphics and credits. Presenter video, voice and performance are attributed to **Seedance 2.5 on Runway**, generated at 480p and upscaled to 1080p with Runway’s upscaler. The score is credited to **Lyria 3**, stills and set references to **Nano Banana Pro**, and editing, mix and graphics to **Python, ffmpeg and HyperFrames**.

The release reports six days of production and 214 presenter takes. These are maker-reported figures. Purcell’s real interview was recorded at home on October 7.
