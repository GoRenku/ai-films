## Why this film matters

John-O'-Lantern flips Halloween customs: pumpkins carve and cook humans, then frighten their children with stories of people who decorate homes with pumpkin heads. Curious Refuge describes cheerful family dialogue played against a gruesome premise. The joke depends on sustaining a domestic tone, so performance and writing matter more than effects.

John-O'-Lantern won the **Audience Favorite** award in Curious Refuge's 2026 AI Horror Film Competition. That award is decided by public vote, not by the jury.
