## Why this film matters

Trick or Trend takes a social-media subject, beauty trends that change overnight, and gives it the logic of a folk bargain: a face borrowed for one night that must be returned by midnight. Curious Refuge's write-up says a final twist sharpens its commentary on body image. It is a compact example of horror used for satire rather than spectacle.

Trick or Trend won **third place** in Curious Refuge's 2026 AI Horror Film Competition.
