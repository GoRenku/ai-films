Clark says the short draws on an event from his father’s life in the 1960s.

## Why this film matters

The speculative premise is anchored in a particular family history. That changes what the imagery has to do: convey vulnerability, circumstance and the connection between generations. It is a useful early example to compare with Clark’s later, larger productions.
