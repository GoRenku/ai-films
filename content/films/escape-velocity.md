A techwear runway becomes an argument about technological status and exclusion. The countdown promises an escape from an underclass, then changes the premise of the show itself.

## Why this music video matters

Fashion presentation supplies an organised visual grammar: looks, tags, signs and repeated walks. The song can redirect that grammar as its argument develops. It also establishes Claudia’s recurring visual identity, giving later films a character to transform rather than a new face for every track.
