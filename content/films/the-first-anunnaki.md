**Episode 2** runs **21 minutes 43 seconds**. It follows Ea’s voyage from Nibiru, through the asteroid belt, to Earth as his civilisation seeks resources for survival.

## Why this episode matters

The voyage connects changing locations to a sustained objective and a political conflict. That gives the series a useful directing challenge: maintaining character motivation and a coherent world while moving through large changes in scale and setting.

The creator explicitly frames this mythology-inspired work as science fiction. The journal does not present its ancient-astronaut premise as archaeology or history. The runtime shown here is for this episode, not the entire series.
