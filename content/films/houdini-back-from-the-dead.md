## Why it matters

The imagined present-day interview turns historical reconstruction into a performance problem: whose words are being spoken, and what separates a record from its interpretation? That distinction deserves attention alongside the synthetic appearance.

FOX says scripts were human-written, supported by archival research and expert review. Its account describes licensed archive footage and more than 400 historical photographs, with actors supplying movement, cadence and performance before AI transformed their appearance. Those are production claims, not this journal’s audit of historical accuracy. It would be misleading to call the series actor-free.
