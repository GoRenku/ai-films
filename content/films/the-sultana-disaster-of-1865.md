## Why this film matters

The Sultana steamboat exploded on the Mississippi in April 1865, overloaded with Union soldiers just released from Andersonville and Cahaba, and left almost no film record. The documentary uses generated reconstruction where no footage could exist. Its job is to carry a chronology: the bribe that overloaded the boat, the boiler repair, the explosion near Memphis and the news cycle, dominated by Lincoln's assassination, that buried the story. That makes it a test of whether synthetic dramatisation can serve a sourced historical argument rather than replace evidence.

It won the Documentary category at the 2026 Austin AI Film Festival, whose organisers list it at 23:15, the same length as the public upload.
