The first seven Nexus episodes explore a shared world through separate adventures. PJ Ace identifies **Prison Break — Part I** as **Episode 8, Part I**, where Anika from Episode 1 and Jace from Episode 4 begin a connected story aboard an imperial ship.

## Release order and titles

PJ Ace's releases establish the viewing order below. The first episode, **Wet Markets of Kafar**, also served as the feature teaser. **Squid Heist**, previously catalogued here as **Sky Line Heist**, is Episode 4. The Episode 5 X release uses **Maki Death Games** and its YouTube counterpart uses **Monkey Death Games**. PJ Ace announced **The Last Whales of Caldera** as Episode 6 on October 21, although its YouTube title repeats the Episode 5 label. The official **Dino Demolition Squad** upload is numbered 7.

The related **Airships of Egypt** competition trailer sits outside the numbered sequence. Parts II and III of Episode 8 were announced with Part I and will have their own release links when available.

## Why it matters

An anthology has to make its world recognisable across different directors, creatures and dramatic premises. Episode 8 puts that shared world to a new use by bringing characters from separate stories into the same action. The individual episode pages retain their own crew credits, release links and production notes.
