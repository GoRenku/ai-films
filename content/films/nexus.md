Nexus is a shared world whose stories each centre on a creature under threat. Each entry is a complete story with its own credits and production notebook, so they are collected here as an anthology rather than a single continuous plot.

PJ Ace identifies **Maki Death Games** as **Nexus Episode 5**. **Sky Line Heist** is presented as a Nexus story without an episode number in the checked release, so no number is assigned to it here. Earlier numbered episodes are not yet catalogued; their absence is not a claim that they were never released.

## Why it matters

An anthology tests a different kind of continuity from a serial: the world, its rules and its recurring concern for vulnerable creatures must stay recognisable while each story finds its own premise and director. Credits stay with each story, as published by the team.
