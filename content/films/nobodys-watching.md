## Why this video matters

The song asks who people are when they are alone. Boesch answers with conspicuously artificial figures whose bodies, clothing and surroundings are difficult to take for granted. A suited figure sitting in water with his head enclosed in a box makes concealment visible without reducing the song to a literal illustration of each line.
