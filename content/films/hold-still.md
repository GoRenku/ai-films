## Why it matters

[Cutting Room Floor](/films/cutting-room-floor/) was a song about the work hidden behind a generated video. **Hold Still** turns from argument to memory. It follows two teenagers across one night in July 1986: golden hour, a tired suburb, an empty mall where they find a VHS-C camcorder, and the road out of town before first light. The release's chapter list tracks that journey against the song's sections.

The concrete choice is texture. Adam Eivy describes grading the video to feel like a shoebox of found flash photos cut together with grainy 16mm film, and the found camcorder adds a third format inside the story itself. The look stands for how the night is remembered, not only for how it might have looked.

## How it was made

The release puts a number on the work: **82 scenes and more than 1,200 takes**, directed, edited and graded by Eivy, with music by atomantic. Shortly before posting it, antic wrote that the video had taken more hours and iterations than people would believe, with a dozen complaints still outstanding. The models behind it have not been named.
