## Why this work matters


Ines Moar follows the footprints of a creature that leaves herring at her window. The gift matters because the island’s merchant controls access to food. The story makes the supernatural visitor part of a conflict over who can feed whom, and who is blamed when supplies and livestock are threatened.

Thousand Words FilmS released the film September 30 and marks the upload as made with AI. Exact models, individual crew and cost are undisclosed.
