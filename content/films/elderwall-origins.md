## Why this film matters

A dragon sanctuary gives the Keepers a place to defend and a reason for their choices to matter. TJ Lederer describes building an emotional line through shot selection, pacing and sound. The short establishes characters and a setting that can carry another story, including the separate [Halloween feature](/films/the-last-nursery-of-elderwall/).

The studio describes this as a completed, independently funded proof of concept. Its campaign describes the short as 24 minutes and its Diffuse page rounds it to 25. The studio directs viewers to its [Cinematics page](https://thelastnursery.com/cinematics) with a free Keeper account. The public Diffuse player sampled here contains a short preview.

## Production

Lederer credits himself as writer, director and editor. The project lists Runway, Seedance, ElevenLabs, Google Lyria and ChatGPT and describes AI-assisted imagery, animation, voices and music. Exact versions, shot-level assignments, complete supporting credits and cost are undisclosed.
