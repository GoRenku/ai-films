## Why this music video matters

Earlier NoiraDEVIL releases such as [Upgraded 2](/films/upgraded-2/) put Noira and her companions into a conflict. Midnight Heist gives the same world a caper structure cut to the song: a rooftop approach, a laser grid, sirens, a rope jump and a getaway car, with the theft of a star as the prize. The chaptered release maps the plan onto the song's sections, including a verse given over to the heist itself and a "meet the crew" break, so the story and the music advance together.
