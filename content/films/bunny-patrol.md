## Why this film matters

The anthology gives different situations a common concern: technology that exposes people when they try to connect. In **Namaste**, scouts encounter a predator learning their movements and voices. **Lighting** makes livestream attention dangerous; **Ahoy** turns dating-app pings into a signal; **Choice** puts a joking deserter opposite a courteous enemy mech.

Communication is part of the dramatic threat rather than decoration on a futuristic setting. The deadpan comic framing connects the horror to Vołkaŭ's wider interest in absurd systems. The four-part release is selected as authored narrative work, without claiming a full critical viewing.
