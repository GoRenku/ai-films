## Why this film matters

Its most useful disclosure is practical: continuity requires references for changes within a character, not just one portrait of that character. Clothing, scene context and repeated descriptive decisions have to remain coordinated across a longer edit.

The released film gives readers a way to assess that method against the result. A complete model inventory and budget have not been verified, and tools from Clark’s earlier films are not assumed to apply here.
