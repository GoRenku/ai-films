## Why this work matters


AIST turns its own line of work into a workplace comedy. Kate arrives for an interview and is handed a music video due by 6 p.m. while the server is down. The deadline gives the episode a clear dramatic problem. The mockumentary style also asks the generated performances and handheld camera to feel as if they are reacting to one another in the room.
