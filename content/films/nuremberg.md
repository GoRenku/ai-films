## Why this work matters

TUPACABRA’s trailer opens with a historical reference point: a broadsheet by Hans Glaser describing strange forms over Nuremberg on April 14, 1561. It carries the idea of a battle in the sky across centuries, juxtaposing armored witnesses with wartime laboratories and fleets emerging from ice. The shifts in period give the same mystery different stakes as the technology around it changes.

AI Film History reports eight million views on X within a day of the trailer’s release. Caeraa says a feature is in production and gives late 2027 and roughly two hours and fifteen minutes as targets. The public video is the trailer. TUPACABRA is credited here for the trailer, with the feature’s directing and crew credits still unconfirmed.
