## Why this series matters

**ATLAS** sets its pilots and machines inside a continuing dramatic world. The reason for inclusion is the sustained work of returning to a cast, its relationships and the consequences of battles over successive episodes. Mechanical spectacle has to coexist with dialogue, recovery and difficult choices; a convincing single robot shot cannot carry that structure by itself.

The original channel provides **Parts 1–5 and a separate Final**, released June 9–July 11. All six complete originals are publicly playable. The finale keeps its published title rather than acquiring an invented Part 6 number. New viewers start with Part 1 above.

An October 12 [Complete Season 1 edition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50Sifa30q3k) runs approximately **1:49:43**. It is linked as an alternative edition, not counted as a seventh episode or assumed to have exactly the same edit as the individual releases. The sequel has its own [ATLAS — NEXT](/films/atlas-next/) entry.

## Authorship and production

The releasing creator attributes story, dialogue, character design, scene direction, music direction and final editing to their own work. The descriptions name **Seedance, Leonardo AI and OpenArt**, with an original ending song made using **Suno AI under a Pro Plan**. Those are maker disclosures; they do not identify every shot’s generator, exact versions, voice performers or the total team.

Original expanded descriptions, all six complete players, durations and release dates were checked, along with release artwork and sampled imagery. This is a sourced series introduction, not a claim of full-season critical viewing or an independent rights audit.
