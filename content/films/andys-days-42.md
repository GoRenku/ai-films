## Why this film matters

*Andy's Days #42* makes comedy from routine. The organiser's synopsis sets it in 2040, when ordinary people can work in space after a little training; Andy, a man in his forties, crash-lands on an unexpected planet with a ship that can keep him alive but cannot call home, and starts recording what happens. The 42nd entry in that log is the film. The filmmaking problem is finding humour in a hopeless situation through a self-recorded, diary-like form.

AI Film Festival Japan named it winner of the **Best AI Comedy Award** at its 2025 edition, announced on November 2, 2025. That result establishes exhibition and recognition, not a critical verdict.

## Production evidence

The complete film is public on the channel of Dentsu Creative Pictures, where Matsumoto is a director; his profile there lists the film and its award.
