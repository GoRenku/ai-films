## Why this film matters

A waste plant is an unusually productive setting for a speculative series: every delivery can bring a new story while the workplace anchors the audience in a recurring world. Li frames the project around the question of who decides what counts as waste. That gives the imagery a social and narrative purpose.

The July 10 original upload runs just under twelve minutes. Li calls it the first original series; this entry covers the released installment and does not imply that an entire season is finished.

## How it was made

Li discloses a combination of **Seedance, Kling AI and TapNow**, with TzuJung Lin editing and Zhetao Huang providing VFX support. He does not specify model versions or allocate every shot to a particular tool. The useful lesson is the division of creative labor: generation, editing and finishing remain distinct contributions, even within a small team.

The credited names are a partial public record, not proof of an exact three-person crew.
