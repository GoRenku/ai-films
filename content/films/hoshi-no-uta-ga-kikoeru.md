## Why this film matters

As a boy in Aso, Kumamoto, Rei loses his parents, and his grandfather takes him up a hill said to grant wishes, where the night sky shows in a tin bucket of water. He grows up, moves to Tokyo and builds a company, and the more he succeeds the emptier he becomes. After a friend's death, a betrayal and a rift with his family, he goes back to the hill.

That is a full feature arc, and the film runs nearly two hours. Long, emotionally continuous animated features made with generation tools are still rare; most AI anime runs a few minutes. Its makers say the scenery draws on the Aso landscape and on the look of Makoto Shinkai's films, and that the story draws on the director's own life.

## How it was made

The director, 結野弘崇, an entrepreneur in his first directing job, is credited with story, script, music and design. Japanese trade reports put the team at three people and the working time at about three months, with roughly 1,500 cuts and more than 10,000 generations. Seedance and MiniMax generated the video. The leading roles are voiced by human actors, credited in the release, with some AI voices alongside them. These figures come from the makers' own announcement, repeated in the press; they have not been independently checked. DELINE PRIME released the film free on YouTube on October 22, 2026.
