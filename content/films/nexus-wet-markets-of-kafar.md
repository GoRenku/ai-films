## Why this episode matters

The creature market introduces the world through what its inhabitants buy and sell. The release is both the feature announcement teaser and the first numbered Nexus story. Anika returns in Episode 8.

PJ Ace describes a roughly five-minute teaser made by three people in two weeks. The description thanks Dan Weller, Karim Youssef and Stephen Quach without assigning individual roles there.
