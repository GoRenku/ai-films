## Why this film matters

A man who can no longer leave his apartment waits every afternoon for a delivery robot that keeps malfunctioning at his door, and comes to believe it has picked him out. KITA is a 26-minute character drama built on that loneliness, set in a near-future city and told in the textures of stop-motion: worn surfaces, small machines and handmade-looking rooms.

It is the musician Jidenna's first film as a director. Wonder Studios says he made it because stop-motion stories centred on Black characters rarely get greenlit, and that he designed the world, wrote the characters and scored it himself. In the making-of, he ties the old man to memories of his father.

## How it was made

KITA is the fifth film in Wonder Studios' Beyond the Loop Season 2 anthology, whose executive producers CapCut, invideo, OpenArt and ElevenLabs supplied the tools. The release stresses that Jidenna had no coding background and built the stop-motion look across the whole film. Which models generated the images, motion and voices is not disclosed.
