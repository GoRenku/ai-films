## Why this film matters

Senior archivist **Pearl** works with memories stored as glowing phials. A difficult memory tests the institution’s separation of good experiences from painful ones. The setting turns an emotional question into a procedure that a character must reconsider.

## What the filmmaker disclosed

The production notebook above follows Kiessling’s own account. Restricting the setting let her spend attention on recurring props and emotional changes rather than continually introducing new locations.
