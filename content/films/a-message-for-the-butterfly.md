## Why this film matters

Hou and Wachholz use a speculative question to organise the film: what might a memory become if it reached someone after its owner was gone?

AI Film Festival Japan programmed the 8:08 short and awarded it **BEST CREATIVE** at its 2026 edition.

Particle Panic’s official YouTube release runs 8:08, matching the festival programme.
