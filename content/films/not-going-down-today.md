Released November 22 on NoiraDEVIL's official channel, **Not Going Down Today** runs 3:43. Its premise is a final boss that wears Noira's face: the same blade, the same eyes and the same rage, fought out over black water with lightning and fire.

## Why this music video matters

The song is a boss-fight taunt ("is that your final phase?"), and the video stages it literally as a duel between light and dark versions of the same character. That gives the track's repetition a dramatic shape, because each chorus is addressed to an opponent who is also the singer. It follows [Upgraded 2](/films/upgraded-2/), where Noira and her companions resisted a shutdown system, and narrows the project's world to one character fighting herself.

NoiraDEVIL credits itself for the song, visuals, edit and colour. The tools have not been disclosed, and no separate human singer is named.
