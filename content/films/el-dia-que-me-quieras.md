## Why this film matters

*El día que me quieras. El viaje de Gardel* uses AI animation for a biographical subject that would be costly to film conventionally: the life of Carlos Gardel, from the Buenos Aires slums to Paris, Hollywood and New York, and his death in 1935. The filmmaking problem is building a period world and a cast of historical figures consistently enough to carry a half-hour story, while keeping the performances human; the credits list a voice cast rather than a cloned Gardel.

AI Film Festival Japan gave it the **Special Jury Award** at its 2025 edition, announced on November 2, 2025. The University of Buenos Aires describes the same prize as a jury award for best animation; the organiser's category name is used here.

## Production evidence

The credits are unusually complete for an AI-assisted film: a university production with named producers, writer, AI realiser, sound and archive researchers, and a human voice cast.
