## Why this film matters

Website turns a familiar action—looking at a screen—into the engine of its speculative world. The premise links audience attention to events outside the screen. That creates a useful dramatic tension: curiosity can become participation, and watching need not be innocent. This reading comes from the filmmaker's published premise, rather than a claim to have reviewed every scene.

The film is a good entry point into Li's work because its central idea demands more than attractive individual shots. A viewer has to understand a rule, anticipate its consequences, and decide how to feel about them.
