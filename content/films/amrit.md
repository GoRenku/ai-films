## Why this film matters

AMRIT is built around a reversal stated in its tagline: an evil that hungers for fear is undone by people who laugh last. That premise asks a horror short to manage tone carefully, holding dread and defiance in the same scenes. The film's Hindi dialogue and Indian setting also widen the genre's frame beyond the English-language default of most AI horror competitions.

The Chroma Awards gallery lists AMRIT as the **first-place winner in Film - Horror & Thriller** in Season 1. Its makers say it was produced in seven days.

## Production evidence

The release and the submission name a small, specific team and an unusually full tool list. What each tool contributed, the exact model versions and the budget are not disclosed, and the seven-day schedule is the makers' account. This entry relies on the release description, its credits and the organiser's page; the journal has not watched the complete film.
