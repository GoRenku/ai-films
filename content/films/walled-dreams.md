## Why this film matters

*Walled Dreams* sets a small, intimate relationship against an oppressive city: a lonely girl, a plush pig that she believes understands her, and walls that reality keeps building higher. The filmmaking problem is giving a toy companion enough presence that its eventual departure matters, while keeping the dream world absurd rather than merely decorative.

AI Film Festival Japan named it winner of the **Best AI Animation Award** at its 2025 edition, announced on November 2, 2025. That result establishes exhibition and recognition, not a critical verdict.

## Production evidence

The festival credits **Bee Han and Yixuan Niu**. Two public uploads carry the same 4:46 cut; the earlier November 17 upload is used here. The Full Moon Studio channel presents itself as the team behind the award, but neither upload names the directors in its description. Exact models, tools, team size and budget are unknown. The journal has not watched the complete film; this introduction relies on the official synopsis, release descriptions and festival records.
