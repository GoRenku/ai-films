The complete **October 19** release runs **2:57**. Two people’s missing memories become material for competing reconstructions. Each new version can change who appears to be protecting the other and who appears to have betrayed them.

## Why it matters

Memory reconstruction is a dramatic device here, not a guarantee of truth. Changing a transition or replaying an encounter alters the characters’ motives as well as the image. The possibility that memory was deliberately rewritten makes the apparently explanatory system another source of uncertainty.
