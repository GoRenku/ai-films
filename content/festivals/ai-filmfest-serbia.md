## Why it matters

AI FilmFest Serbia is run by a university art faculty, the Faculty of Contemporary Arts (FSU) in Belgrade. It has a public call on FilmFreeway, a six-member jury of filmmakers, faculty and AI researchers, cash prizes paired with study scholarships, and a full published list of results down to quarter-finalists and a 209-film official selection. Its finalists came from Türkiye, Argentina, Hong Kong, Italy, France and the United Kingdom, a spread that reaches well beyond the usual centres of AI filmmaking.

## Editions

The 2026 edition closed for entries on April 30 and announced its results on June 30. Anton Dubrovin's [The First Day](/films/the-first-day-anton-dubrovin/), a five-minute film about his great-grandfather on the first day of the German invasion of the Soviet Union, took first place. César Augusto Cabrera Boggio's AI Control, which the jury praised for its documentary approach, came second; no third place was published. Seven finalists followed, among them Migration, Good Days, THE FINAL CHAPTER and HORIZONTAL – A Tale of the Vertical World, and the jury gave special mentions to Sulafa Hijazi's The Green Protocol – Diary and Mirko Ristanović Petković's Maybe She Sleeps. The awards were presented at FSU on July 3, followed by screenings through July 8.

The festival's own site calls this the first AI film festival in Serbia, though earlier FSU posts refer to a 2025 event; only the 2026 edition is recorded here.
