## Why it matters

Now in its fourth year, this is one of the longest-running genre contests for short AI films. Its rules make it a tight test of short-form craft: three minutes at most, AI in every shot and original stories only. A five-person jury chooses the top three; the audience vote decides only the Audience Favorite. Catalogue artists [Verena Puhm](/artists/verena-puhm/) and [Dave Clark](/artists/dave-clark/) sat on the 2026 jury.

## The 2026 edition

[Kagome](/films/kagome/) by Sasha Fevralskaya won first place, followed by [Nix](/films/nix/) and [Trick or Trend](/films/trick-or-trend/); [John-O'-Lantern](/films/john-o-lantern/) took the Audience Favorite. The organiser does not state when entries opened or how many films were entered. Epidemic Sound's music requirement is a contest rule, not a disclosure about any particular film. The page also lists the 2025 winners, led by Sam Lavy's *The Missing Segment*.
