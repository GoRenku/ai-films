## Why it matters

AIMA started with a public screening and jury in Mallorca and expanded to London with James Drake Media. Its London awards put authored animation, longer narrative films and music videos in the same programme, while retaining separate prizes for advertising.

## Editions

The first Mallorca edition took place April 24–25. James Drake Media says it presented a documentary award there. The complete official Mallorca prize list remains unverified.

In London, Robert Gaudette’s [A Face Only a Mother Could Love](/films/a-face-only-a-mother-could-love/) won Best Short. The Best Feature award went to Hal Watmough’s [The Trials](/films/the-trials-hal-watmough/), a 32-minute film from Wonder Studios’ <em>Beyond the Loop</em> anthology. The festival’s release credits Alex Marco on the Best Thriller award for [María, María](/films/maria-maria-alex-marco-tomas-bucetto/). Its London partner also credits co-director [Tomas Bucetto](/artists/tomas-bucetto/).

The official call for the March 2027 return to Magaluf opened November 11. FilmFreeway lists March 7, 2027 as the extended entry deadline.
