## Why it matters

Austin pairs a juried competition with craft sessions in which filmmakers explain how their films were made. Its rules require largely human-authored screenplays, and its eleven categories run from micro-shorts to features, series, music videos and hybrid live action. That breadth makes its results a curated cross-section of AI filmmaking in a given year.

## The 2026 edition

A selection committee chose a 40-film official selection, and a named panel of judges picked one winner per category, announced on October 17 at ZACH Theatre. MindPods from [Blurred Horizon](/films/blurred-horizon/) won Feature Film, [The Reformation Room](/films/the-reformation-room/) won Series and [PsychoPsypher](/films/psychopsypher/) won Music Video. Other category winners include [The Morisca](/films/the-morisca/) (Short Film), [Kev](/films/kev/) (Micro-Short), [Hazama](/films/hazama/) (Hybrid) and [The Sultana Disaster of 1865](/films/the-sultana-disaster-of-1865/) (Documentary). [The Yellow Knight](/films/the-yellow-knight/) and [Le Drip](/films/le-drip/) received honourable mentions. The avant-garde, brand and trailer winners are linked from their results but are not narrative films, so they are not catalogue entries; the student winner has no public release.

The festival abbreviates itself as AAIFF, as does the unrelated Astana AI Film Festival. 2026 was its second edition; cash prize amounts, entry fees and a 2027 call have not been published.
