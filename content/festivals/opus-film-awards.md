## Why it matters

OPUS brings several AI filmmaking forms into one screened, juried London programme. Its published results distinguish narrative work, animation, documentary and music videos from advertising and trailers. The festival now meets the listing standard through its public rules, named organiser, jury and completed first edition.

## First-edition results

[PRAY TO GACHA](/films/pray-to-gacha/) received the Grand Prix. Nine awarded works link to canonical film or music-video records, including the existing [Candy](/films/candy/) and [When You Run From Your Mom](/films/when-you-run-from-your-mom/). Candy remains a short film in this catalogue; **Best AI Feature Film** is the organiser’s award label.

[The Siesta Job](/films/the-siesta-job/) has a verified full 2:45 release in CLOWD’s screening room. Its co-creator [Miguel Picazo](/artists/miguel-picazo/) is credited alongside Tamara Llorens in the film record, while the award preserves OPUS’s Tamara Llorens credit. Six other newly catalogued works currently have festival-screening records with playback checks still open. Advertising winner Chicken Rider and the winning trailer remain award-only entries.

The Luminary Award is presented by **She Builds with AI**. **SHAIKE** is listed as a festival partner.
