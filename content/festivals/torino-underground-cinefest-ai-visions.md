## Why it matters

Torino Underground Cinefest is an independent festival in its thirteenth edition. Its AI competition shows an established festival giving AI-made work a competitive lane of its own, screened in a cinema alongside conventional features and documentaries rather than in a separate technology showcase.

## Editions

The AI award was new in 2026: [Cornered](/films/sinuca-de-bico-cornered/) by Odair Faléco and Davi Moori won Best AI Short, announced at the festival's close on October 4 and published the next day. The organiser's TUC13 history page carries an older awards block that contradicts these results; the official results article is used here. No jury is named for the AI section.

For 2027 the section becomes **AI Visions**, "a new competitive section dedicated to films created partially or entirely using Artificial Intelligence", with a Best AI Film award. The rules give September 2, 2026 as the call opening. FilmFreeway lists September 11 as the platform opening.

### November 5, 2026

The festival’s FilmFreeway listing gives November 9 for its next paid entry tier and August 9, 2027 for its final entry deadline. The written rules still say mid-August. FilmFreeway lists the festival for September 27 to October 3, 2027. The rules’ August 16 date is for delivering screening copies of selected films.

### November 12, 2026

The festival’s [FilmFreeway listing](https://filmfreeway.com/TUCFEST) now gives November 27 for Deadline B. The final deadline is still August 9, 2027.
