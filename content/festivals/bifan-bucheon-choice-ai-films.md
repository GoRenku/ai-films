## Why it matters

BIFAN started its AI competition in 2024. The festival places these films in a genre programme with public screenings and gives audiences their own vote. The 2026 programme expanded to include features. BIFAN reports 576 submissions and 15 selected films that year.

## Results across the first three editions

Léo Cannone’s [Where Do Grandmas Go When They Get Lost?](/films/where-do-grandmas-go/) won the first Best AI Film award. In 2025, Takeshi Kushida’s *Last Dream* took that prize. [More Tears Than Harm](/films/more-tears-than-harm/) won the technical award and [Total Pixel Space](/films/total-pixel-space/) received a jury mention. In 2026, [Cornered](/films/sinuca-de-bico-cornered/) won Best AI Film. *Big Ball*, *Let There Be Light* and *Ask Me Anything* won the three sponsor-named awards.
