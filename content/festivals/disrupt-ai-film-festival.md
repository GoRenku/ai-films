## Why it matters

DISRUPT is run by the advertising agency TBWA Australia and is open only to filmmakers based in Australia, with free entry and a jury of twelve from film, academia, government and advertising. Its judging criteria put original storytelling ahead of what the tools can do, and its terms allow conventional editing as long as the core creative work is generated. The agency publishes an annual report on the entries, which makes the festival a useful record of how one national scene is changing year to year.

## Editions

The second edition, on the theme Humans and Their Tools, drew 487 submissions, up from 371 the year before. Jamin Tasker's [Everything Soft](/films/everything-soft/) won the Grand Prix at the Melbourne gala on November 18, 2026. Genki Yamasaki's [Daylight Saving Time!](/films/daylight-saving-time/) won Best Long Film, Jonathan Heath's [QWERTY](/films/qwerty-jonathan-heath/) Best Short Film and Qian Deng's [Ding!](/films/ding-qian-deng/) Best Student Film. The festival published all four on its YouTube channel that night. In the report, jury chair Paul Reardon says the jury kept coming back to restraint: knowing what to leave out, especially in the longer films.

The 2025 first edition's four winners are published on the organiser's site, but its pages do not date that edition's gala, so it is not recorded here yet.
