## A science-fiction world in four chapters

[Earth Fall 2763](/films/earth-fall-2763/) starts with a catastrophic pact and returns to the resulting world through *Lethal World*, *The Bridge* and *CYBERTIA*. The guide starts with *Agreement* and keeps the four original chapter links in order.

## Four ways to tell a creature story

[The River Mother](/films/the-river-mother/) follows two generations through the Reedlands. [The Samruk Keeper](/films/the-samruk-keeper/) builds its conflict around the value of a bird’s feathers. [The Tall Beasts](/films/the-tall-beasts/) moves from a hidden valley to a migration route in its sequel, and [The Wulver’s Gift](/films/the-wulvers-gift/) makes a creature’s generosity a threat to a merchant’s power. The entries distinguish the studio’s world-building disclosures from film-specific tool credits.

## A nursery with a world around it

[The Last Nursery of Elderwall](/films/the-last-nursery-of-elderwall/) is the studio’s feature-length Halloween special. Its original release is distinct from the earlier *Origins* development short and the six proposed episodes described in the creator’s campaign.
