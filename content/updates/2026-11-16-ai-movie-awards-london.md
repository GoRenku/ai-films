## Three films already in the collection

[A Face Only a Mother Could Love](/films/a-face-only-a-mother-could-love/) took Best Short. Hal Watmough’s [The Trials](/films/the-trials-hal-watmough/) won Best Feature, the festival’s category for the 32-minute film. Vincenzo Lo Re’s [The Finding](/films/the-finding/) received Best Cultural Heritage.

## Six more full releases

Junho Her’s [The Gift](/films/the-gift-junho-her/) and Edmond Yang’s [When You Run From Your Mom](/films/when-you-run-from-your-mom/) use animation for two intimate stories. Her follows the bond between a woman and a dog. Yang narrates a childhood memory in a world of folded paper and hand-drawn faces.

Marcus Grip’s [Elara & The Ash King](/films/elara-and-the-ash-king/) takes its heroine through a forest full of small creatures and an outsized adversary. A school guard’s CCTV system supplies a second point of view in Leighanne Fernandes’s [The One Who Watches](/films/the-one-who-watches/). Alex Marco and Tomas Bucetto shared the same thriller award for [María, María](/films/maria-maria-alex-marco-tomas-bucetto/), which sets its horror during a night shift at a psychiatric residence. In Justinas Vosylius’s [They Came When We Left](/films/they-came-when-we-left/), an alien child learns about humans through the objects they abandoned.
