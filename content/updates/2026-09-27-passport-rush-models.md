**Passport Rush** now has more specific production credits for its tools. In a post quoting the original release, Higgsfield identifies **Seedance 2.5** for final video generation, **Claude Opus 5.5** for scene prompts and **Photoshop** for fixes. These sit alongside the previously documented Soul 2.0 designs, hand-drawn storyboards and Blender motion planning.

The distinction matters: planning action, preparing prompts, repairing images and generating footage are separate jobs. Naming their tools makes the studio’s process more useful to study without suggesting that a single model authored the whole film. The disclosure remains the studio’s account; individual crew credits and a shot-by-shot model inventory are still unresolved.
