## Good Boy, Part II

Two days ago Magnific released the trailer. Now the first episode of **[Good Boy Part II](/films/good-boy-part-ii/)** is out, and at 32 minutes it is closer to a television episode than a short. Dair Karakeev's original [GOOD BOY](/films/good-boy/) spent 51 minutes on a survivor and the dog she walked two days to save. The sequel gives the pair a concrete errand: Argos has four newborn pups, they need vaccines, and *You Don't Know Jane* takes Jane to an abandoned research station where she finds a pass to a sealed underground facility.

The production has changed shape. The first film was a self-funded project; this one is commissioned by Magnific's Creative Hub and published on the platform's channel, with a credited producer (Max Balter), sound engineer (Ivan Yurchenko) and cinematography adviser (Artur Gubin) alongside Karakeev as writer and director. The release does not name its models. Good Boy Part II now opens the homepage carousel.

## Nexus: Prison Break, Part II

**[Prison Break — Part II](/films/nexus-prison-break-part-two/)** continues the three-part eighth episode of [Nexus](/films/nexus/) that PJ Ace and director Beau Fipke began with [Part I](/films/nexus-prison-break-part-one/). Anika and Jace are now fighting their way out through an imperial hangar, against the Crimson Knight.

PJ Ace's release thread is mostly about the camera. Plates are designed first, in Seedream 5.5 and Nano Banana Pro, to fix an anamorphic look. Seedance then generates the coverage from structured prompts written with a Claude skill the team uses in production. Each prompt names the scene, the camera position, a duration of 15 to 30 seconds, the number of shots and the lens behaviour, such as 100mm compression, handheld shake and rack focus. The thread also lays out every reference asset behind a single 30-second hangar scene. The figure of $102,725 covers all three parts, which Dreamina funds. Part III is due next.

## Hazama, in full

Victor Moreno's **[Hazama](/films/hazama/)**, Best Hybrid at the Austin AI Film Festival, was listed here as a festival screening with a teaser. Moreno has now published a [making-of](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uI6gH1kVux0) that links the full 10:47 film on Vimeo, so it moves to Watch now.

The making-of is a useful map of where a hybrid film draws its lines. The frozen lake was shot for real in northern Sweden at −20°C. Generated images stand in for matte paintings in the wide views. For the bookstore and hospital, AI backgrounds were projected behind the actors on set. The airplane was built with Cuebric and Infinity Studio, and the Japanese settings, a tower in the ocean and a Kyoto temple, were generated.

## FRIENDLY FIRE

TechTalkNavigator, whose robot anime [EISENRITTER](/films/eisenritter-confronting-the-world/) screened in Japan, has entered the Higgsfield Talent Contest with **[FRIENDLY FIRE](/films/friendly-fire-techtalknavi/)**. In seven minutes a rookie pilot is shot down by his own captain, accuses him of betrayal and, ten years later, understands the shot. The creator separates the work clearly: images, video, voices and music are generated, while titles, subtitles and the edit were done by hand. The Higgsfield project page with the character and mech design sheets is public.

## Malfoid reaches Part 13

schmede's [Malfoid](/films/malfoid-i-wasnt-joking/) serial has kept going since our last update at Part 6. Parts 7 to 13, from *Books* to *Keep Her Busy*, have now been added to the episode guide. Most run about two minutes, so the story now advances in short steps every few days. The new descriptions do not name the models.

## Two music videos

NoiraDEVIL's **[Midnight Heist](/films/midnight-heist-noiradevil/)** gives Noira and her crew a caper cut to the song: rooftops, a laser grid, sirens and a getaway car, with the brightest star in the sky as the prize. The release credits Noira's vocals and links four other AI creators as the crew, without stating their roles.

Higginswerx's **[November Treat](/films/november-treat/)** is a straight-faced 80s hair-metal Thanksgiving ballad, with a Trans Am on a rooftop, a harvest-gold kitchen and a pair of pilgrim salt and pepper shakers that will not behave. The creator lists Seedance 2.5, Gemini Omni and Nano Banana, edited in DaVinci Resolve.
