## A veteran director's announcement, clarified

September reporting of **Paul Schrader's** plans described one project as all-AI and another with roughly 15 minutes of AI material. [The Playlist's September 4 account](https://theplaylist.net/paul-schrader-three-guns-at-dawn-all-ai-actors-20260904/) connects the first to the reconfigured **Three Guns at Dawn** and treats **The Doors of Perception** as a possible identity for the second, not an established identification.

The underlying THR and Deadline interviews could not be fetched in this check. This is attributed announcement coverage, with no completed-film claim, model inventory, release date or assertion that the 15-minute sequences have been produced.

The distinction matters artistically: generating performers throughout a noir and generating an altered-state passage inside a conventional film pose different continuity and performance problems. The announcements support following those approaches; they do not yet demonstrate either finished result.
