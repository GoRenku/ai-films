## Documentary craft: archive, illustration and revision

Alex Patrascu's [Bruce McLaren documentary](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2107428182370693460) is a distinct release from his earlier explainer. Original playback runs approximately **9 minutes 32 seconds**. The maker says he directed and gave notes while Claude Code coordinated research, production agents and an edit pipeline. It is an independent production, not endorsed by McLaren.

The [toolset](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2107428185495232868) names **Claude Opus 5.5**, **Nano Banana 2** stills, **Seedance 2.5** video, **Seed Audio 1.0**, **ElevenLabs v4** and **ElevenMusic v2.5**, alongside ffmpeg and code for graphics and checking. These are disclosed versions, not an independently audited production log.

The [archive distinction](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2107428194995650682) is essential: he reports **30 AI shots out of 120**, tagged as illustration, with real archive used for Bruce McLaren's face. Generated atmosphere is a different category of evidence from historical footage. The [narrator is designed and cloned](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2107428198350819729), not presented here as a newly generated performance by Bruce. When the voice repeatedly mispronounced Hulme, the spoken wording used Denny while the full name stayed on screen.

His [cutting disclosure](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2107428204268945520) addresses an ordinary finishing error: source excerpts running a few frames beyond an archive cut introduced unintended flashes. He describes a custom detector, a three-frame margin from cuts and a two-second minimum shot length. The practical lesson is to check source boundaries as well as the intended edit.

He also [reports correcting factual mistakes after review](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2107428219758780673). Automated sound, timing and image checks did not replace a person assessing meaning, pacing and facts. This documentary receives craft coverage rather than a narrative-film collection card; source rights, every historical assertion and the complete credit roll have not been independently audited. No full critical review is claimed.
