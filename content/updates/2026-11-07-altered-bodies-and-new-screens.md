## Machines in everyday roles

[Au cas où](/films/au-cas-ou/) puts ClaudiaGI’s French pop alongside creatures and machines performing recognisable human routines. The original 3:27 release has its own credited artwork.

## The Keepers before the feature

[The Last Nursery of Elderwall: Origins](/films/elderwall-origins/) adds the studio’s completed short. TJ Lederer credits himself with writing, direction and editing. The entry distinguishes the public preview from the full account-gated release and preserves the project’s disclosed tools.

## A festival date in Ankara

[A Woman Asleep](/films/a-woman-asleep/) joins the Ankara Film Festival’s Taptaze / Fresh programme. The listing names Büyülü Fener Kızılay as the venue and the festival’s ticketing partner lists November 13.
