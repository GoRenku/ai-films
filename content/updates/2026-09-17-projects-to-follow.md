## In development or on an upcoming slate

**Touch Grass — Dave Clark / Promise.** Clark’s studio essay describes a developing feature about synthetic online identities. Follow for its treatment of identity and for production disclosures; no completed feature release is claimed.

**Kira — Damien Steck and Julien Petit.** K5 lists Kira on its upcoming slate, under Steck’s name; the fuller co-creator credit comes from the reported project announcement. A completed pilot and episode availability still need verification. The project is worth following for sustained stylized world-building, not yet as a released series.

**Space Nation — Martin Weisz.** K5 lists the science-fiction series as upcoming. The unresolved question is delivery and viewing availability, alongside a clear account of the boundary between conventional and generated production.

**Misaligned — Eline van der Velden / Particle6.** The studio’s July announcement, reported by TheWrap, describes a developing comedy-drama with the synthetic character Tilly Norwood. Van der Velden is the studio founder; this note does not invent a film-director credit. A completed work and named film crew remain to be checked.

## Work awaiting a stronger viewing source

**The Dwarka Project — BULLET / Samraddhi Jain.** Jain’s own announcement verifies her concept and story contribution and points to a trailer made with Trinetra AI. It does not establish that she directed the film, or that the complete feature is available.

**Sibiloi — Edwin Kiaraho / Dan Kairo.** In their published interview, the filmmakers describe a ten-minute adaptation of the novel’s first two chapters. That is a partial adaptation, not a completed feature. The original upload and film-specific workflow remain to be checked.

**Asiri Ilu Awon Osu — Mysterious Tech Studio / Tanwa Hameed.** The producers’ statement reported by Punch describes a Yoruba-language hybrid production completed in 2025. We have not verified a complete official stream or the full creative credits. Promotional ‘world first’ claims are not treated as established history.
