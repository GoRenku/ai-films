## An army that is a promise

**[The Clay Legion](/films/the-clay-legion/)** is the new dark fantasy series from Thousand Words FilmS. A thousand years ago an army became fired clay to keep a door shut under a red hill. In the first episode the river below the village of Stonemill sinks and returns one of those soldiers, and a militia captain and an old potter disagree about what it means.

The studio's director's note describes the army as a promise rather than a weapon, and the soldier as a warning rather than the threat itself. That links the series to the studio's earlier creature fables, which turn on how people treat what they do not yet understand. The 22-minute episode is cut into titled chapters, from a prologue in the distant past to the opening of the door. The studio credits its own story and screenplay and discloses AI-generated visuals and voices; the models have not been named. The Clay Legion now opens the homepage carousel.

## Elderwall's backstory

LedertsWorld Studios has released **[Veyara and Her Dragon](/films/veyara-and-her-dragon/)**, an eight-and-a-half-minute prequel to [The Last Nursery of Elderwall](/films/the-last-nursery-of-elderwall/). It explains how a fight between two sisters turned the world's dragons to stone, starting from a small act of care: Veyara keeps her fading dragon alive by borrowing, and then stealing, other dragons' magic. The confrontation in the nursery is set to the studio's original song, Not Our Home. Telling the catastrophe from the side of the sister who caused it gives the feature's world a personal origin.

## A first attempt at length

Danioros's **[Do Not Wake Her](/films/do-not-wake-her/)** now has its own entry. Released on X in August, it runs about two minutes. The maker called it a first attempt at a longer story after keeping earlier videos under 90 seconds, and says it took four days with Seedance 2.5. It sits before [Storm-Born](/films/storm-born/), where Danioros planned cameras in a 3D previs before generating, and before the sequel announced this month.

## Two music videos

NoiraDEVIL's **[Not Going Down Today](/films/not-going-down-today/)** stages its boss-fight chorus as a duel between Noira and a dark double with her face, credited to NoiraDEVIL for visuals, edit and colour. Javier Fan's **[PUT THE CHIP IN](/films/put-the-chip-in/)** turns brain-chip telepathy into a retro-futurist slopcore number; the announcement credits Suno and slopcannontv without stating their roles.

## Kavan on The Chronicles of Bone

Magnific has published a short [interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Le631J6Ziv0) with Kavan the Kid about **[The Chronicles of Bone](/films/chronicles-of-bone/)**. According to Magnific's summary, it covers building a shared universe from public-domain characters, keeping a dark tone across a large cast and designing clay creatures such as the Collector. The series page now lists it alongside the earlier episode-three breakdown.
