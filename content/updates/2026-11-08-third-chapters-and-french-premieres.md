## The expedition continues

[Treasure of the Himalayas](/films/treasure-of-the-himalayas/) now includes the complete third part from the original Grindhouse Glitch and Roninfilm release. The makers continue to use 1960s adventure cinema as the visual reference for their Victorian expedition. The new post names Dreamina and credits the co-production.

## An opening slot in Paris

[A Woman Asleep](/films/a-woman-asleep/) is scheduled to open the Paris edition of Signes de Nuit on November 9. The festival lists it as the French premiere and places it in a programme about life in a technological world. The entry now also gives the two dates listed for Cottbus.
