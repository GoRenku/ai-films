## Journey to the Center of the Earth

[Thousand Words FilmS](/artists/thousand-words-films/), whose [The Clay Legion](/films/the-clay-legion/) began last week, has released a 39-minute standalone adventure. **[Journey to the Center of the Earth](/films/journey-to-the-center-of-the-earth/)** sends Verne's Lidenbrock, Axel and Hans down through Snaefells in 1863, adds a rival professor with an umbrella and his cheerful porter, and ends on a sea under a stone sky.

The release is unusually open about its adaptation. It lists what comes from the novel and what the studio invented, and the director's note explains the central change: Verne's professor follows runes, but this map has only drawings, so the route down is read from pictures the way the audience reads them. Its creature, Glim, is a five-metre version of the olm, the real blind salamander of the Dinaric karst. The note also records an editing choice: chapter cards were renamed from events to places, so the screen says where the travellers are, never what is coming. The film now opens the homepage carousel.

## Six returning filmmakers, one contest

The Higgsfield Talent Contest keeps bringing back filmmakers already in the collection, and this week's entries cover a wide range of tone.

- **[The Last Warmth](/films/the-last-warmth/)**: [Sasha Fevralskaya](/artists/sasha-fevralskaya/), who won Curious Refuge's horror competition with Kagome, tells a dark folk tale in which the sun is held captive and a village girl carries the last ember into the snow. It was made in Higgsfield Cinema Studio.
- **[And first place and $200,000 goes to…](/films/and-first-place-and-200000-goes-to/)**: [Shestak](/artists/nikolay-shestak/) satirises the contests themselves. A photographer who shoots on film keeps losing, until a new contest makes Soul a requirement; Soul is also the name of the Higgsfield image model the release credits alongside Seedance 2.5.
- **[HOLD YOUR HORSES](/films/hold-your-horses/)**: [Myrzakhan Zhumakhan](/artists/myrzakhan-zhumakhan/), Best Character winner at Astana, makes a pony mockumentary about envy, a stolen dating profile and an interview that should never have been filmed.
- **[WARM](/films/warm-junho-her/)**: [Junho Her](/artists/junho-her/) of GRAB AI STUDIO animates a small dog who learns to hear warmth in a neighbour dog's bark, because that is when Grandma holds her closer.
- **[My story has nothing](/films/my-story-has-nothing/)**: [Abhick Banerjee](/artists/abhik-banerjee/) and Rollsound Studios follow a cheaply painted figure through a museum's masterpieces at night, in search of one drop of blue for his mother.
- **[Hitchhiking](/films/hitchhiking-production-pi/)**: [Production Pi](/artists/production-pi/), the studio behind the paper-craft comedy FIX, sends a French backpacker into a car full of friendly aliens on a 2000s Australian highway. Three writer-directors share the credit, and the release is subtitled in Korean, English and French. The release says it was made with Higgsfield but does not call it a contest entry.

Most of these releases name the platform but not the models, and none gives crew size or schedule. Shestak's is the exception on tools.

## Grey

Outside the contest, [Ibraheem Diab](/artists/ibraheem-diab/), whose The Beginning won WAIFF's Best AI Emotion Film, has made **[Grey](/films/grey-ibraheem-diab/)**, a wordless short in a clay stop-motion style. On his 80th birthday, a grandfather loses his colour every time someone at the table looks at a phone. With no dialogue, the colour of one figure carries the whole story.
