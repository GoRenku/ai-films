**Verena Puhm’s Nobody Dies on Mars placed second** in Future Vision XPRIZE, receiving a $100,000 runner-up cash award. Her profile now records the result rather than only finalist status.

The grand-prize entry, **The Gifted by Jeff Synthesized**, received $2.5 million in equity investment toward feature production and $100,000 for screenplay development, according to XPRIZE. Range Media Partners and Google’s 100 ZEROS initiative are supporting development and production.

These were trailers and treatments competing for future filmmaking support. The result matters as a route from a proposed story to financed production; it is not evidence that either feature is already complete or available to watch.
