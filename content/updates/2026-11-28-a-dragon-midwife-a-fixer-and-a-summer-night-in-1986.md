## The Dragon Midwife

Right after its Verne adaptation, [Thousand Words FilmS](/artists/thousand-words-films/) has released another long fable. **[The Dragon Midwife](/films/the-dragon-midwife/)** runs 36 minutes. For thirty-six years Elsbeth has delivered every child in a high Alpine valley, until one night she is led to a cave where a mother dragon cannot lay her egg. The hunters drive the mother over the pass, and Elsbeth is left with a hatchling to hide until spring.

The studio builds the story on a folk legend told across Europe, the midwife fetched at night to a birth among the hidden folk. In most versions she is paid and sent home; here she is handed the egg. As in the Verne film, the release separates its sources, among them the Lucerne dragon stone of 1421 and the Swiss dragon reports collected by Kircher and Scheuchzer, from what the studio invented. The director's note explains two choices: the hunt leader is a man Elsbeth delivered as a baby, so the last scene turns on her hands rather than a weapon, and a scripted night climb up a trail of glowing coals was cut.

## The Fixer

[Nathaniel Chan](/artists/nathaniel-chan/)'s earlier shorts ran about a minute. **[The Fixer](/films/the-fixer-nathaniel-chan/)** is close to nine: a man repairs everything for everyone in his neighbourhood because he cannot face the one thing he cannot repair. The release places it in **PLAYGROUND Season 1**, an anthology produced by Storybook Studios and made on Cuelist, and Chan opens it with a personal note about piling on work to avoid a hurt. The models, cast and the anthology's other stories have not been named. Chan's [Midnight Snack](/films/midnight-snack-nathaniel-chan/) now plays from the filmmaker's own YouTube channel.

## Hold Still

[antic](/artists/antic/), whose [Cutting Room Floor](/films/cutting-room-floor/) was a song about the work behind a generated video, now puts a number on that work. **[Hold Still](/films/hold-still/)** follows two teenagers across one July night in 1986, from a tired suburb to an abandoned mall with a found VHS-C camcorder and out past the edge of town. The release credits **Adam Eivy** as director and atomantic for the music, and says the video was built from **82 scenes and more than 1,200 takes**, graded to look like found 35mm flash prints spliced with grainy 16mm. Before posting it, antic wrote that it took more hours and iterations than people would believe. The models are not named.

## Two series move on

- **[Bone China](/films/bone-china/)**: [Daniił Vołkaŭ](/artists/daniil-volkau/)'s porcelain war-horror reaches Episode 3, **Ambrosia**. Klara is now inside the Spire, where every guest at the October Ball must drink the substance that keeps porcelain whole, and she learns it is made from the men of her own sector. The series' rule turns inward: survival is now something she must take part in.
- **[The House of Amun](/films/house-of-amun/)**: two days after the feature cut of Season 1, [Isaac Rodriguez](/artists/isaac-rodriguez/) opens Season 2 with the 15-minute **The Widow of Egypt**. Tutankhamun is dead, Ay is crowned, and Ankhesenamun, now the prize of the succession, slips through Thebes at night to have a scribe write a dangerous message.
