## Two ways to meet

[You’re Too Young to Remember](/films/youre-too-young-to-remember/) puts the same couple into a household-landline encounter in 1994 and a dating-app match in the present. Keeping their ages fixed makes the changing social machinery visible. Claudia’s opening scan frames the earlier scenes as a reconstruction; counters and phone interfaces give the modern scenes a more measured rhythm.

Andrew at **Direct the Machine** documents the choices behind the video. His account describes auditioning eighteen song versions, trimming the selected outro and timing about two hundred shots to a beat grid from the 120 BPM track. Interface graphics and counters were rendered separately from the generated footage. These are practical editing decisions that connect the two periods, rather than a new costume for the same recurring singer.

## What the production record contributes

The credits identify Andrew’s direction, Claude in Claude Code’s production role, and a Suno track performed by the synthetic Claudia persona. The filmmaker names the still-image, sung-performance and finishing tools on the film page, without identifying an additional human singer or a complete human crew. The shot-by-shot account gives viewers a way to compare the final construction with its parts.
