## A historical witness

[Forever | Special Episode](/films/forever-special-episode/) places Atatürk’s last days in the account of his childhood friend and aide Salih Bozok. The release identifies its historical references, and The Diary of Father credits a creator partnership with Higgsfield.

## Blocking the camera before generation

[Storm-Born](/films/storm-born/) uses a rider and winged creature to tie two short fantasy instalments together. Danioros’s first-part comparison shows a Three.js camera plan alongside the finished images. The maker explains how selected frames from that previs became Seedance references.

## What the voice carries

[Yuri’s AI opera](/films/yuri-ai-opera/) gives sung performance room through close-ups and bilingual subtitles. Yuri credits Miora storyWeave for platform support and describes a workflow spanning code, generation, editing and visual effects.

[Absurd](/artists/absurd/) separately explains that its hybrid workflow preserved the actors’ own voices in the example shown in its making-of.
