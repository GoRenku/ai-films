Today’s five additions include two full music videos, two narrative shorts and an older collaborative anime pilot newly discovered through its makers.

## What happens when judgment is delegated?

Extraltodeus’s **[Demande à l’IA!](/films/demande-a-lia/)** turns ordinary requests for advice into ClaudiaGI’s satire of surrendered attention. **[GOLDEN LIFE (S.I.)](/films/golden-life/)** gives VØXELLE’s future imagery a personal measure: family time that keeps being postponed. Both sustain a released song through changing scenes and a recurring performer; neither needs to imitate a conventional narrative short to belong here.

**[Escaping the Onion](/films/escaping-the-onion/)**, shared by Gossip Goblin under the alternate caption Cracking The Onion, asks what is lost in the pursuit of a final reality. **[StoryWeave](/films/storyweave/)** makes competing memory reconstructions change the apparent motives within a relationship. Its creator specifically credits Opus and Miora without supplying exact underlying video-model versions.

## A shared fantasy world and a visible team

**[Lyrian Chronicles RIFT](/films/lyrian-chronicles-rift/)** is a July 3 pilot, not a new October premiere. Its full 31:37 upload includes promotional material and credits. The original names six collaborators, thanks a further contributor and separately identifies the creator of its underlying RPG world. A dedicated [team profile](/artists/lyrian-chronicles-team/) preserves those roles rather than assigning the whole project to one visible account. Another episode is announced for a few days from now.

## What the production accounts add

antic’s new essay expands **[Cutting Room Floor](/films/cutting-room-floor/)** with planning, placeholder editing and PortOS preview context. **[Space Pirates](/films/space-pirates/)** gains a later Dreamina disclosure alongside its earlier Pika account; the sources do not explain the division of work. **[Malfoid](/films/malfoid-i-wasnt-joking/)** gains the complete Don’t Wait for Me Part 2, while WRATH’s full-song waveform presentation remains a soundtrack link. **[The Sage](/films/the-sage/)** has an October 21 next-episode announcement, not a newly verified playable episode.
