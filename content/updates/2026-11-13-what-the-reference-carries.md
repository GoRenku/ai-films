## A deadline becomes a pilot

AIST’s [ARTIFICIAL](/films/artificial-aist/) opens with a job interview that becomes a same-day music-video assignment. The studio uses a problem it knows well to give its generated office mockumentary a simple test of character under pressure.

## Staging action

Danioros reduced the amount of detail in the [Storm-Born](/films/storm-born/) Part 2 previs after rough elements appeared in the generated shots. The reference still carried the camera plan. For [CHUMBO](/films/chumbo/), the production team used overhead staging and specific hand directions to make a fight over a bottle legible. Magnific also reports that more than half the working hours were spent after generation, and that the characters’ nonverbal sounds were performed by human actors.

## From Short Shorts Film Festival & Asia

TechTalkNavigator’s [Immune Prime](/films/immune-prime/) follows a visitor to a future in which Earth itself is eliminating humanity. MINIMA’s [kusarabi](/films/kusarabi/) uses yōkai, a grotesque banquet and an invented register of song to explore how people pass responsibility around.
