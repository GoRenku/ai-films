## Three complete musical performances

Extraltodeus’s [Qu’est-ce qu’elle a que j’ai pas?](/films/quest-ce-quelle-a-que-jai-pas/) gives ClaudiaGI a domestic world for romantic comparison and self-mockery. [Can’t Avoid Malfoid](/films/cant-avoid-malfoid/) brings Skyebrows back with an animated performance spectacle and named collaborators. Korgamiai’s [A Million Malfoids](/films/a-million-malfoids/) has its own fight-staging and tool account. Sharing a character and collaborators does not make these one release or give them an identical pipeline.

## Suspense, retakes and a small observer

[Stalk](/films/stalk/) introduces 88probs through a complete Halloween short and a particularly useful account of sparse dialogue. Gwinn’s [When Death Whispers Your Name](/films/when-death-whispers-your-name/) moves from exploratory character stills to a written villa mystery. Vołkaŭ’s [untitled rubber-duck comedy](/films/untitled-rubber-duck-short/) keeps a comic relationship intact while a director changes genre around his performer.

Festival research also adds [Mianmian](/films/mianmian/), credited to Yingjie Chen and awarded Lumara’s Jury Selection. The organiser’s pet-fish point of view and official still establish a specific narrative approach. Its exhibited status is verified; a standalone full stream and optional production details remain unknown.

## A free feature and another part

[Odysseus: The Fall](/films/odysseus-the-fall/) now has a checked free 150-minute X upload. Fountain 0 also lists it free, but its Watch route asks signed-out visitors to sign in. The new access does not erase the earlier collaborators or generation support.

[Malfoid](/films/malfoid-i-wasnt-joking/) adds the complete I Was Joking, Part 4: Thank You, with the maker’s distinction between prior AI-video experience and newly developed narrative practice. The existing series starting link is preserved.

## Counting the attempts

[CHUMBO](/films/chumbo/) gains a later maker account: the final-shot count stays at 272, while the reported video-generation count and schedule differ from the earlier case study. Both accounts are retained with their scope unresolved. The reported 74 attempts for one fall make iteration’s uneven distribution more tangible than a single average.
