## Out of Character

The Higgsfield Talent Contest's second wave brings a new name. **[Out of Character](/films/out-of-character/)** is a seven-minute science-fiction comedy written and directed by Avaren and produced by The Blue Horizon, the studio that released the horror short [María, María](/films/maria-maria-alex-marco-tomas-bucetto/). Its heroine, Mira, is an actress who exists only inside the worlds she was created to perform in; the release image finds her in a sunlit Western street, pointing at an orc warrior who plainly belongs to another film.

The idea, developed with Alex Marco, is a sharp one for this medium. Generated characters really are written into whatever scene they are asked for, and the film gives one of them the awareness to object. The release says it was made entirely on Higgsfield's platform without naming the models. Out of Character now opens the homepage carousel.

## ONE LAST CALL

Three days after the comedy [OTSUKAI](/films/otsukai/), Allan Ripley has entered a second contest film in the same 1990s cel-animation look, and in the opposite mood. **[ONE LAST CALL](/films/one-last-call/)** follows an ageing hitman through a routine contract, a neon bar and an envelope left "by the lady" that holds his own photograph circled in red. The release asks for long silences and cold grey streets, a test of whether generated animation can hold stillness.

Ripley's credits are again a useful map of a pipeline: GPT Image 2 for character, prop and model sheets, the reference pages that keep a design consistent from shot to shot; Seedream for the painted backgrounds; Seedance 2.5 for motion; and Suno for an original ballad in the style of Showa-era kayokyoku.

## Wouldn't It Be Something

**[Wouldn't It Be Something](/films/wouldnt-it-be-something/)** is a departure for [Kelly Boesch](/artists/kelly-boesch/), whose videos usually set her songs to surreal fashion and invented bodies. This one tells the song's story: two children, Sammy and Bella, who find in each other someone who sees nothing wrong with the way they think. Boesch writes that Seedance made a real storyline possible for her, and ties Bella to her own childhood with dyslexia. She says more story-led videos are coming within the month.
