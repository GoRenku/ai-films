[AI Film Festival Japan](/festivals/ai-film-festival-japan/) published 18 prizes from its November 1 ceremony. The films below have documented festival screenings.

## The Grand Prix and AI Movie awards

- [The Finding](/films/the-finding/) received **GRAND PRIX**. In Sicily, a hidden treasure brings a bargain with the Devil back into force.
- [TOKYO STORIES『TOKYO 雨/RAIN』](/films/tokyo-stories-rain/) received **BEST AI MOVIE**. A visitor sheltering from the rain meets a ballet student in a Tokyo café.
- [PUNCHLINE](/films/punchline-norihiko/) received **OUTSTANDING**. Two feared high school students try their hand at manzai, a two-person comedy form.
- [TOKYO STORIES (TOKYO IMITATION)](/films/tokyo-stories-imitation/) received **OUTSTANDING**. A Tokyo Stories short that borrows the texture of documentary for a fictional portrait.

## Animation awards

- [ABYSS SIREN](/films/abyss-siren/) received **BEST ANIMATION**. A salvager retrieves a girl from a crystal formation deep under the sea.
- [THE AMAZING KITSUVERSE](/films/the-amazing-kitsuverse/) received **OUTSTANDING**. A fox lures an older woman into a world where a clap can grant any wish.
- [Ticket to Neverland](/films/ticket-to-neverland/) received **OUTSTANDING**. Fairy-tale characters compete on a reality show for a single ticket to Neverland.

## Creative and voice awards

- [A Message for the Butterfly](/films/a-message-for-the-butterfly/) received **BEST CREATIVE**. Fragments of memory pass through changing bodies and imagined futures.
- [TOKYO STORIES (TOKYO NEW WAVE)](/films/tokyo-stories-new-wave/) received **OUTSTANDING**. An experimental Tokyo Stories short built around the energy of the city.
- [TOKYO ECHO](/films/tokyo-echo/) received **SPECIAL MENTION**. Young people’s online accounts of Tokyo become the basis for fictional figures on screen.
- [Little Sun](/films/little-sun-liz-chen/) received **ElevenLabs Award**. An American short recognised by AIFJ for its use of AI voice performance.

The organiser also awarded three film scores, three vocal tracks and the CapCut prize for TechTalkNavigator’s *EISENRITTER Confronting the World*. The awarded cut of the ongoing series has its own result row.
