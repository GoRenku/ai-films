Three music-video additions explore different uses of a synthetic figure. **Kelly Boesch’s [Between Me and You](/films/between-me-and-you/)** gives emotional distance a surreal fashion world. **Nightway Fantasy’s [in Humilitate](/films/in-humilitate/)** uses animated transformation for a spiritual struggle. **Astra Blue’s [KILL SWITCH](/films/kill-switch/)** turns the pop persona into a fictional threat.

## Production choices worth reading

Boesch explains why she retained the unusual bodies imposed by a Midjourney style reference, then pursued futuristic dance through Kling. Nightway Fantasy reports text-to-video without keyframes or image references, with most effects included in the designs. Astra Blue’s prompt-only claim appears alongside its acknowledgement of added film grain and a five-to-six-hour estimate. Each disclosure belongs to its particular work; missing model settings and personnel remain unknown.
