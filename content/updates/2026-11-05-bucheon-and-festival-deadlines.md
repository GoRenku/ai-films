## Three years of Bucheon Choice

[Bucheon Choice AI Films](/festivals/bifan-bucheon-choice-ai-films/) joins the festival watch with the organiser’s results for 2024 through 2026. The [2026 Best AI Film](https://www.bifan.kr/eng/community/comm01_view.asp?actEvent=view&pk_seq=92660&sc_board_seq=1) was [Cornered](/films/sinuca-de-bico-cornered/), Odair Faléco and Davi Moori’s story of a workday that pushes its protagonist to breaking point. The first edition’s main prize went to Léo Cannone’s [Where Do Grandmas Go When They Get Lost?](/films/where-do-grandmas-go/).

The 2025 results bring [More Tears Than Harm](/films/more-tears-than-harm/) into the collection. Herinarivo Rakotomanana’s short uses collage to place childhood memories of Madagascar alongside the present. It won BIFAN’s technical award. Jacob Adler’s [Total Pixel Space](/films/total-pixel-space/) received a special mention from the jury.

## Two calls with firmer dates

[Chroma](/festivals/chroma-awards/) confirms January 7, 2027 at 11:45 p.m. Pacific time in its rules. [Torino’s AI Visions](/festivals/torino-underground-cinefest-ai-visions/) lists August 9, 2027 as the final entry date on the festival’s FilmFreeway page. Its next paid entry tier closes November 9, 2026.
