## TEMPLES

TUPACABRA's [Nuremberg](/films/nuremberg/) trailer, built on a 1561 broadsheet about strange objects in the sky, is still the only public footage from the feature. His account also carries shorter pieces that sit around it. **[TEMPLES](https://x.com/tupacabra/status/2101605649755124211)**, first posted on September 20 and shared again on November 24, runs two minutes and fifty-one seconds and is titled and nothing more.

It is not a film, and we are not listing it as one. Its interest is in world-building. In the frames we sampled, craft hang low over a grey modern city while a man with a suitcase walks beneath them. A tower is carved with colossal robed figures, a pope raises a vessel before rows of clergy, and a white temple complex rises to a single spire. The images suggest a present in which the craft have been absorbed into religion and state architecture. It reads as the same subject as the trailer, unexplained craft and the order that forms around them, seen from a later century.

TUPACABRA has not said that TEMPLES is a scene from Nuremberg, and we do not present it as one. It now has its own section on the Nuremberg page and on [TUPACABRA's artist page](/artists/tupacabra/). More pieces seem likely as the feature develops, so his account now sits among the creators we check on every daily run.
