## Prison Break’s episode budget

PJ Ace explains that the roughly $100,000 figure covers all three parts of [Prison Break](/films/nexus-prison-break-part-one/). He reports $102,725 for the episode, with 80% for labor and 20% for animation costs, and identifies Dreamina as the funder. The released Part I runs 4:40. His estimate for the full episode is 15–20 minutes.

His generation figures describe a smaller unit: three to seven attempts at each 30-second sequence, at roughly $15–25 per run. Those figures show how retries accumulate at the shot level. The reported episode budget also accounts for the people making and finishing the work.

## Start with Treasure of the Himalayas Part One

The [series guide](/films/treasure-of-the-himalayas/#episodes) now starts with Peter Tei-Hyoguchi’s playable 8:35 upload. Roninfilm embeds it as Part One. The video title and opening scenes support that identification, although its YouTube description header says Part Three.
