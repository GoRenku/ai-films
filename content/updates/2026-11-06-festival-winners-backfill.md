## Three more ways into Bucheon’s 2026 programme

[Big Ball](/films/big-ball/) uses a Wall Street trader to explore what happens when a character starts to question the reality a film has built for him. [Let There Be Light](/films/let-there-be-light/) shifts between styles from cinema history as its protagonist moves through simulated memories. The 75-minute [Ask Me Anything](/films/ask-me-anything/) sends a participant through worlds shaped by fear. Each has a filmmaker page and an official BIFAN still. The catalogue records their festival screenings while public full-film links remain unverified.

## Following the work beyond the award

[Chairman](/films/chairman/), Studio 011’s narrative winner at AI Film Festival Japan, builds a comedy around a man who can turn into a chair. WAIFF’s release of [RendAI-vous](/films/rendai-vous/) gives viewers a way into Marius Doicov’s short about a courtship disrupted by technology. And in [Soulless](/films/soulless-yoav-yariv/), a robot dog helps an injured jazz pianist recover what a driverless car took away. The film is available through its original Higgsfield project.
