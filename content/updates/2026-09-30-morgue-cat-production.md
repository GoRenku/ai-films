Absurd’s **Morgue Cat** production account follows Jonesy, a space-station cat whose gift to a pathologist becomes a threat. Rommel Villa describes collaborators Robin, Mengqing and Daniella handling different sequences; their surnames, complete credits and total team size are not established here.

Three useful craft disclosures:

- **Identity:** project rules preserve which eye has which color; expression references support feline performance.
- **Handoffs:** shared character and locked-environment references connect separate scene pages, with feedback attached to selected takes.
- **Continuity:** a recovered shot failed to match newer blocking, lighting and color, requiring a rebuilt transition.

The editorial interest is how decisions survive collaboration and iteration. Shared assets alone cannot ensure that adjoining shots make dramatic or spatial sense.

This is a platform-published production account, not independent testing. It does not establish a full public release, runtime, exact models or budget. **Morgue Cat remains a forthcoming project in this journal.**
