## A useful continuity practice before generation

In his original Seedance 2.5 workflow post, **Finn McKenty** describes preparing character sheets, a four-angle location grid and an ordered set of uploaded references in FLORA. He stresses keeping those upload positions aligned with the reference numbers in the prompt. A reply clarifies that the location reference is a multi-angle grid.

The practical point is spatial and identity preparation: a consistent reference set gives each new shot a clearer starting point. His claim of almost no bad generations and reduced credit waste remains a maker account, without a measured benchmark. No specific completed film, complete crew or commercial relationship is established by the post, so this is craft coverage rather than a new film credit.
