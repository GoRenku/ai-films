## Four Tokyo Stories releases

TYO’s official releases bring four AI Film Festival Japan award films online in the catalogue. In Int.Lab’s [Rain](/films/tokyo-stories-rain/), a chance encounter with a ballet student sends a visitor back into a rainy Tokyo street. Kentaro Osawa’s [Tokyo Imitation](/films/tokyo-stories-imitation/) borrows the form of documentary for a fictional portrait. Arata Fukoe’s [Tokyo New Wave](/films/tokyo-stories-new-wave/) uses a consistent visual style to carry the energy of a city portrait. Masaaki Uchino’s [Tokyo Echo](/films/tokyo-echo/) builds fictional characters from young people’s online impressions of the city.

## The EISENRITTER festival cut

TechTalkNavigator’s [EISENRITTER Confronting the World](/films/eisenritter-confronting-the-world/) won the CapCut Award. The filmmaker describes the full upload as a theatrical edit of the first four episodes, with revisions and previously unreleased scenes. The film now has its own catalogue record and play button on the festival page.
