## A gunner’s mission

**[Oni-Slaying Maiden IV: The Gunner](/films/oni-slaying-maiden/)** is the complete LostJoker Films release that matches the OPUS programme’s short. Shio Kuroki enters the ruins after Ren fails to return; her transforming rifle makes the assignment specific to this heroine. The original description confirms Japanese dialogue and English captions. The public player runs about 5:33 against the programme’s 5:32. The numeral is preserved without inventing a complete publicly released series.

## Fairy tales and an honest face

**[Ticket to Neverland](/films/ticket-to-neverland/)** has a complete WAIFF Seoul upload: 10:18, with chapters for Pinocchio, Hansel and Gretel, Mr. Wolf and the result. Its reality-show structure turns familiar characters’ hardships into competitive assets. The catalogue preserves AIFJ’s separate 10:00 programme timing.

**[The First Honest Day](/films/the-first-honest-day/)** is available in Nikolay Shestak’s full 20:15 release, uploaded November 13. Newsreader Veronika Hale’s changing face brings a worldwide phenomenon into the intimate space of a live broadcast. Its description credits Shestak with writing, direction, editing and production, and names SIA SHESTAK.

The Russian dub is credited to **SYNCMER**, with **Olga Verbitskaya, Anatoly Fechin, Victoria Babanova and Dmitry Shandro**. These are dub credits, not an inferred English voice cast or character assignments. The film page preserves the creator’s tool disclosures and distinguishes an ambiguous product spelling from verified model names. Its original song is **The Honest Men — Leave the light on**.

## Landscapes, labour and an encounter

The official AIFJ upload makes **[The Tree](/films/the-tree-ivan-yeh/)** playable in full at about 2:52. Ivan Yeh uses one tree as a point of reference through changes in time; the festival’s description names LTX Studio and KlingAI without versions.

**[The Hidden Tremor](/films/the-hidden-tremor/)** plays in NOWNESS’s complete 4:45 release. Its original account places Léo Cannone’s imagined forces inside landscapes documented during travel through Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam.

The complete original **[The Man behind the Cartoon](/films/the-man-behind-the-cartoon/)** remains a 3:50 story about the unseen human cost of a cartoon rabbit’s indestructibility. **[RendAI-vous](/films/rendai-vous/)** also gains a verified official WAIFF player, running 5:13, preserving its contrast between a fantastical virtual courtship and an ordinary encounter.
