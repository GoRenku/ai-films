## A third film in Babel

**[HIVE](/films/hive/)** gains **HUSH**, Daniił Vołkaŭ's third film set in the city where a place to live costs somebody else's life. It brings back Mercy from VACANCY, now auditing the Quiet Works, a factory that sells calm in sage-green bottles. She arrives on the same night as Ana, a mother who has not slept for forty-one nights, and finds the factory's shortfall already assigned to her.

The new film matters because it widens the system rather than repeating it. VACANCY and KNOCK were about housing; HUSH turns sleep and silence into goods paid for with someone else's suffering. Seeing Mercy in a second role shows how the same person can hold a different position in the city's economy. The creator again discloses AI-generated visuals and voices without naming models. HIVE now opens the homepage carousel, starting with VACANCY.

## ATLAS — NEXT closes its run

Sho AI Studio has released **[ATLAS — NEXT](/films/atlas-next/)** Part 7 (November 7) and a 27-minute **Final** (November 21). Eight instalments now follow the new generation of pilots, ten years into a ceasefire meant to last a century. Like the first [ATLAS](/films/atlas/) season, the sequel ends with a separately titled finale.

The studio describes the same method for every part: character design, storyboards and visual references first, then individually generated scenes with Seedance 2.0 and 2.5. Over eleven weeks that preparation has supported a full serial season on a regular schedule. Voice, music and team credits have not been disclosed.

## A persona played for laughs

**[AGI BEFORE GTA6](/films/agi-before-gta6/)** gives [Astra Blue](/artists/astra-blue-ai/)'s recurring performer a comic brief: the joke that artificial general intelligence might arrive before a long-delayed video game. The video borrows the look of a Miami crime game, from rooftop parties and a festival stage to a supercar on a causeway and a five-star wanted meter. Astra's earlier videos used the same character to make AI power unsettling; this one uses her for pastiche. The release calls itself an unofficial fan-art parody, and its tools have not been disclosed.

## A player that went offline

The YouTube upload of Junho Her's **[The Gift](/films/the-gift-junho-her/)**, the AIMA London Best Animation winner, is no longer available, and no other official upload has been found on the studio channel. The film is listed by its festival screenings until a full release returns.
