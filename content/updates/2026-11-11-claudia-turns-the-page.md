[Fable](/films/fable-claudia/) builds a music video around a single storybook. Its paper scenes become stages for Claudia’s performance, and the middle passage uses silhouettes animated in code.

The production record describes a second pass prompted by a song choice. Anabology listened to the first version, selected a different Suno vocal take, and had the performances timed to that recording. The film credits Claude Fable 5.2 for writing, painting and directing.
