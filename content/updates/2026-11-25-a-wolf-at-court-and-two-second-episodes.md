## The King's Wolf

Thousand Words FilmS has spent the past week releasing creature stories: [The Clay Legion](/films/the-clay-legion/) on November 21, [The Red Wolf](/films/the-red-wolf/) the day after. **[The King's Wolf](/films/the-kings-wolf/)** is the first to adapt a written legend. Its source is Bisclavret, a twelfth-century Breton lay by Marie de France and one of the oldest werewolf tales in Europe. A lord who turns into a wolf every third night is trapped in that shape when his clothes are stolen, and he ends up in the King's kennels.

The studio's change is the point of view. The 30-minute film follows Gwen, a kennel girl who sleeps among the hounds and notices that the new wolf will not touch raw meat and takes half of her bread each night. The director's note says she was added because the original never mentions anyone like her. As with the studio's other recent releases, the description separates what is inherited from what is invented: the premise and the setting come from the lay, while the kennel, Gwen, the wolf's name and the bread are new. The film is cut in five titled chapters. Visuals and voices are AI-generated; the models and crew are not named.

## The Black Box, Episode 2

Magnific's **[The Black Box](/films/the-black-box/)** returns with **The Favor**, a 13-minute second episode. Kendrick's first fight is against Kraken, the league's undefeated champion, with his sister Nia's treatment depending on the result. The release says the sound fades and time stretches in the cage, and Kendrick starts to wonder how much of the fight he controls.

The credits have been reorganised since the first episode. [John Olivos](/artists/john-olivos/), who directed Episode 1, keeps the original idea and art direction and works as one of three AI visual artists. Iván Jiménez, formerly the generative editor, now directs, edits, scores and sound-designs. Laura Posada is credited as writer. Magnific says the scenes were built in its Magnific Spaces tool with Seedance 2.5 Pro for video.

## SALT, Episode 2

GlassWeaver Productions' Cornish drama **[SALT](/films/salt/)** continues with **The Lift Home**, six days after the first episode. The 14-minute episode leaves the café for a tasting, an encounter that is less accidental than it looks, and a drive home in the rain with the couple's old song on the radio, until Aubrey's fiancé Freddie arrives early. GlassWeaver now posts each episode a week early on Patreon and promises longer cuts there from Episode 5. The episode guide lists the YouTube releases.

## The House of Amun as a feature

Isaac Rodriguez has released **[The House of Amun](/films/house-of-amun/)** as a 1 hour 51 minute feature, on the date its trailer gave. According to the description, it joins all ten Season One episodes into a single film, and it invites viewers to follow Ankhesenamun and Ay into a second season. The series page still opens with Episode 1, and the feature is linked for viewers who would rather watch it in one sitting.

## How Y2Y2R's lip sync was done

Andrew at [Direct the Machine](/artists/direct-the-machine/) has answered the most common question about **[You’re Too Young to Remember](/films/youre-too-young-to-remember/)**. Claudia's sung shots were generated in Seedance 2.5 or MiniMax H3, and each prompt carried the lyrics plus that shot's vocal, cut to length. The face and outfit sheets, the scene and the vocal went into one job, and no separate lip-sync tool was used afterwards. He made most of the film on Runway, because both models were available there with the same references, and he notes that the post was not sponsored.
