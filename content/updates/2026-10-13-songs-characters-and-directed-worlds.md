Music videos join today’s collection on their own terms: direction, continuity and a sustained concept edited to a released song. **Extraltodeus** brings [Chromatique](/films/chromatique/) and [C’est de l’IA](/films/cest-de-lia/), with original French releases and unusually specific local-tool disclosures. **anabology** brings four connected Claudia videos: [Escape Velocity](/films/escape-velocity/), [Clodyssey](/films/clodyssey/), [Bliss](/films/bliss/) and [Airplane Mode](/films/airplane-mode/).

## What the detailed record changes

Airplane Mode’s public making-of separates human direction from delegated production. It describes grey Blender passes for motion, image references for appearance, copy-mode lip sync and notes asking the edit to linger. Its ledger distinguishes generated material, final selections and service costs. Those details are more useful than treating an overnight claim as a complete description of authorship.

[Astra Blue AI’s P DOOM](/films/p-doom/) and [VØXELLE’s The Seventh Reset](/films/seventh-reset/) are included with explicit project identities and undisclosed individual credits left unknown. [Stellorado](/films/stellorado/), an older Skyebrows release, is now reconsidered as a complete music video rather than an unconfirmed narrative pilot. These are discovery dates, not ten new premieres.

## Narrative performance and exhibition

[Malfoid — I Wasn’t Joking](/films/malfoid-i-wasnt-joking/) adds a complete, credited fan-fiction adaptation whose turn depends on sincerity being mistaken for mockery. [Cornered](/films/sinuca-de-bico-cornered/) gains the exact **Best AI Short** recognition from [Torino Underground Cinefest’s official results](https://www.tucfest.com/i-premi-del-torino-underground-cinefest-2026/).
