## From the festival circuit

Wonder Studios’ [The Trials](/films/the-trials-hal-watmough/) gives an alien challenge a personal stake through the bond between a grieving girl and an injured cheetah. The film runs 32:29 and won the London AI Movie Awards’ Best Feature category. Hal Watmough’s earlier animated short [LATE](/films/late-hal-watmough/) also joins the catalogue, with first place from Curious Refuge’s animation competition.

Mateo AI Studio and MBC C&I announce a November 25 South Korean theatrical opening for [Raphael](/films/raphael-mateo-ai-studio/). The Korean Film Council’s account of a seven-person team using Kling AI and Photoshop points to the work of keeping characters consistent across a feature. In Cottbus, programmer Joshua Jádi described an enthusiastic audience at one screening of [A Woman Asleep](/films/a-woman-asleep/). The film’s entry now records that firsthand account alongside its festival dates.

## Episodes and shorts

S.A.V’s [Exile Protocol](/films/exile-protocol/) reaches Episode 9, *Reckoning*. The maker says the 21-minute episode took about three weeks of solo production. [For The Record](/films/for-the-record-higginswerx/) opens with a reconstruction of the Vilet Torrez case that identifies its generated performers as invented likenesses. Gossip Goblin’s [Grieving Freshies](/films/grieving-freshies/) uses a vertical frame to hold a distressed figure between two suited escorts. Samyaza Speaks has posted an [Obliteration](/films/obliteration/) Episode 2 teaser, with the complete episode still forthcoming.

## Four music videos

ClaudiaGI turns her reply to Quentin into a public performance in [Pas calculé](/films/pas-calcule/). DotPockett’s [The Words You Never Said](/films/the-words-you-never-said/) takes the quieter route, letting hesitation and small gestures tell a story between recurring characters. JeffTriesThings reports using 44 shots and one recurring protagonist for an [original-song video](/films/untitled-music-video-jeff-tries-things-november/). Kelly Boesch’s [Christmas With Myself](/films/christmas-with-myself/) places a solitary holiday song in a snow-covered village.

## Directing the pause before a line

Monique Pryce describes prompting three details in a scene from her Beethoven feature: Karl tosses a quill, turns toward Beethoven and delivers his line as a dry question. Pryce says she chose the take for how believable that reaction felt. The disclosure gives a practical example of directing behaviour between lines. She confirmed the feature is still in production.
